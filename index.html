2014 vs 2024 rules: what's the difference

2014

Classic fifth edition

  • Race and subrace grant ability score bonuses
  • Backgrounds have a feature
  • Bards, sorcerers, warlocks and rangers know a fixed list of spells
  • Includes the artificer, Tasha's Cauldron, Xanathar's Guide and races from Monsters of the Multiverse
2024

New Player's Handbook

  • Backgrounds grant ability score bonuses and an Origin feat
  • Weapon Mastery for martial classes
  • All classes choose a subclass at level 3
  • Supplements: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer, Heroes of Faerûn, Arcana Unleashed, Ravenloft: The Horrors Within and more

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, the builder is completely free and ad-free.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Without an account, characters are stored in your browser and can be exported to a file. An account lets you open your characters on any device: sign up with email or sign in with Google.

Which rules can I build a character with?

The 2014 Player's Handbook (classic fifth edition) with official supplements — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, Xanathar's Guide to Everything, Monsters of the Multiverse and setting books — and the 2024 Player's Handbook with its supplements. You choose the edition when creating a character.

Can I print the character sheet or play from a screen?

Both. On the Character Sheet step there's a Print / PDF button: print the sheet or save it as a PDF. Or play with the digital sheet on a phone, tablet or computer: tap to track current and temporary hit points, damage and healing, Hit Dice, death saves, inspiration, exhaustion and conditions, and don or doff your shield and armor with one button — AC recalculates automatically. Everything is saved with the character. Add and remove notes as you like.

Is multiclassing supported?

Yes. Ability score requirements are checked automatically, and spell slots are based on your combined spellcaster level.

Can I convert a character from the 2014 rules to 2024?

No: the edition is chosen when creating a character and can't be changed later — origins, feats and spells work differently in the 2024 rules. Build a new character with the 2024 rules; the old one stays in your list.

Where are my characters stored?

Without signing in — only in your browser on this device. With an account — on the site, and only you can see them.

Are there subclasses and spells from supplements?

Yes. For the 2014 rules — the artificer, Tasha's Cauldron, Xanathar's Guide, Monsters of the Multiverse, setting and adventure books. For the 2024 rules — Heroes of Faerûn, Eberron: Forge of the Artificer, Ravenloft: The Horrors Within, Arcana Unleashed and more, plus, for compatibility, subclasses, spells and species from 2014 books. Both editions include a catalog of magic items.
