Cleric (2014): Twilight Domain

The strongest cleric of Tasha’s Cauldron: Twilight Sanctuary hands out Temporary Hit Points to the whole party every round.

Why It’s Strong

  • Twilight Sanctuary: for 1 minute, each ally ending their turn in the 30-ft. sphere gains 1d6 + cleric level Temporary Hit Points or ends the Charmed or Frightened condition.
  • Heavy armor and martial weapons, 300-ft. darkvision and Advantage on Initiative for an ally.
  • Steps of Night (6): flight in darkness; Radiant Divine Strike or Blessed Strikes (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • Tasha’s Cauldron expands the cleric list: Spirit Shroud, Summon Celestial, Sunbeam; Harness Divine Power restores spell slots.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Twilight Domain (from level 1).
  • Race: Dwarf — Hill Dwarf.
  • Customizing Your Origin (Tasha’s Cauldron): Wisdom +2, Constitution +1 instead of the race’s book bonuses.
  • Background: Acolyte.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Wisdom 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Strength 13, Charisma 12, Intelligence 10, Dexterity 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, Religion.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Additional Cleric Spells, Channel Divinity: Harness Divine Power, Cantrip Versatility, Blessed Strikes.
  • Equipment: Chain Mail, Shield, Warhammer, Holy Symbol, Priest’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Fey-Touched feat (+1 Wisdom)
  • Level 8: Wisdom +2
  • Level 12: War Caster feat
  • Level 16: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 19: Constitution +2

Level by Level

  • 1: Hill Dwarf with Customizing Your Origin (Tasha’s Cauldron): Wisdom +2, Constitution +1, +1 Hit Point per level, heavy armor doesn’t slow you. Twilight Domain: heavy armor, martial weapons, Eyes of Night, Vigilant Blessing.
  • 2: Twilight Sanctuary; Harness Divine Power (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 4: Fey-Touched (+1 Wisdom): Misty Step and Bless once a day without a slot.
  • 5: Spirit Guardians, Spirit Shroud (Tasha’s Cauldron); Aura of Vitality and Leomund’s Tiny Hut always prepared.
  • 6: Steps of Night: flight in dim light and darkness.
  • 8: Wisdom 20; Blessed Strikes (Tasha’s Cauldron): +1d8 Radiant to a cantrip or weapon hit.
  • 9: Summon Celestial (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 10: Divine Intervention.
  • 12: War Caster.
  • 16: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 17: Twilight Shroud: Half Cover inside the sanctuary.

Spells

Cantrips: Sacred Flame, Guidance, Light, Thaumaturgy, Spare the Dying.

Prepared (by priority): Spirit Guardians, Bless, Healing Word, Guiding Bolt, Shield of Faith, Spiritual Weapon, Aid, Revivify, Hold Person, Command, Lesser Restoration, Spirit Shroud, Dispel Magic, Mass Healing Word, Banishment, Death Ward, Freedom of Movement, Summon Celestial, Greater Restoration, Scrying, Heal, Sunbeam, Heroes’ Feast, Regenerate, Fire Storm, Sunburst, Holy Aura, Power Word Heal, Mass Heal, Gate.

How to Play

  • Twilight Sanctuary in the first round of a hard fight: Temporary Hit Points every turn often cover all the enemies’ damage.
  • Cast Spirit Guardians and stand among the enemies — the sanctuary sphere moves with you.
  • Harness Divine Power: trade Channel Divinity for a spell slot when you don’t need the sanctuary.

Alternatives

  • Peace Domain (Tasha’s Cauldron) — bonds between allies, one of the strongest domains; Forge Domain (Xanathar’s) — AC 20+ already at low levels.
  • The Watchers Dark Gift or the Pious supernatural gift — with your DM’s approval, chosen at the Race step.
  • Peace Domain (Tasha’s Cauldron) — Emboldening Bond for the whole party.
  • Life Domain — the strongest healing spells.
