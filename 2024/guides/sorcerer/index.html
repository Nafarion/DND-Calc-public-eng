Sorcerer: Aberrant Sorcery

Metamagic plus a dozen free origin spells: a Quickened Fireball and a Twinned Hold Monster.

Why It’s Strong

  • Aberrant Sorcery gives you 10 always-prepared spells — a huge plus for a class with few spells of its own.
  • The 2024 Innate Sorcery: +1 to your spell save DC and Advantage on spell attacks; from level 7 (Sorcery Incarnate) you can apply two Metamagic options to one spell while it’s active.
  • Quickened Spell and Twinned Spell — two spells per turn and double control.
  • Psionic Sorcery from level 6 lets you cast your origin spells without components.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Aberrant Sorcery (from level 3).
  • Species: Human, Alert feat.
  • Background: Charlatan — Skilled feat, bonuses: Charisma +2, Constitution +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Charisma 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 12, Intelligence 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Arcana, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, Stealth.
  • Equipment: Spear, Dagger ×2, Arcane Focus, Dungeoneer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: War Caster feat (+1 Charisma)
  • Level 8: Charisma +2
  • Level 12: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 16: Spell Sniper feat (+1 Wisdom)
  • Level 19: Boon of Spell Recall feat (+1 Charisma)

Level by Level

  • 1: Human: Alert. Charlatan background: Skilled — three skills. Innate Sorcery. Shield and Mage Armor.
  • 2: Font of Magic and Metamagic: Quickened Spell and Twinned Spell.
  • 3: Aberrant Sorcery: Arms of Hadar, Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers and Mind Sliver always prepared. Telepathic Speech.
  • 4: War Caster (+1 Charisma).
  • 5: Fireball, Counterspell; Hunger of Hadar and Sending always prepared. Sorcerous Restoration.
  • 6: Psionic Sorcery; Resistance to Psychic damage.
  • 7: Evard’s Black Tentacles and Summon Aberration always prepared. Sorcery Incarnate.
  • 8: Charisma 20.
  • 10: Metamagic: Empowered Spell and Careful Spell.
  • 12: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 14: Revelation in Flesh.
  • 16: Spell Sniper.
  • 17: Metamagic: Subtle Spell and Distant Spell.
  • 18: Warping Implosion.
  • 19: Boon of Spell Recall (Charisma 21).
  • 20: Arcane Apotheosis.

Spells

Cantrips: Sorcerous Burst, Fire Bolt, Prestidigitation, Mage Hand, Minor Illusion, Light.

Prepared (by priority): Shield, Mage Armor, Chromatic Orb, Sleep, Misty Step, Hold Person, Web, Mirror Image, Fireball, Counterspell, Haste, Hypnotic Pattern, Greater Invisibility, Polymorph, Banishment, Synaptic Static, Wall of Stone, Hold Monster, Chain Lightning, Disintegrate, Teleport, Plane Shift, Dominate Monster, Incendiary Cloud, Wish, Time Stop.

How to Play

  • Innate Sorcery as a Bonus Action before a strong spell — +1 to the DC.
  • A Twinned Hold Person or Haste — two effects for one slot.
  • Keep Shield and Counterspell for your Reaction; War Caster protects your Concentration.

Alternatives

  • Spellfire Sorcery (Heroes of Faerûn) — one of the strongest sorcerers; Shadow Sorcery (Ravenloft); compatible option — Divine Soul (Xanathar’s).
  • The Spell Resistant feat (Arcana Unleashed) — protection against enemy magic.
  • Clockwork Sorcery — party protection and Restore Balance.
  • Draconic Sorcery — more Hit Points and AC, flight from level 14.
