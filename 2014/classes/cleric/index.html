Cleric

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. An intermediary between mortals and the gods. The cleric heals, protects and smites in the name of their deity.

Class features

Level 1 Spellcasting

Each day you prepare spells from the whole cleric list: the number prepared equals your cleric level + Wisdom modifier (minimum one). A holy symbol serves as your focus.

Level 1 Divine Domain

You choose your deity's domain: it grants features and always-prepared spells.

Level 2 Channel Divinity

Once per Short or Long Rest (twice from level 6, three times from level 18) you channel divine power. Turn Undead: undead within 30 ft. that fail a Wisdom saving throw flee from you for 1 minute. Your domain grants another option.

Level 2 Divine Domain feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Divine Domain).

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 5 Destroy Undead

Turned undead of CR 1/2 or lower are destroyed (CR 1 from level 8, 2 from level 11, 3 from level 14, 4 from level 17).

Level 6 Channel Divinity (2/rest)

You can use Channel Divinity twice between rests.

Level 6 Divine Domain feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Divine Domain).

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 8 Destroy Undead (CR 1)

Turned undead of CR 1 or lower are destroyed.

Level 8 Divine Domain feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Divine Domain).

Level 10 Divine Intervention

As an action you call on your deity for aid: if you roll no higher than your cleric level on a d100, your deity intervenes. On a success you can't use it again for 7 days, otherwise until after a Long Rest.

Level 11 Destroy Undead (CR 2)

Turned undead of CR 2 or lower are destroyed.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 14 Destroy Undead (CR 3)

Turned undead of CR 3 or lower are destroyed.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 17 Destroy Undead (CR 4)

Turned undead of CR 4 or lower are destroyed.

Level 17 Divine Domain feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Divine Domain).

Level 18 Channel Divinity (3/rest)

You can use Channel Divinity three times between rests.

Level 19 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 20 Divine Intervention Improvement

Your call for divine intervention succeeds automatically.

Subclasses: Divine Domain

  • Life DomainGods of healing and vitality. Clerics of this domain are the finest healers.
  • Light DomainGods of the sun, fire and truth. Clerics of this domain burn their foes and drive back darkness.
  • Knowledge DomainGods of learning, secrets and revelation. Clerics of this domain gather knowledge and read minds.
  • Nature DomainGods of forests, beasts and fertility. Clerics of this domain command animals and plants.
  • Tempest DomainGods of thunder, sea and sky. Clerics of this domain call down lightning and thunderclaps upon their foes.
  • Trickery DomainGods of mischief, thievery and pranks. Clerics of this domain confound their foes with illusions.
  • War DomainGods of battle and valor. Clerics of this domain fight in the front ranks.
  • Order DomainTasha's Cauldron of EverythingGods of law and order. Clerics of this domain lead their allies and subjugate their foes.
  • Peace DomainTasha's Cauldron of EverythingGods of harmony and friendship. Clerics of this domain bind their allies together.
  • Twilight DomainTasha's Cauldron of EverythingGods of twilight and sleep. Clerics of this domain watch over travelers in the dark.
  • Forge DomainXanathar's Guide to EverythingGods of craft and smithing. Their clerics forge weapons and armor for the righteous.
  • Grave DomainXanathar's Guide to EverythingKeepers of the line between life and death: they lay undead to rest and keep others from dying before their time.
  • Arcana DomainSword Coast Adventurer's GuideGods who guard the secrets of magic: Azuth, Mystra, Corellon Larethian, the moon gods of Krynn.
  • Death DomainDungeon Master's GuideGods of death, undeath and negative energy: Myrkul, Chemosh, Wee Jas, as well as of murder, pain and disease — Bhaal, Loviatar, Talona. A Dungeon Master's Guide subclass for evil characters: it isn't in the set available by default — take it only with your DM's permission.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 1 Expanded spells

New spells from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything are added to the cleric spell list — you can choose them on the Spells step.

Level 2 Channel Divinity: Harness Divine Power

As a bonus action, expend a use of Channel Divinity to regain a spell slot of a level no higher than half your proficiency bonus (rounded up). Once per long rest (twice from 6th level, three times from 18th).

Level 4 Cantrip Versatility

When you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace one cantrip of the class with another from its list.

Level 8 Blessed Strikes

Instead of the domain's Divine Strike or Potent Spellcasting: once per turn a cantrip or weapon attack deals an extra 1d8 radiant damage. Replaces: Divine Strike, Potent Spellcasting.

Starting equipment

  • Mace, Scale Mail, Light Crossbow, Bolts (20), Priest's Pack, Shield, Holy Symbol
  • Starting wealth: about 125 GP
