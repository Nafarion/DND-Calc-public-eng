Ranger

D&D 2024 class. A hunter on the edge of civilization. The ranger tracks prey where others get lost and wields the magic of the wilds.

Class features

Level 1 Spellcasting

You cast ranger spells using Wisdom as your spellcasting ability.

Level 1 Favored Enemy

You always have Hunter's Mark prepared and can cast it without expending a slot. Uses equal your Proficiency Bonus.

Level 1 Weapon Mastery

You can use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons.

Level 2 Deft Explorer

You gain Expertise in one skill and know two additional languages.

Level 2 Fighting Style

You gain a Fighting Style feat or the Druidic Warrior option (two druid cantrips).

Fighting styles: Archery, Blind Fighting, Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Interception, Protection, Thrown Weapon Fighting, Two-Weapon Fighting, Unarmed Fighting, Arcane Warrior.

Level 3 Ranger Subclass

You choose a ranger archetype.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 5 Extra Attack

You attack twice instead of once when you take the Attack action.

Level 6 Roving

Your speed increases by 10 ft., and you gain a climb and swim speed equal to your speed.

Level 7 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen archetype.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 9 Expertise

Two more skills get double your Proficiency Bonus.

Level 10 Tireless

As an action you gain 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier temporary hit points (uses equal your Wisdom modifier). A Short Rest removes one level of exhaustion.

Level 11 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen archetype.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 13 Relentless Hunter

Damage can't break your concentration on Hunter's Mark.

Level 14 Nature's Veil

As a bonus action you become invisible until the end of your next turn.

Level 15 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen archetype.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 17 Precise Hunter

You have Advantage on attacks against a target marked by Hunter's Mark.

Level 18 Feral Senses

You gain blindsight with a range of 30 feet.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Epic Boons: Boon of Combat Prowess, Boon of Dimensional Travel, Boon of Energy Resistance, Boon of Fate, Boon of Fortitude, Boon of Irresistible Offense, Boon of Recovery, Boon of Skill, Boon of Speed, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Spell Recall, Boon of Truesight, Boon of Bloodshed, Boon of the Furious Storm, Boon of the Soul Drinker, Boon of the Bright Sun, Boon of Terror, Boon of Revelry, Boon of Poison Mastery, Boon of Fortune’s Favor, Boon of Exquisite Radiance, Boon of Fluid Forms, Boon of Desperate Resilience, Boon of Communication, Boon of Bountiful Health, Boon of Siberys, Boon of Erupting Spellpower, Boon of the Iron Mind, Boon of Magic School Mastery, Boon of Blazing Dawn, Boon of Looming Shadows, Boon of Misty Escape.

Level 20 Foe Slayer

Hunter's Mark's damage die becomes a d10.

Subclasses: Ranger Subclass

  • Beast MasterA ranger who fights side by side with a primal companion — a beast bound to them by magic.
  • Fey WandererA ranger marked by the Feywild: their strikes carry Psychic damage, and their speech is strangely persuasive.
  • Gloom StalkerA ranger of darkness and the first strike. They see where it's dark even for Darkvision.
  • HunterA versatile ranger who adapts tactics to specific prey — from a lone giant to a horde.
  • Winter WalkerForgotten Realms: Heroes of FaerûnRuthless rangers of Icewind Dale: they hunt the monsters of the arctic wastes and become the terror of the ice themselves.
  • Hollow WardenRavenloft: The Horrors WithinRangers who draw power from the ancient horrors of the wilds and become ruthless monstrous guardians.
