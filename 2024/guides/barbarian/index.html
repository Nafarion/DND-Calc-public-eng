Barbarian: Berserker with a Greatsword

The simplest and most reliable source of damage in the game: Rage, Reckless Attack and Great Weapon Master.

Why It’s Strong

  • Reckless Attack gives Advantage on every Strength attack, so misses are rare and Critical Hits happen twice as often.
  • The 2024 Berserker’s Frenzy adds as many d6s as your Rage Damage bonus (up to 4d6) to your first hit each turn — with no Exhaustion, unlike in 2014.
  • Great Weapon Master adds your Proficiency Bonus to every hit with a Heavy weapon and grants a Bonus Action attack after a Critical Hit or a kill.
  • Resistance to physical damage while raging and a d12 Hit Die make the barbarian very hard to kill.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Path of the Berserker (from level 3).
  • Species: Goliath — Fire Giant.
  • Background: Soldier — Savage Attacker feat, bonuses: Strength +2, Constitution +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Strength 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 12, Charisma 10, Intelligence 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Perception, Survival.
  • Weapon Mastery: Greatsword, Handaxe, Maul, Javelin (as you gain them).
  • Equipment: Greatsword, Handaxe ×4, Javelin ×4, Explorer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Great Weapon Master feat (+1 Strength)
  • Level 8: Strength +2
  • Level 12: Constitution +2
  • Level 16: Durable feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 19: Boon of Irresistible Offense feat (+1 Strength)

Level by Level

  • 1: Rage, Unarmored Defense (AC 10 + Dexterity + Constitution), Weapon Mastery: Greatsword (Graze — damage even on a miss) and Handaxe (Vex) for throwing.
  • 2: Reckless Attack — use it almost always, unless you’re surrounded.
  • 3: Path of the Berserker: Frenzy. Primal Knowledge — one more skill.
  • 4: Great Weapon Master feat (+1 Strength). Third mastery: Maul (Topple) — knock the enemy Prone so the whole party attacks it with Advantage.
  • 5: Extra Attack and Fast Movement (+10 ft.).
  • 8: Strength +2 (20).
  • 9: Brutal Strike: forgo Advantage on one attack for +1d10 and a push or a slow.
  • 10: Retaliation: a Reaction attack against whoever hurt you in melee.
  • 12: Constitution +2.
  • 14: Intimidating Presence: mass fear as a Bonus Action.
  • 16: Durable (+1 Constitution): Advantage on Death Saving Throws and healing with Hit Point Dice as a Bonus Action.
  • 19: Epic Boon: Boon of Irresistible Offense (+1 Strength to 21): your attacks ignore Resistance.
  • 20: Primal Champion: Strength and Constitution +4 (Strength 25).

How to Play

  • Rage as a Bonus Action on your very first turn, then attack recklessly.
  • Against a single target, use the greatsword: Graze guarantees damage even on a miss. Against crowds and big foes, switch to the maul and Topple.
  • The Fire Giant legacy adds 1d10 Fire damage to a hit — a number of times equal to your Proficiency Bonus per Long Rest.
  • Reckless Attack makes you easy to hit — that’s fine: you have Resistance to physical damage and lots of Hit Points.

Alternatives

  • Compatible 2014 options (with the DM’s approval): Path of the Giant (Bigby), Path of the Ancestral Guardian and Path of the Storm Herald (Xanathar’s).
  • Heroes of Faerûn backgrounds with the Purple Dragon Rook or Zhentarim Ruffian feat — combat-oriented origin feats.
  • Path of the Wild Heart (Bear): Resistance to almost all damage — the best tank in the game.
  • Path of the World Tree: teleports enemies to you and allies around you — strong battlefield control.
  • Dwarf instead of goliath: +1 Hit Point per level and Resistance to Poison.
