Ranger (2014): Fey Wanderer with a Longbow

Tasha’s Cauldron fixed the ranger: Favored Foe, Deft Explorer and a Fey Wanderer adding Wisdom to every Charisma check.

Why It’s Strong

  • Dreadful Strikes: +1d4 Psychic damage on a hit (1d6 from level 11), plus Favored Foe (Tasha’s Cauldron) — damage like Hunter’s Mark, but without a slot (it still needs Concentration).
  • Otherworldly Glamour: Wisdom added to Charisma checks — the ranger becomes the party’s face.
  • Deft Explorer: Expertise (1), +5 ft. Speed, climbing and swimming (6), Temporary Hit Points (10).
  • The Archery style and Sharpshooter; Misty Step and Summon Fey for free.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Fey Wanderer (from level 3).
  • Race: Variant Human, Sharpshooter feat.
  • Background: Outlander.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Dexterity 15 + 1 = 16, Wisdom 14 + 1 = 15, Constitution 13, Strength 12, Charisma 10, Intelligence 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Athletics, Insight, Nature, Perception (Expertise), Persuasion, Stealth, Survival.
  • Fighting Style: Archery.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Favored Foe, Deft Explorer, Primal Awareness, Nature’s Veil, Additional Ranger Spells, Spellcasting Focus, Martial Versatility.
  • Equipment: Leather Armor, Longbow, Arrows (20) ×2, Quiver, Shortsword ×2, Explorer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Dexterity +2
  • Level 8: Dexterity +2
  • Level 12: Wisdom +2
  • Level 16: Alert feat
  • Level 19: Wisdom +1, Constitution +1

Level by Level

  • 1: Variant Human: Dexterity +1, Wisdom +1, Sharpshooter. Favored Foe and Deft Explorer (Tasha’s Cauldron): Expertise in Perception.
  • 2: Archery style, spells, Spellcasting Focus.
  • 3: Fey Wanderer: Dreadful Strikes, Otherworldly Glamour (Persuasion), Charm Person. Primal Awareness (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 4: Dexterity 18; Martial Versatility.
  • 5: Extra Attack, Misty Step, Summon Beast.
  • 6: Deft Explorer: +5 ft. Speed, climbing and swimming.
  • 7: Beguiling Twist.
  • 8: Dexterity 20.
  • 10: Deft Explorer: Temporary Hit Points; Nature’s Veil — invisibility as a Bonus Action.
  • 11: Fey Reinforcements: Summon Fey without a slot.
  • 12: Wisdom +2.
  • 15: Misty Wanderer.
  • 16: Alert.
  • 19: Wisdom +1, Constitution +1.
  • 20: Foe Slayer.

Spells

Known (by priority): Hunter’s Mark, Goodberry, Entangle, Pass without Trace, Summon Beast, Spike Growth, Conjure Animals, Lightning Arrow, Conjure Barrage, Summon Elemental, Freedom of Movement, Swift Quiver, Conjure Volley, Greater Restoration, Cure Wounds, Silence, Protection from Energy.

How to Play

  • Favored Foe on your first hit if your Concentration is free; when it’s busy with a Summon spell, rely on Dreadful Strikes. Then use Sharpshooter’s −5/+10.
  • Nature’s Veil (10) grants Advantage on your shots — it cancels the −5 penalty.
  • Do the talking in negotiations yourself: Persuasion with Wisdom.

Alternatives

  • Gloom Stalker (Xanathar’s) — the best start to a fight: an extra attack and invisibility in darkness; Drakewarden (Fizban’s).
  • Hunter — the Hunter’s Prey, Defensive Tactics, Multiattack and Superior Hunter’s Defense features are chosen in the builder.
  • Swarmkeeper (Tasha’s Cauldron) — control through movement.
  • Hunter — Colossus Slayer and Volley.
