Artificer

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything. A maker who infuses magic into objects. The artificer casts spells through tools, grants allies' gear wondrous properties and finds a solution to any problem.

Class features

Level 1 Magical Tinkering

As an action with thieves' tools or artisan's tools, you give a Tiny nonmagical object one property: light, a recorded message, a sound, an odor or a picture on its surface. You can have such objects up to your Intelligence modifier (minimum one) at a time.

Level 1 Spellcasting

Each day you prepare artificer spells: half your artificer level + Intelligence modifier (minimum one). You cast through thieves' tools, artisan's tools or (from level 2) an item bearing your infusion. A prepared spell with the ritual tag can be cast as a ritual.

Level 2 Infuse Item

You know infusions (4 at level 2, 6 at level 6, 8 at level 10, 10 at level 14, 12 at level 18) and after a Long Rest infuse them into nonmagical objects, making them magic items. You can have infused items at once: 2, 3 from level 6, 4 from level 10, 5 from level 14, 6 from level 18. 2nd-level infusions: Enhanced Arcane Focus, Enhanced Defense, Enhanced Weapon, Homunculus Servant, Mind Sharpener, Repeating Shot, Replicate Magic Item, Returning Weapon. From level 6: Boots of the Winding Path, Radiant Weapon, Repulsion Shield, Resistant Armor, Spell-Refueling Ring; from level 10 — Armor of Magical Strength, Helm of Awareness; from level 14 — Arcane Propulsion Armor.

Level 3 Artificer Specialist

You choose a specialization: Alchemist, Armorer, Artillerist or Battle Smith. It grants spells, proficiencies and features.

Level 3 The Right Tool for the Job

After 1 hour of work (possibly during a rest) with thieves' tools or artisan's tools, you create a set of any nonmagical artisan's tools nearby. It vanishes when you use this feature again.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Level 5 Artificer Specialist feature

You gain a feature from your chosen specialization.

Level 6 Tool Expertise

Double your Proficiency Bonus for checks with any tools you're proficient with.

Level 7 Flash of Genius

As a reaction, when you or a creature within 30 ft. makes an ability check or saving throw, add your Intelligence modifier to the roll. Uses equal your Intelligence modifier per Long Rest.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

Level 9 Artificer Specialist feature

You gain a feature from your chosen specialization.

Level 10 Magic Item Adept

You can attune to four magic items. Crafting common and uncommon magic items takes a quarter of the time and costs half as much.

Level 11 Spell-Storing Item

After a Long Rest you store an artificer spell of 1st or 2nd level with a casting time of an action in a weapon or focus. The holder can cast it as an action using your ability. Uses equal twice your Intelligence modifier (minimum two).

You can attune to five magic items and ignore class, species, spell and level requirements for attuning to and using them.

Level 20 Soul of Artifice

+1 to saving throws for each magic item you're attuned to. If you drop to 0 hit points but aren't killed, you can use your reaction to end one of your infusions and drop to 1 hit point instead.

Infuse Item

After a long rest you infuse them into nonmagical items (2 items at a time, from 6th level 3, from 10th 4, from 14th 5, from 18th 6). When you gain a level, you can replace one known infusion.
