Paladin: Oath of Devotion with a Glaive

An aura of protection for the whole party, accurate blows with Sacred Weapon and a Bonus Action attack with the haft.

Why It’s Strong

  • Aura of Protection from level 6 adds your Charisma to all saving throws — yours and nearby allies’.
  • Sacred Weapon adds Charisma to your attacks for 10 minutes — near-guaranteed hits.
  • Polearm Master grants a Bonus Action attack with the haft and an attack against an enemy entering your reach.
  • Divine Smite now uses a Bonus Action and works once per turn — but it’s still the best burst damage against one target.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Oath of Devotion (from level 3).
  • Species: Aasimar — Heavenly Wings.
  • Background: Noble — Skilled feat, bonuses: Strength +2, Charisma +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Strength 15 + 2 = 17, Charisma 14 + 1 = 15, Constitution 13, Wisdom 12, Dexterity 10, Intelligence 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Perception, Persuasion.
  • Weapon Mastery: Glaive, Javelin (as you gain them).
  • Fighting Style: Great Weapon Fighting.
  • Equipment: Chain Mail, Glaive, Javelin ×6, Holy Symbol, Priest’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Polearm Master feat (+1 Strength)
  • Level 8: Great Weapon Master feat (+1 Strength)
  • Level 12: Strength +1, Charisma +1
  • Level 16: Charisma +2
  • Level 19: Boon of Combat Prowess feat (+1 Strength)

Level by Level

  • 1: Aasimar: Healing Hands, and Celestial Revelation (wings) from level 3. Noble background: the Skilled feat — three skills. Weapon Mastery: glaive (Graze) and javelin (Slow).
  • 2: Great Weapon Fighting style; Divine Smite always prepared.
  • 3: Oath of Devotion: Sacred Weapon. Protection from Evil and Good and Shield of Faith always prepared.
  • 4: Polearm Master (+1 Strength): a 1d4 Bonus Action attack with the haft.
  • 5: Extra Attack; Find Steed always prepared.
  • 6: Aura of Protection.
  • 7: Aura of Devotion: Immunity to the Charmed condition for you and nearby allies.
  • 8: Great Weapon Master (+1 Strength): + Proficiency Bonus to glaive damage.
  • 9: Abjure Foes.
  • 11: Radiant Strikes: +1d8 Radiant to every hit.
  • 12: Strength 20, Charisma +1.
  • 15: Smite of Protection: Half Cover for the party when you smite.
  • 16: Charisma +2.
  • 18: Auras reach 30 ft.
  • 19: Boon of Combat Prowess (Strength 21).
  • 20: Holy Nimbus.

Spells

Prepared (by priority): Bless, Command, Heroism, Cure Wounds, Compelled Duel, Shining Smite, Magic Weapon, Lesser Restoration, Prayer of Healing, Aura of Vitality, Revivify, Spirit Shroud, Crusader’s Mantle, Elemental Weapon, Death Ward, Banishment, Aura of Purity, Aura of Life, Summon Celestial, Circle of Power, Banishing Smite, Destructive Wave.

How to Play

  • First turn: Sacred Weapon (Channel Divinity), then glaive attacks; your Bonus Action — the haft or a smite.
  • Keep the party within your aura — +Charisma to saves often matters more than your damage.
  • Smite on a Critical Hit: the smite dice are doubled too.
  • Once you have 1,500 GP, buy plate: your Strength 15 is enough, and your AC becomes 18 (20 with a shield if you switch to a one-handed weapon).

Alternatives

  • Oath of the Noble Genies (Heroes of Faerûn) — AC without armor and elemental magic; compatible option — Oath of Conquest (Xanathar’s).
  • Forge of the Artificer feats: a dragonmark (Mark of Sentinel) — sentinel spells.
  • Oath of Glory — Temporary Hit Points for the party when you smite and +10 ft. Speed.
  • Oath of Vengeance — Vow of Enmity for Advantage on attacks and Haste at level 9.
