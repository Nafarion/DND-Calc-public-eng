Ranger

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. A hunter and guide on the edge of civilization. The ranger tracks favored enemies and knows the wilds like the back of their hand.

Class features

Level 1 Favored Enemy

Choose a type of favored enemy (e.g. dragons or undead, or two humanoid races). Advantage on Survival checks to track them and Intelligence checks to recall information about them; you learn one of their languages. Another type at levels 6 and 14.

Level 1 Natural Explorer

Choose a favored terrain. There you get double your Proficiency Bonus on related Intelligence and Wisdom checks, and travel grants many benefits. Another terrain at levels 6 and 10.

Level 2 Fighting Style

You choose a Fighting Style.

Fighting styles: Archery, Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection, Two-Weapon Fighting, Blind Fighting, Interception, Superior Technique, Thrown Weapon Fighting, Unarmed Fighting, Blessed Warrior, Druidic Warrior.

Level 2 Spellcasting

You know ranger spells; your spellcasting ability is Wisdom. The number of known spells grows with your level.

Level 3 Ranger Archetype

You choose a ranger archetype.

Level 3 Primeval Awareness

By expending a spell slot, for 1 minute per slot level you sense within 1 mile (6 miles in favored terrain) the presence of favored enemies and certain other creatures.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 5 Extra Attack

You attack twice when you take the Attack action.

Level 6 Favored Enemy (2nd type)

Choose another type of favored enemy and learn one of its languages.

Level 6 Natural Explorer (2nd terrain)

Choose another favored terrain.

Level 7 Ranger Archetype feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Ranger Archetype).

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 8 Land's Stride

Nonmagical difficult terrain doesn't slow you, and plants don't harm you. Advantage on saving throws against magical plants.

Level 10 Hide in Plain Sight

After spending a minute camouflaging yourself, you gain +10 to Stealth while you don't move.

Level 10 Natural Explorer (3rd terrain)

Choose another favored terrain.

Level 11 Ranger Archetype feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Ranger Archetype).

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 14 Vanish

You can take the Hide action as a bonus action; you can't be tracked by nonmagical means.

Level 14 Favored Enemy (3rd type)

Choose another type of favored enemy.

Level 15 Ranger Archetype feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Ranger Archetype).

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 18 Feral Senses

You sense invisible creatures within 30 ft. and attack them without Disadvantage.

Level 19 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 20 Foe Slayer

Once per turn you add your Wisdom modifier to the attack roll or damage roll against a favored enemy.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 1 Expanded spells

New spells from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything are added to the ranger spell list — you can choose them on the Spells step.

Level 1 Favored Foe

When you hit a creature, you can mark it for 1 minute (concentration): once per turn your attacks against it deal an extra 1d4 damage (1d6 from 6th level, 1d8 from 14th). Number of uses — your proficiency bonus per long rest. Replaces: Favored Enemy, Favored Enemy (2nd type), Favored Enemy (3rd type).

Level 1 Deft Explorer (Canny)

Expertise in one skill you're proficient in, and two languages. Replaces: Natural Explorer, Natural Explorer (2nd terrain), Natural Explorer (3rd terrain).

Level 6 Deft Explorer (Roving)

Your walking speed increases by 5 feet; you gain a climbing speed and a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

Level 10 Deft Explorer (Tireless)

As an action, gain 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier temporary hit points (number of uses — your proficiency bonus per long rest); a short rest removes one level of exhaustion.

Level 2 Fighting Style Options

The Blind Fighting, Druidic Warrior, Interception and Thrown Weapon Fighting styles become available.

Level 2 Spellcasting Focus

You can use a druidic focus as a spellcasting focus for your ranger spells.

Level 3 Primal Awareness

You know Speak with Animals (3), Beast Sense (5), Speak with Plants (9), Locate Creature (13) and Commune with Nature (17); they don't count against your spells known, and each can be cast once per long rest without a spell slot. Replaces: Primeval Awareness.

Level 4 Martial Versatility

When you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace a fighting style with another available one (a fighter can also replace a Battle Master maneuver).

Level 10 Nature's Veil

As a bonus action, you become invisible until the end of your next turn. Number of uses — your proficiency bonus per long rest. Replaces: Hide in Plain Sight.

Level 3 Primal Companion

Beast Master: instead of the usual companion, a beast of the land, sea or sky whose statistics grow with your level; it acts after you, attacks when you command it as a bonus action, and from 5th level in place of one of your attacks. Replaces: Ranger's Companion.

Starting equipment
