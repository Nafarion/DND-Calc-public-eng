Deck of Many More Things

Wondrous item, legendary

Type
Wondrous items
Rarity
Legendary
Attunement
not required
Source
The Book of Many Things
Suggested price
from 50,001 gp

Properties

44 additional cards for the Deck of Many Things; often combined with it into a single deck of 66 cards. The drawing rules are the same: declare the number of cards in advance and draw them one at a time, no later than an hour after the previous one, otherwise the remaining ones take effect at once; extra cards have no effect. Each card except the Fool and the Jester returns to the deck after it takes effect and can be drawn again.

Card selection (d100): 1 Aberration, 2 Balance*, 3 Beast, 4 Book, 5 Bridge, 6 Campfire, 7 Cavern, 8 Celestial, 9 Comet*, 10 Construct, 11 Corpse, 12 Crossroads, 13 Donjon*, 14 Door, 15 Dragon, 16 Elemental, 17 Euryale*, 18 Expert, 19 Fates*, 20 Fey, 21 Fiend, 22 Flames*, 23 Fool*, 24 Gem*, 25 Giant, 26 Humanoid, 27 Jester*, 28 Key*, 29 Knight*, 30 Lance, 31 Mage, 32 Map, 33 Maze, 34 Mine, 35 Monster, 36 Moon*, 37 Ooze, 38 Path, 39 Pit, 40 Plant, 41 Priest, 42 Prisoner, 43 Idiot*, 44 Ring, 45 Rogue*, 46 Ruin*, 47 Vizier*, 48 Shield, 49 Ship, 50 Skull*, 51 Staff, 52 Stairway, 53 Star*, 54 Statue, 55 Sun*, 56 Talons*, 57 Tavern, 58 Temple, 59 Throne*, 60 Tomb, 61 Tower, 62 Tree, 63 Undead, 64 Void*, 65 Warrior, 66 Well, 67–100 reroll. Cards marked * work as in the Deck of Many Things.

New cards:

Aberration — telepathy 90 ft. Beast — for 2d12 days you turn into a random beast with a CR no higher than 5 (statistics are replaced, nonmagical gear melds into the form, magic items fall nearby); only wish changes the form. Book — 1d6 + 2 languages of your choice. Bridge — time stop 1d3 times. Campfire — you immediately gain the benefits of a long rest. Cavern — a climbing speed equal to your walking speed, walking on walls and ceilings without hands. Celestial — wings, flying speed 30 ft. Construct — a homunculus appears that considers you its creator. Corpse — your hit points drop to 0, you're unconscious and make death saving throws; healing doesn't work until you're stabilized; if you die, only wish can bring you back. Crossroads — d20: even — you age 1d10 years, odd — you become 1d10 years younger (minimum 1 year). Door — gate 1d4 times without material components. Dragon — a wyrmling devoted to you hatches (the DM chooses the kind). Elemental — immunity to acid, cold, fire, lightning or thunder damage (your choice). Expert — Dexterity +2 (maximum 22). Fey — you're pulled into the Feywild and draw no more cards (the passage stays open for another minute). Fiend — a powerful fiend offers a deal: a reward for a task; it keeps the bargain but may twist the wording. Giant — height +2d10 inches, hit point maximum and current hit points +20. Humanoid — you can stop drawing cards immediately. Lance — all ability scores +1 (maximum 20). Mage — Intelligence +2 (maximum 22). Map — once within a year you learn the location and distance to a known creature or object (even on another plane), as well as whether it's alive and its conditions / whether the object is intact and how many charges it has. Maze — 1d3 levels of exhaustion. Mine — 2d6 gems worth 5,000 gp each and 1d10 ingots of ore worth 2,500 gp each. Monster — a hostile Large or smaller monster with a CR no higher than 10 immediately attacks; it vanishes when killed or when your hit points drop to 0. Ooze — a gelatinous cube appears in your space and engulfs you. Path — walking speed +10 ft. Pit — you fall from a height of 3d6 × 10 ft. and are knocked prone. Plant — speak with plants without a slot once per long rest (or for a slot of 3rd level or higher). Priest — Wisdom +2 (maximum 22). Prisoner — magical chains restrain you: while restrained, you can't cast spells, the properties of your magic items are suppressed, and you draw no more cards; the chains are indestructible and can't be dispelled, but disintegrate destroys them or another creature can remove them with a DC 30 Dexterity check with thieves' tools. Ring — a rare or rarer magic ring appears on your finger (you attune automatically if you have room). Shield — rare or rarer magic armor you're proficient with; if you aren't proficient with armor, your AC without armor is 12 + your Dexterity modifier. Ship — proficiency in three skills (the DM chooses). Staff — a rare or rarer rod, staff or wand. Stairway — reduce the number of remaining cards by 2 or gain a rare or rarer wondrous item. Statue — you're petrified until greater restoration or similar magic. Tavern — Charisma +2 (maximum 22). Temple — a deity must answer your call once as an action (divine intervention); if you don't use it before you die, it resurrects you (resurrection). Tomb — once within a year, true resurrection without a slot or material components. Tower — draw 2 extra cards, keep one (it takes effect) and return the other. Tree — bark-like skin: AC without armor 15 + Dexterity modifier, but vulnerability to fire (removed only by wish or divine intervention). Undead — a revenant rises and hunts you for a year until it kills you or is banished by wish. Warrior — Strength +2 (maximum 22). Well — three cantrips of your choice from any lists.

For the cards' spells, the spellcasting ability is Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma of your choice. Crossroads is undone only by wish, divine intervention or similar magic.
