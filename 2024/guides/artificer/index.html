Artificer: Battle Smith with a Shield and a Steel Defender

An Intelligence-based fighter: a magic weapon, a shield, a steel defender at your side and useful items for the whole party.

Why It’s Strong

  • Battle Ready: attacks with magic weapons use Intelligence, so the whole build rests on one ability score.
  • The Steel Defender is a second body on the battlefield: its Reaction hinders enemies attacking your allies, and it attacks too.
  • Replicate Magic Item: a +1 weapon, a +1 shield and a Bag of Holding as early as level 2, a +2 weapon from level 10.
  • Warforged (Eberron: Forge of the Artificer): Integrated Protection gives +1 AC and Resistance to Poison; a shield and medium armor give AC 20 by level 6.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Battle Smith (from level 3).
  • Species: Warforged.
  • Background: Sage — Magic Initiate (Wizard) feat, bonuses: Intelligence +2, Constitution +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Intelligence 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 12, Strength 10, Charisma 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Arcana, Athletics, History, Investigation, Perception.
  • Equipment: Scale Mail, Shield, Warhammer, Light Crossbow, Bolts (20), Thieves’ Tools, Tinker’s Tools, Dungeoneer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: War Caster feat (+1 Intelligence)
  • Level 8: Intelligence +2
  • Level 12: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 16: Alert feat
  • Level 19: Boon of Combat Prowess feat (+1 Intelligence)

Level by Level

  • 1: Warforged: Integrated Protection (+1 AC), Resistance to Poison, the Athletics skill. Sage background: Magic Initiate (Wizard) — Mind Sliver, Prestidigitation and Find Familiar. Tinker’s Magic; artificer spells: Cure Wounds, Faerie Fire.
  • 2: Replicate Magic Item: +1 weapon, +1 shield, Bag of Holding, Sending Stones.
  • 3: Battle Smith: Battle Ready (magic weapon attacks use Intelligence), Steel Defender, Smith’s Tools. Heroism and Shield always prepared.
  • 4: War Caster (+1 Intelligence).
  • 5: Extra Attack: two weapon attacks per action.
  • 6: Magic Item Tinker; the +1 Armor plan.
  • 7: Flash of Genius: add Intelligence to an ally’s check or saving throw.
  • 8: Intelligence 20.
  • 9: Arcane Jolt: extra damage or healing on a hit. 3rd-level slots: Haste, Fly.
  • 10: The +2 Weapon plan.
  • 12: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 15: Improved Defender: the defender and its Reaction get stronger.
  • 16: Alert.
  • 19: Boon of Combat Prowess (Intelligence 21).
  • 20: Soul of Artifice.

Spells

Cantrips: Guidance, True Strike, Mending, Fire Bolt, Shocking Grasp.

Prepared (by priority): Cure Wounds, Faerie Fire, Sanctuary, Longstrider, Grease, Detect Magic, Aid, Enhance Ability, Web, Magic Weapon, Lesser Restoration, Invisibility, Haste, Fly, Dispel Magic, Revivify, Glyph of Warding, Blink, Summon Construct, Freedom of Movement, Stoneskin, Otiluke’s Resilient Sphere, Arcane Eye, Greater Restoration, Animate Objects, Bigby’s Hand, Wall of Stone, Creation, Circle of Power.

How to Play

  • First turn: command the defender with a Bonus Action and attack with your weapon using Intelligence; the defender stands next to your most vulnerable ally.
  • Shield as a Reaction plus a +1 shield make the artificer very hard to hit — hold the front line next to your defender.
  • Concentrate on Haste (on yourself or the fighter) or on Web for control.

Alternatives

  • Armorer (Forge of the Artificer) — stronger defense, armor models can be changed after a rest; Cartographer — support and scouting.
  • Reanimator (Ravenloft) — a necromancer artificer; Artillerist — ranged spell damage.
  • Species: dwarf (+1 Hit Point per level) or human (one more Origin feat).
