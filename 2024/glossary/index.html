D&D 2024 glossary: conditions, actions, weapon properties

Short explanations of the words used in feature and spell descriptions, by the 2024 rules. What changed between the editions: Glossary: 2014 vs 2024.

Conditions · Actions in combat · Weapon properties · Weapon mastery properties · General terms

Conditions

Unconscious
The creature is incapacitated, can't move or speak, is unaware of its surroundings, drops whatever it's holding and falls prone. Attack rolls against it have advantage, and a melee hit from within 5 feet is a critical hit. It automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saving throws.
Frightened
While the source of its fear is within line of sight, the creature has disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls and can't willingly move closer to it.
Exhaustion
Measured in levels: each level reduces your D20 Tests by 2 and your Speed by 5 feet; at level 6 the creature dies. A Long Rest removes one level.
Invisible
The creature has Advantage on Initiative rolls. It isn't affected by effects that require their target to be seen unless the effect's creator can see it. Attack rolls against it have Disadvantage, and its attack rolls have Advantage unless the target can see it.
Incapacitated
The creature can't take actions, Bonus Actions or Reactions and can't speak; Concentration is broken. If it is Incapacitated when it rolls Initiative, it has Disadvantage on the roll.
Petrified
The creature is turned to stone along with its clothing and gear: it is incapacitated, can't move and is unaware of its surroundings, has resistance to all damage and is immune to poison and disease.
Restrained
The creature's speed is 0. Its attack rolls and Dexterity saving throws have disadvantage, and attack rolls against it have advantage.
Deafened
The creature can't hear and automatically fails ability checks that require hearing.
Blinded
The creature can't see and fails ability checks that require sight. Its attack rolls have disadvantage, and attack rolls against it have advantage.
Poisoned
The creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
Charmed
The creature can't attack the charmer or target it with harmful effects, and the charmer has advantage on ability checks to interact with it socially.
Paralyzed
The creature is incapacitated and can't move or speak. It fails Strength and Dexterity saving throws, attack rolls against it have advantage, and a melee hit from within 5 feet is a critical hit.
Prone
The creature lies on the ground: it can only crawl or spend half its speed to stand up. Its attack rolls have disadvantage. Attack rolls against it from within 5 feet have advantage, from farther away — disadvantage.
Grappled
The creature's Speed is 0, and it has Disadvantage on attack rolls against any target other than the grappler. The grappler can drag or carry it. Escaping takes an action — a Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against the grapple's escape DC.
Stunned
The creature is Incapacitated (so it can't speak either). It fails Strength and Dexterity saving throws, and attack rolls against it have Advantage.

Actions in combat

Attack
One weapon attack or Unarmed Strike. Features such as Extra Attack let you make several attacks with this action.
Dash
For the rest of the turn you gain extra movement equal to your speed.
Disengage
Your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks for the rest of the turn.
Evasion
Until the start of your next turn, attack rolls against you have disadvantage if you can see the attacker, and you make Dexterity saving throws with advantage.
Help
An ally gains Advantage on the next check with a skill or tool you're proficient with, or on the next attack roll against an enemy within 5 feet of you whom you distract.
Hide
A DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) check while you are Heavily Obscured or behind Three-Quarters or Total Cover and out of enemies' line of sight. On a success you have the Invisible condition, and the check's total becomes the DC to find you. The condition ends if you make noise, are found, attack or cast a spell with a Verbal component.
Search
A Wisdom check to notice something that isn't obvious: Insight — a creature's state, Medicine — an ailment, Perception — something concealed, Survival — tracks or food.
Study
An Intelligence check to recall or work out something important: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature or Religion, depending on the subject.
Influence
An attempt to sway a creature. The DM decides whether it is willing, unwilling or hesitant; for a hesitant one — a Charisma or Wisdom check against DC 15 or the creature's Intelligence score, whichever is higher. After a failure you can try again in 24 hours.
Use an Object
An action for an object that needs particular attention: drinking a potion, opening a tricky lock and the like.
Ready
You choose a trigger and an action. When the trigger occurs before your next turn, you take the action as a reaction.

Weapon properties

Ammunition
You need ammunition (an arrow, a bolt, a bullet) to attack; after a fight you can recover half of it.
Finesse
You can use Dexterity instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls.
Heavy
Attack rolls with such a weapon have Disadvantage if your Strength (melee) or Dexterity (ranged) is lower than 13.
Light
When you attack with a Light weapon as part of the Attack action, you can attack with a different Light weapon as a Bonus Action, without your ability modifier to the damage (unless it's negative).
Loading
You can fire such a weapon only once per action, Bonus Action or Reaction.
Ranged
Two ranges: normal and long; at long range the attack roll has disadvantage.
Reach
The weapon adds 5 feet to your reach when you attack with it.
Thrown
You can throw the weapon and make a ranged attack using the same ability as for a melee attack.
Two-Handed
You need both hands to attack with it.
Versatile
Can be used with one or two hands; with two — with a larger damage die.

Weapon mastery properties

Cleave
When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can make an attack against a second creature within 5 feet of the first. The damage doesn't include your ability modifier. Once per turn.
Graze
When you miss with an attack, you still deal damage to the target equal to the ability modifier you used with this weapon.
Nick
You can make the extra attack of two-weapon fighting as part of the Attack action instead of as a Bonus Action. Once per turn.
Push
When you hit a Large or smaller creature with an attack, you push it 10 feet away.
Sap
When you hit with an attack, the target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
Slow
When you hit a creature with an attack, you reduce its Speed by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. Once per turn.
Topple
When you hit with an attack, you force the target to make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + ability modifier + Proficiency Bonus) or fall Prone.
Vex
When you hit with an attack, you have Advantage on your next attack against the same target before the end of your next turn.

General terms

Advantage and Disadvantage
With advantage you roll the d20 twice and use the higher result, with disadvantage — the lower. If you have both, they cancel each other out.
Bonus Action
A quick action in addition to your main one — only what a feature or spell explicitly allows. No more than one per turn.
Reaction
An instant response to a trigger, such as an opportunity attack. One until the start of your next turn.
Concentration
You can maintain only one such effect at a time. When you take damage, make a Constitution saving throw (DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher, up to 30), or Concentration ends. It also ends if you are Incapacitated or die; you can end it at any time, no action required.
Ritual
A spell with the ritual tag can be cast 10 minutes longer without expending a spell slot.
