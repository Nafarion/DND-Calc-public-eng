Class features

Level 1 Spellcasting

You cast artificer spells through tools: thieves' tools, tinker's tools or other artisan's tools you're proficient with (so your spells always have a material component). Your spellcasting ability is Intelligence. Two cantrips (three from level 10, four from level 14); prepared spells per the class table, which you can change after a Long Rest.

Level 1 Tinker's Magic

You know the Mending cantrip. Holding tinker's tools, you can take the Magic action to create one simple item from a list nearby (rope, lamp, crowbar, torch, vial and so on); it vanishes after a Long Rest. Uses equal your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) per Long Rest.

Level 2 Replicate Magic Item

You know magic item plans (4 at level 2, 5 at level 6, 6 at level 10, 7 at level 14, 8 at level 18), and after a Long Rest, holding tinker's tools, you create items from them: 2 at once, 3 from level 6, 4 from level 10, 5 from level 14, 6 from level 18. A created weapon or wand serves as a spellcasting focus.

Level 3 Artificer Subclass

You choose a subclass: Alchemist, Artillerist, Battle Smith, Armorer or Cartographer.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 5 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen artificer subclass.

Level 6 Magic Item Tinker

As a bonus action you charge a created item that has charges by expending a spell slot (charges = slot level), or once per Long Rest you dismiss a created item and gain a 1st-level slot (common) or a 2nd-level slot (uncommon or rare); the slot vanishes after a Long Rest. Once per Long Rest you can take the Magic action to transform a created item into another from a plan you know.

Level 7 Flash of Genius

When you or a creature you can see within 30 feet fails an ability check or saving throw, you can use your reaction to add your Intelligence modifier to the roll. Uses equal your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) per Long Rest.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 9 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen artificer subclass.

Level 10 Magic Item Adept

You can be attuned to up to four magic items at once.

Level 11 Spell-Storing Item

After a Long Rest you store a 1st- to 3rd-level artificer spell with a casting time of an action and no consumed material components in a simple or martial weapon or a focus. The holder can take the Magic action to cast it using your ability, no more than once per turn — twice your Intelligence modifier times (minimum 2).

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 14 Advanced Artifice

You can be attuned to up to five magic items; a Short Rest restores one use of Flash of Genius.

Level 15 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen artificer subclass.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 18 Magic Item Master

You can be attuned to up to six magic items.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Epic Boons: Boon of Combat Prowess, Boon of Dimensional Travel, Boon of Energy Resistance, Boon of Fate, Boon of Fortitude, Boon of Irresistible Offense, Boon of Recovery, Boon of Skill, Boon of Speed, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Spell Recall, Boon of Truesight, Boon of Bloodshed, Boon of the Furious Storm, Boon of the Soul Drinker, Boon of the Bright Sun, Boon of Terror, Boon of Revelry, Boon of Poison Mastery, Boon of Fortune’s Favor, Boon of Exquisite Radiance, Boon of Fluid Forms, Boon of Desperate Resilience, Boon of Communication, Boon of Bountiful Health, Boon of Siberys, Boon of Erupting Spellpower, Boon of the Iron Mind, Boon of Magic School Mastery, Boon of Blazing Dawn, Boon of Looming Shadows, Boon of Misty Escape.

Level 20 Soul of Artifice

Cheat Death: when you drop to 0 hit points, you can dismiss your uncommon and rare created items — your hit points become 20 for each. A Short Rest restores all uses of Flash of Genius if you're attuned to at least one item.

Magic Item Plans

Plans for the Replicate Magic Item feature: the artificer knows four plans at level 2 and one more each at 6, 10, 14 and 18. After a Long Rest, holding Tinker's Tools, you create items from them (2 at a time, from level 6 — 3, from 10 — 4, from 14 — 5, from 18 — 6). When you gain a level, you can replace one plan.

Subclasses: Artificer Subclass

  • AlchemistEberron: Forge of the ArtificerA specialist in mixing reagents: brews elixirs, heals allies and corrodes foes.
  • ArtilleristEberron: Forge of the ArtificerRains elements, missiles and explosions on the battlefield: creates an eldritch cannon and empowers spells through an arcane firearm.
  • Battle SmithEberron: Forge of the ArtificerA defender and medic: fights with a magic weapon alongside their steel defender.
  • ArmorerEberron: Forge of the ArtificerTurns armor into a second skin: a dreadnaught, an infiltrator or a guardian with built-in weapons.
  • CartographerEberron: Forge of the ArtificerA navigator and scout: magical maps link the party, illuminate enemies and open portals.
  • ReanimatorRavenloft: The Horrors WithinAn artificer who has turned necromancy into a gruesome science: stitches servants together from scattered bodies and empowers the living with foul magic.
