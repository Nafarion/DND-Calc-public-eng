Rogue

D&D 2024 class. A master of the precise strike and the unseen maneuver. The rogue finds the weak spot — in an enemy's defense, a lock or someone else's pocket.

Class features

Level 1 Expertise

Choose two skills you're proficient in: your Proficiency Bonus is doubled for them.

Level 1 Sneak Attack

Once per turn you deal an extra 1d6 damage (growing by 1d6 every two levels) with a finesse or ranged weapon attack if you have Advantage or an ally is next to the target.

Level 1 Thieves' Cant

You know Thieves' Cant and one more language of your choice from the language tables.

Level 1 Weapon Mastery

You can use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons.

Level 2 Cunning Action

As a bonus action you can take the Dash, Disengage or Hide action.

Level 3 Rogue Subclass

You choose a roguish archetype.

Level 3 Steady Aim

As a bonus action you gain Advantage on your next attack this turn if you haven't moved.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 5 Cunning Strike

When you deal Sneak Attack, you can forgo some damage dice for an effect: Poison, Trip (knock prone) or Withdraw (move without provoking).

Level 5 Uncanny Dodge

As a reaction you halve the damage from an attack you can see.

Level 6 Expertise

Two more skills get double your Proficiency Bonus.

Level 7 Evasion

When you make a Dexterity saving throw, you take no damage on a success and half on a failure.

Level 7 Reliable Talent

In ability checks that use your Proficiency Bonus, a d20 roll below 10 counts as 10.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 9 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen archetype.

Level 10 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 11 Improved Cunning Strike

You can apply two Cunning Strike effects at once.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 13 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen archetype.

Level 14 Devious Strikes

Cunning Strike gains new effects: Daze (the target is limited in its actions), Knock Out (the target falls unconscious) and Obscure (the target is blinded).

Level 15 Slippery Mind

You gain proficiency in Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 17 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen archetype.

Level 18 Elusive

Attacks against you don't gain Advantage while you aren't incapacitated.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Epic Boons: Boon of Combat Prowess, Boon of Dimensional Travel, Boon of Energy Resistance, Boon of Fate, Boon of Fortitude, Boon of Irresistible Offense, Boon of Recovery, Boon of Skill, Boon of Speed, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Spell Recall, Boon of Truesight, Boon of Bloodshed, Boon of the Furious Storm, Boon of the Soul Drinker, Boon of the Bright Sun, Boon of Terror, Boon of Revelry, Boon of Poison Mastery, Boon of Fortune’s Favor, Boon of Exquisite Radiance, Boon of Fluid Forms, Boon of Desperate Resilience, Boon of Communication, Boon of Bountiful Health, Boon of Siberys, Boon of Erupting Spellpower, Boon of the Iron Mind, Boon of Magic School Mastery, Boon of Blazing Dawn, Boon of Looming Shadows, Boon of Misty Escape.

Level 20 Stroke of Luck

When you miss, you can turn the miss into a hit, or turn a failed check into a 20. Once per rest.

Subclasses: Rogue Subclass

  • Arcane TricksterA rogue who has added illusions and enchantment magic to their thieving kit.
  • AssassinA rogue who specializes in the prepared kill: disguise, poison and one decisive strike.
  • SoulknifeA psionic rogue who manifests blades of pure thought and speaks to allies without words.
  • ThiefThe classic rogue: fast hands, fast feet and a knack for being where no one expects.
  • Scion of the ThreeForgotten Realms: Heroes of FaerûnRogues empowered by the Dead Three of Baldur's Gate — Bhaal, Bane and Myrkul: the embodiment of malice and dread.
  • PhantomRavenloft: The Horrors WithinA rogue who has stepped beyond the line between life and death: empowers strikes with deathly power and steals knowledge from passing souls.
