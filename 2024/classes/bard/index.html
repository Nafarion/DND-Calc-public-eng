Class features

Level 1 Bardic Inspiration

As a bonus action, you give a creature within 60 ft. an inspiration die (d6, growing with your level). Within an hour the creature can add it to an ability check, attack roll or saving throw. Uses equal your Charisma modifier (minimum 1) and recharge on a Long Rest.

Level 1 Spellcasting

You cast bard spells using Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

Level 2 Expertise

Choose two skills you're proficient in: your Proficiency Bonus is doubled for them.

Level 2 Jack of All Trades

You add half your Proficiency Bonus (rounded down) to ability checks that don't already include it.

Level 3 Bard Subclass

You join a bard college.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 5 Font of Inspiration

You regain all uses of Bardic Inspiration on a Short or Long Rest.

Level 6 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen college.

Level 7 Countercharm

When you or a creature within 30 ft. fails a saving throw against an effect that imposes Charmed or Frightened, you can use your reaction to grant a reroll with Advantage.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 9 Expertise

Two more skills get double your Proficiency Bonus.

Level 10 Magical Secrets

When you prepare spells, you can choose them from the bard, druid, wizard and cleric lists.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 14 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen college.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 18 Superior Inspiration

When you roll Initiative, you regain uses of Bardic Inspiration up to two if you have fewer.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Epic Boons: Boon of Combat Prowess, Boon of Dimensional Travel, Boon of Energy Resistance, Boon of Fate, Boon of Fortitude, Boon of Irresistible Offense, Boon of Recovery, Boon of Skill, Boon of Speed, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Spell Recall, Boon of Truesight, Boon of Bloodshed, Boon of the Furious Storm, Boon of the Soul Drinker, Boon of the Bright Sun, Boon of Terror, Boon of Revelry, Boon of Poison Mastery, Boon of Fortune’s Favor, Boon of Exquisite Radiance, Boon of Fluid Forms, Boon of Desperate Resilience, Boon of Communication, Boon of Bountiful Health, Boon of Siberys, Boon of Erupting Spellpower, Boon of the Iron Mind, Boon of Magic School Mastery, Boon of Blazing Dawn, Boon of Looming Shadows, Boon of Misty Escape.

Level 20 Words of Creation

You always have Power Word Heal and Power Word Kill prepared and can target one more creature within 10 ft. of the first with each.

Subclasses: Bard Subclass

  • College of DanceA bard for whom movement is the spell. Dance makes them elusive and deadly.
  • College of GlamourA bard who trained in the Feywild. Their performance bends the will of the audience.
  • College of LoreA collector of secrets and others' magic. The Lore bard can undercut another's success with a well-chosen word.
  • College of ValorA skald who sings of heroes — and fights alongside them in the front rank.
  • College of the MoonForgotten Realms: Heroes of FaerûnBards of the druidic circles of the Moonshae Isles: they keep the history of the isles and bolster their allies with fey magic and the power of the moonwells.
  • College of SpiritsRavenloft: The Horrors WithinBards who summon spirits from ancient legends — willful ones, so it's not always the expected spirit that answers.
