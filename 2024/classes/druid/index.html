Class features

Level 1 Spellcasting

You cast druid spells using Wisdom as your spellcasting ability.

Level 1 Druidic

You know the secret language of druids and always have Speak with Animals prepared.

Level 1 Primal Order

Choose a path: Magician (an extra druid cantrip and your Wisdom modifier to Arcana and Nature checks) or Warden (training with medium armor and martial weapons).

Level 2 Wild Shape

As a bonus action you take the shape of a beast you've seen for hours equal to half your druid level. Uses — twice per rest, growing with your level.

Level 2 Wild Companion

You can expend a use of Wild Shape to cast Find Familiar without a spell slot.

Level 3 Druid Subclass

You join a druid circle.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 5 Wild Resurgence

You can trade a spell slot for a use of Wild Shape and vice versa, once per rest.

Level 6 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen circle.

Level 7 Elemental Fury

Choose Potent Spellcasting (add your Wisdom modifier to the damage of your druid cantrips) or Primal Strike (once per turn a weapon hit or a beast attack in Wild Shape deals an extra 1d8 thunder, fire, cold or lightning damage). The choice is on the Levels & Feats step.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 10 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen circle.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 14 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen circle.

Level 15 Improved Elemental Fury

Potent Spellcasting: the range of your druid cantrips (10 feet or more) increases by 300 feet. Primal Strike: extra damage 2d8.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 18 Beast Spells

In beast form you can cast spells with verbal and somatic components.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Epic Boons: Boon of Combat Prowess, Boon of Dimensional Travel, Boon of Energy Resistance, Boon of Fate, Boon of Fortitude, Boon of Irresistible Offense, Boon of Recovery, Boon of Skill, Boon of Speed, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Spell Recall, Boon of Truesight, Boon of Bloodshed, Boon of the Furious Storm, Boon of the Soul Drinker, Boon of the Bright Sun, Boon of Terror, Boon of Revelry, Boon of Poison Mastery, Boon of Fortune’s Favor, Boon of Exquisite Radiance, Boon of Fluid Forms, Boon of Desperate Resilience, Boon of Communication, Boon of Bountiful Health, Boon of Siberys, Boon of Erupting Spellpower, Boon of the Iron Mind, Boon of Magic School Mastery, Boon of Blazing Dawn, Boon of Looming Shadows, Boon of Misty Escape.

Level 20 Archdruid

When you roll Initiative with no uses of Wild Shape left, you regain one. You can convert uses of Wild Shape into a spell slot (2 slot levels per use, once per Long Rest). You age ten times more slowly.

Elemental Fury

At level 7, the Druid chooses one of two options; at level 15 it improves.

  • Potent Spellcasting
  • Primal Strike

Subclasses: Druid Subclass

  • Circle of the LandA keeper of nature lore whose magic reflects the character of their home land: arid, polar, temperate or tropical.
  • Circle of the MoonA shapeshifter druid for whom beast form is the main combat form.
  • Circle of the SeaA druid of tides and storms, around whom a squall rises of its own accord.
  • Circle of the StarsA druid who reads fate in the constellations and takes on their form.

Starting equipment
