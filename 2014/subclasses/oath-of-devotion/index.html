Oath of Devotion

A subclass of the Paladin class (sacred oath), D&D 5e (2014 rules). Chosen at level 3. The ideal of the knight in shining armor: honor, justice, protecting the weak.

Create a character: Paladin — Oath of Devotion

Subclass features

Level 3 Channel Divinity

Sacred Weapon: for 1 minute your weapon glows and adds your Charisma modifier to attack rolls. Turn the Unholy: fiends and undead within 30 feet flee from you (Wisdom saving throw).

Level 7 Aura of Devotion

You and allies within 10 feet (30 feet from 18th level) can't be charmed.

Level 15 Purity of Spirit

You're always under the effects of protection from evil and good.

Level 20 Holy Nimbus

As an action, you shed light for 1 minute: enemies in it take 10 radiant damage at the start of their turn, and you have advantage on saving throws against spells cast by fiends and undead.

Subclass spells

Always prepared; they don't count against your prepared spells.
