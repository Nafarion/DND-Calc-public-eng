Factions of the Forgotten Realms: the Harpers, the Zhentarim and others

The five factions many characters of the Forgotten Realms belong to: what they want, how they act and which skill the Faction Agent background gives an agent of each.

Harpers, Order of the Gauntlet, Emerald Enclave, Lords' Alliance · Zhentarim · Faction Agent

Each faction has its own motives, goals and philosophy. Some are more heroic than others, but in the face of a great threat all five unite. The factions matter especially for characters of the Forgotten Realms and for organized play.

Harpers

A secret network of spellcasters and spies who side with the innocent, the weak and the poor. Harpers pride themselves on being incorruptible and never refuse help to the oppressed. They prefer to work behind the scenes: overthrowing tyrants and nipping evil in the bud while remaining unseen.

Agents work alone, relying on their wits and wide networks of informants: knowledge is power. Harpers seek out powerful items to keep them from villains. An agent looks for help from other Harpers, sympathetic bards and innkeepers, rangers and priests of gods with similar ideals.

Agent skill: Investigation. Symbol: a silver harp and crescent moon.

Order of the Gauntlet

A relatively new society devoted to fighting evil wherever it lurks. The order’s goals are close to the Harpers’, but its methods differ: its holy warriors never hide in the shadows but ride out to the most dangerous dungeons and caves.

The order’s members — paladins, monks and clerics — remember that the shadow of evil lies in everyone and pray at length to cleanse themselves from within. A strict code of honor lets them strike only when an evil deed is already being done. An agent looks for help from the order’s friends among law enforcers and priests of the order’s patron gods.

Agent skill: Religion. Symbol: an upraised gauntlet gripping a sword by the blade.

Emerald Enclave

A far-ranging society that opposes threats to the natural world and helps others survive in the wild. Its branches are scattered across Faerûn and often act alone, so Enclave members are self-reliant and skilled at fighting and survival.

The Enclave isn’t against civilization but seeks to keep it in balance with the wild, restores the natural order and destroys all that is unnatural. An Enclave ranger might guide a caravan over a pass, a druid might help a village survive a harsh winter. An agent looks for help from foresters, hunters, rangers, barbarian tribes, druid circles and priests of nature gods.

Agent skill: Nature.

Lords' Alliance

An alliance of the rulers of Faerûn’s cities, above all in the North, who believe that evil can be held back only together. The rulers of Waterdeep, Neverwinter and other free cities lead it; each lord cares first about the fate of their own settlement.

Alliance agents — refined bards, zealous paladins, talented mages and seasoned warriors — are chosen above all for their loyalty. They remove threats to civilization by any means, but often chase glory and compete with the agents of other cities. An agent looks for help from governments, groups and leaders that belong to the Alliance or share its ideals.

Agent skill: History.

Zhentarim

The Black Network — an organization of seasoned mercenaries, cunning rogues and crafty warlocks that seeks to expand its influence and power across Faerûn. Its agents believe you get nowhere playing by the rules and want to make the rules themselves; they walk the edge of the law and don’t shy away from shady deals.

For the Zhentarim, wealth is power. When a merchant needs a caravan guard, a noble family needs bodyguards and a city needs trained soldiers, the Zhentarim provides the best fighters money can buy. The organization rewards ambition: results are everything. An agent looks for help from wizards, mercenaries, merchants and priests allied with the Zhentarim.

Agent skill: Deception. Symbol: a winged snake with a coin in its mouth.

The Faction Agent background

The agent served one of the factions of the North and the Sword Coast — secretly or openly, depending on the faction’s goals. The background grants Insight and one Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma skill suited to the faction: Harper agents are most often proficient in Investigation, the Order of the Gauntlet in Religion, the Emerald Enclave in Nature, the Lords’ Alliance in History, the Zhentarim in Deception. Through secret signs and passwords the agent recognizes their own: they will offer shelter and information, but won’t risk their lives or their cover.

Factions in the 2024 rules (Heroes of Faerûn)

Heroes of Faerûn has eight factions. Seven of them come with a background describing training with the faction and related feats: an origin feat at level 1 and a general feat you can take from level 4. You don’t have to join: the background simply explains where your character learned their trade.

The DM can award renown with a faction for completing its missions — the more renown, the more help the faction offers. Each faction has allies and rivals: their members are Friendly or Hostile toward each other by default.

Cult of the Dragon

Doomsayers who perform profane rituals to turn dragons into dracoliches and hasten the apocalypse. They are usually villains, but a character might join for power — or to bring it down from within.

Druids and survivalists who maintain the balance between nature and civilization and protect the land from unnatural monsters.

Harpers

Skalds, sleuths and spies who work in secret to make Faerûn a peaceful home for all: toppling tyrants and nipping evil in the bud.

Red Wizards

Mages based in the land of Thay with enclaves all over Faerûn, masters of arcane magic who quest for ancient artifacts. Most often they oppose the heroes.

Allies
Zhentarim
Rivals
Emerald Enclave, Harpers, Lords’ Alliance, Order of the Gauntlet, Purple Dragon Knights

Zhentarim

A network of mercenaries and traders who call themselves a family: greed and pride are virtues to be celebrated in the pursuit of ever-greater fortunes.

Background
Zhentarim Mercenary
Feats

Allies

Rivals
