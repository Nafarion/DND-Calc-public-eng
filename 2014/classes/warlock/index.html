Warlock

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. A spellcaster who has made a pact with a powerful entity. The warlock has few spell slots, but they're always of the highest level and return after a Short Rest.

Class features

Level 1 Otherworldly Patron

You've made a pact with a patron: it grants features and an expanded spell list.

Level 1 Pact Magic

A few spell slots of the same level (up to 5th), regained on a Short Rest. You know warlock spells; your spellcasting ability is Charisma.

Level 2 Eldritch Invocations

You know two invocations — permanent magical abilities (e.g. an empowered Eldritch Blast). You gain more as you level: up to eight by level 18.

Level 3 Pact Boon

Your patron grants a pact: the Chain (a special familiar), the Blade (a summoned weapon) or the Tome (extra cantrips).

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 20 Eldritch Master

After a minute of entreating your patron, you regain all your pact slots. Once per Long Rest.

Pact Boons

At 3rd level, your patron bestows one pact boon on the warlock. Some invocations depend on it. Pact of the Talisman is an option from Tasha's Cauldron.

Eldritch Invocations

Eldritch invocations are a warlock's permanent magical abilities. At 2nd level you know two, then more (up to eight at 18th level); when you gain a warlock level, you can replace one known invocation. Tasha's Cauldron invocations are available if its optional feature Eldritch Versatility (new invocations and Pact of the Talisman) is enabled.

Subclasses: Otherworldly Patron

  • The FiendA pact with a being of the Lower Planes: a demon, a devil or another fiend.
  • The Great Old OneA pact with an unfathomable entity from the depths of the Far Realm.
  • The ArchfeyA pact with a lord of the Feywild — capricious and powerful.
  • The FathomlessTasha's Cauldron of EverythingA patron from the depths of the sea — a kraken, an ancient elemental or a forgotten god.
  • The GenieTasha's Cauldron of EverythingThe patron is a mighty genie: a dao, djinni, efreeti or marid.
  • The CelestialXanathar's Guide to EverythingA patron from the Upper Planes: an empyrean, a solar or a ki-rin.
  • The HexbladeXanathar's Guide to EverythingA patron from the Shadowfell, tied to a sentient cursed weapon.
  • The UndyingSword Coast Adventurer's GuideA patron who has defeated death: Vecna, the lich-queen Vlaakith, Larloch, the Shadow King.
  • The UndeadVan Richten's Guide to RavenloftA pact with an immortal undead: Acererak, Kas the Bloody-Handed, Vlaakith, Azalin Rex.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 1 Expanded spells

New spells from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything are added to the warlock spell list — you can choose them on the Spells step.

Level 2 Eldritch Invocation Options and Pact of the Talisman

The Eldritch Mind, Far Scribe, Gift of the Protectors, Investment of the Chain Master, Protection of the Talisman, Rebuke of the Talisman, Bond of the Talisman and Undying Servitude invocations and the Pact of the Talisman (3) become available: the amulet adds 1d4 to a failed ability check.

Level 4 Eldritch Versatility

When you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace a cantrip, your Pact Boon (3) or a Mystic Arcanum spell (12).
