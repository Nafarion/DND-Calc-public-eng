Lunar Sorcery

A subclass of the Sorcerer class (sorcerous origin), D&D 5e (2014 rules). Chosen at level 1. A sorcerer into whom (or into whose ancestor) the magic of their world's moon has entered — on Krynn these are the three moons of the gods of magic. The magic draws on a chosen phase: full moon, new moon or crescent moon. Source: Dragonlance: Shadow of the Dragon Queen.

Create a character: Sorcerer — Lunar Sorcery

Subclass features

Level 1 Lunar Embodiment

You know the lunar spells of all three phases as you gain levels (1st: shield, ray of sickness, color spray; 3rd: lesser restoration, blindness/deafness, alter self; 5th: dispel magic, vampiric touch, phantom steed; 7th: death ward, confusion, hallucinatory terrain; 9th: Rary's telepathic bond, hold monster, mislead). They count as sorcerer spells and don't count against your spells known. After a long rest, choose a phase — full moon, new moon or crescent moon: you can cast the 1st-level spell of that phase once per long rest without a slot.

Level 1 Moon Fire

You know the sacred flame cantrip (it doesn't count against your spells known); it can strike one creature or two creatures within 5 feet of each other.

Level 6 Lunar Boons

Metamagic for spells of your phase's schools (full moon — abjuration and divination, new moon — enchantment and necromancy, crescent moon — illusion and transmutation) costs 1 sorcery point less (minimum 0). Uses equal your proficiency bonus, regained after a long rest.

Level 6 Waxing and Waning

As a bonus action, for 1 sorcery point change your phase. Now you can cast one 1st-level spell of each phase without a slot — if the current phase matches the spell's phase; each once per long rest.

Level 14 Lunar Empowerment

Your chosen phase also grants: full moon — as a bonus action, bright light in a 10-foot radius (and dim light for another 10 feet), in which you and chosen creatures have advantage on Investigation and Perception checks; new moon — advantage on Stealth checks and disadvantage on attacks against you in darkness; crescent moon — resistance to necrotic and radiant damage.

Level 18 Lunar Phenomenon

As a bonus action (or together with changing your phase) — the phase's power: full moon — chosen creatures within 30 feet make a Constitution saving throw, or are blinded until the end of their next turn, and one creature regains 3d8 hit points; new moon — Dexterity saving throw, or 3d10 necrotic damage and speed 0, and you're invisible until the end of your next turn or until you attack or cast a spell; crescent moon — teleport up to 60 feet with a willing creature within 5 feet, and both gain resistance to all damage until the start of your turn. Each option once per long rest or for 5 sorcery points.

Subclass spells

Always prepared; they don't count against your prepared spells.
