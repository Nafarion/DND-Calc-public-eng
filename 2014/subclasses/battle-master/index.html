Battle Master

A subclass of the Fighter class (martial archetype), D&D 5e (2014 rules). Chosen at level 3. A tactician and master of maneuvers who turns combat into an art.

Create a character: Fighter — Battle Master

Subclass features

Level 3 Combat Superiority

You know three maneuvers (two more at 7th, 10th and 15th level) and have four d8 superiority dice (five from 7th, six from 15th level), regained after a rest. The maneuver DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + Strength or Dexterity modifier.

Level 3 Student of War

Proficiency with one type of artisan's tools.

Level 7 Know Your Enemy

After observing a creature for a minute, learn whether it's superior to you in two characteristics of your choice.

Level 10 Improved Combat Superiority

Superiority dice become d10s (d12s from 18th level).

Level 15 Relentless

If you have no superiority dice when you roll initiative, you regain one.

Level 18 Improved Combat Superiority (d12)

Superiority dice become d12s.

Maneuvers

Battle Master maneuvers expend superiority dice. The Superior Technique fighting style gives one maneuver, the Martial Adept feat gives two. At 3rd level you know three maneuvers, and at 7th, 10th and 15th two more each; when you learn new ones, you can replace an old one. The save DC is 8 + proficiency bonus + Strength or Dexterity modifier. Tasha's Cauldron maneuvers are available if the optional feature Maneuver Options is enabled.
