Compatible: from 2014 books

The 2024 Player's Handbook is compatible with older books: a subclass, species or spell from a 2014 book that has no new version can be used with your DM's approval. Subclass features of levels 1 and 2 are gained at level 3, and species don't increase ability scores (backgrounds do). In the builder they are in a separate group; their descriptions are in the 2014 reference.
