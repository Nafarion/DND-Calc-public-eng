D&D 2024 mounts and vehicles

All equipment · Weapons · Armor and shields · Tools · Adventuring gear and packs · Mounts and vehicles · Services and lifestyle

A mount speeds up travel and carries cargo, a vehicle carries even more, and a ship carries the whole party with supplies. An animal pulling a vehicle can move up to five times its carrying capacity, including the vehicle’s weight.

You can ride faster only for a short time: about an hour at a gallop. Mounted combat is covered on the page “Combat”.
