Circle of Wildfire

A subclass of the Druid class (druid circle), D&D 5e (2014 rules). Chosen at level 2. Druids who know that fire not only destroys but also gives rise to new life. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything.

Create a character: Druid — Circle of Wildfire

Subclass features

Level 2 Circle Spells

Your circle spells are always prepared and don't count against your prepared spells.

Level 2 Summon Wildfire Spirit

As an action, spend a use of Wild Shape: a wildfire spirit appears nearby (hit points 5 × druid level + 5), other creatures nearby — Dexterity saving throw or 2d6 fire. The spirit acts after you, teleports allies and sets enemies ablaze.

Level 6 Enhanced Bond

While the spirit is nearby, once per turn your spell that deals fire damage deals +1d8 or heals +1d8; you can cast spells from the spirit's space.

Level 10 Cauterizing Flames

When a creature dies within 30 feet of you or the spirit, a spectral flame appears; as a reaction, you can use it to heal or burn a creature for 2d10 + Wisdom. Number of times — your proficiency bonus.

Level 14 Blazing Revival

When you drop to 0 hit points and the spirit is nearby, the spirit vanishes, and you rise with half your hit points. Once per long rest.

Subclass spells

Always prepared; they don't count against your prepared spells.
