Wizard (2014): Bladesinging

A wizard nobody can touch: Bladesong adds Intelligence to AC, Shield adds another +5, and Booming Blade makes you a fighter.

Why It’s Strong

  • Bladesong: + Intelligence to AC and to Concentration saves, +10 ft. Speed. With Mage Armor your AC is 18–21, and with Shield 23–26.
  • Extra Attack (6) with a cantrip in place of one attack; Song of Victory (14) — Intelligence added to melee weapon damage.
  • A high elf with Customizing Your Origin (Tasha’s Cauldron): Intelligence +2, Dexterity +1 and Booming Blade as the racial cantrip.
  • The whole wizard spellbook: Haste, Wall of Force, Tasha’s Otherworldly Guise, Simulacrum, Wish.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Bladesinging (from level 2).
  • Race: Elf — High Elf.
  • Customizing Your Origin (Tasha’s Cauldron): Intelligence +2, Dexterity +1 instead of the race’s book bonuses.
  • Background: Sage.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Intelligence 15 + 2 = 17, Dexterity 14 + 1 = 15, Constitution 13, Wisdom 12, Charisma 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Perception, Performance.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Additional Wizard Spells, Cantrip Formulas.
  • Equipment: Rapier, Dagger, Component Pouch, Scholar’s Pack, Spellbook.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Intelligence +1, Dexterity +1
  • Level 8: Intelligence +2
  • Level 12: War Caster feat
  • Level 16: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 19: Lucky feat

Level by Level

  • 1: High Elf with Customizing Your Origin (Tasha’s Cauldron): Intelligence +2, Dexterity +1, the Booming Blade cantrip, Perception. Shield, Mage Armor, Find Familiar.
  • 2: Bladesinging: light armor, rapier, Performance; Bladesong (Proficiency Bonus times per day).
  • 3: Cantrip Formulas (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 4: Intelligence 18, Dexterity 16.
  • 5: Haste, Counterspell, Fireball; Booming Blade +1d8.
  • 6: Extra Attack: rapier + Booming Blade.
  • 8: Intelligence 20.
  • 10: Song of Defense: spend a slot instead of taking damage.
  • 11: Tasha’s Otherworldly Guise: +2 AC, flight, weapon attacks with Intelligence (you already have two attacks).
  • 12: War Caster.
  • 14: Song of Victory: Intelligence added to melee weapon damage.
  • 16: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 17: Wish, Blade of Disaster.
  • 19: Lucky.

Spells

Cantrips: Green-Flame Blade, Mind Sliver, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation, Minor Illusion.

Prepared (by priority): Shield, Mage Armor, Find Familiar, Magic Missile, Detect Magic, Tasha’s Caustic Brew, Misty Step, Mirror Image, Tasha’s Mind Whip, Web, Haste, Counterspell, Fireball, Fly, Greater Invisibility, Polymorph, Banishment, Summon Aberration, Wall of Force, Simulacrum, Animate Objects, Bigby’s Hand, Tasha’s Otherworldly Guise, Globe of Invulnerability, Contingency, Forcecage, Teleport, Mind Blank, Feeblemind, Wish, Foresight, Blade of Disaster, Time Stop.

How to Play

  • Mage Armor after a rest, Bladesong as a Bonus Action at the start of combat, Shield as a Reaction.
  • Before level 6, use Booming Blade as your action in melee; from level 6 — a rapier attack + a cantrip.
  • Against dangerous foes the wizard is still a wizard: Haste on yourself, Wall of Force, Banishment.

Alternatives

  • Chronurgy Magic (Wildemount) — control over rolls and Intelligence to Initiative; Order of Scribes (Tasha’s Cauldron); War Magic (Xanathar’s).
  • Race: an elf with the Drow High Magic or Fey Teleportation feat (Xanathar’s).
  • School of Divination — Portent for control.
  • Order of Scribes (Tasha’s Cauldron) — changing damage types and rituals.
