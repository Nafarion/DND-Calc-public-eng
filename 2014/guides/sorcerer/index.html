Sorcerer (2014): Aberrant Mind

The main trouble of the 2014 sorcerer is too few spells known. Aberrant Mind adds eleven and casts them without components.

Why It’s Strong

  • Psionic Spells: Dissonant Whispers, Mind Sliver, Detect Thoughts, Hunger of Hadar, Evard’s Black Tentacles, Summon Aberration, Telekinesis — they don’t count against your spells known.
  • Psionic Sorcery: you can cast them with sorcery points without verbal and somatic components — unnoticed and safe from Counterspell.
  • Telepathic Speech, Resistance to Psychic damage and protection from being charmed.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron: Tasha’s Caustic Brew, Tasha’s Mind Whip, Tasha’s Otherworldly Guise, Magical Guidance.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Aberrant Mind (from level 1).
  • Race: Variant Human, War Caster feat.
  • Background: Sage.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Charisma 15 + 1 = 16, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 12, Intelligence 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Arcana, Deception, History, Perception, Persuasion.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Additional Sorcerer Spells, Metamagic Options, Sorcerous Versatility, Magical Guidance.
  • Equipment: Light Crossbow, Bolts (20), Arcane Focus, Dagger ×2, Dungeoneer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Charisma +2
  • Level 8: Charisma +2
  • Level 12: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 16: Constitution +2
  • Level 19: Lucky feat

Level by Level

  • 1: Variant Human: Charisma +1, Constitution +1, War Caster. Aberrant Mind: Arms of Hadar, Dissonant Whispers, Mind Sliver; Telepathic Speech.
  • 2: Font of Magic.
  • 3: Metamagic: Quickened Spell and Subtle Spell; Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts.
  • 4: Charisma 18; Sorcerous Versatility.
  • 5: Fireball, Counterspell; Hunger of Hadar, Sending; Magical Guidance (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 6: Psionic Sorcery, Psychic Defenses.
  • 7: Evard’s Black Tentacles, Summon Aberration.
  • 8: Charisma 20.
  • 9: Rary’s Telepathic Bond, Telekinesis.
  • 10: Metamagic: Twinned Spell.
  • 12: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 14: Revelation in Flesh.
  • 17: Metamagic: Empowered Spell.
  • 18: Warping Implosion.
  • 19: Lucky.
  • 20: Sorcerous Restoration.

Spells

Cantrips: Fire Bolt, Prestidigitation, Mage Hand, Minor Illusion, Message, Light.

Known (by priority): Shield, Tasha’s Caustic Brew, Misty Step, Tasha’s Mind Whip, Fireball, Counterspell, Haste, Polymorph, Banishment, Wall of Stone, Hold Monster, Chain Lightning, Tasha’s Otherworldly Guise, Teleport, Plane Shift, Dominate Monster, Wish, Meteor Swarm.

How to Play

  • Use Psionic Sorcery for spells nobody should see you cast: the enemy won’t know who is casting and can’t answer with Counterspell.
  • Mind Sliver with your action on the main target, then a Quickened Fireball — it saves with −1d4.
  • Tasha’s additional spells — Caustic Brew and Mind Whip — are stronger than their book counterparts.

Alternatives

  • Divine Soul (Xanathar’s) — cleric spells; Clockwork Soul (Tasha’s Cauldron); Lunar Sorcery (Dragonlance).
  • Xanathar’s racial feats: Elven Accuracy for an elf sorcerer with attack spells.
  • Clockwork Soul (Tasha’s Cauldron) — a defensive option with Wall of Force.
  • Draconic Bloodline — AC 13 + Dexterity and extra Hit Points.
