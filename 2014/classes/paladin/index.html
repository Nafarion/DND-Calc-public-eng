Paladin

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. A holy warrior bound by an oath. The paladin heals by laying on hands and brings divine smiting down on foes with their weapon.

Class features

Level 1 Divine Sense

As an action you sense celestials, fiends and undead within 60 ft. until the end of your next turn. Uses equal 1 + your Charisma modifier per Long Rest.

Level 1 Lay on Hands

Your pool of healing power equals five times your paladin level. As an action, touch a creature to restore hit points from the pool, or spend 5 points to cure a disease or poison.

Level 2 Fighting Style

You choose a Fighting Style.

Fighting styles: Archery, Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Protection, Two-Weapon Fighting, Blind Fighting, Interception, Superior Technique, Thrown Weapon Fighting, Unarmed Fighting, Blessed Warrior, Druidic Warrior.

Level 2 Spellcasting

Each day you prepare paladin spells: half your paladin level + your Charisma modifier (minimum one).

Level 2 Divine Smite

When you hit with a melee weapon attack, expend a spell slot: +2d8 radiant damage for a 1st-level slot and +1d8 for each level above (maximum 5d8). Another +1d8 against undead and fiends.

Level 3 Divine Health

Immunity to disease.

Level 3 Sacred Oath

You swear an oath: it grants spells, features and Channel Divinity options.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 5 Extra Attack

You attack twice when you take the Attack action.

Level 6 Aura of Protection

You and allies within 10 ft. add your Charisma modifier (minimum +1) to saving throws while you're conscious. 30 ft. from level 18.

Level 7 Sacred Oath feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Sacred Oath).

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 10 Aura of Courage

You and allies within 10 ft. can't be frightened while you're conscious. 30 ft. from level 18.

Level 11 Improved Divine Smite

Every hit with a melee weapon deals an extra 1d8 radiant damage.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 14 Cleansing Touch

As an action, touch yourself or a willing creature to end one spell on it. Uses equal your Charisma modifier per Long Rest.

Level 15 Sacred Oath feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Sacred Oath).

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 18 Aura improvements

The radius of Aura of Protection, Aura of Courage and your oath aura increases to 30 ft.

Level 19 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 20 Sacred Oath feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Sacred Oath).

Subclasses: Sacred Oath

  • Oath of DevotionThe ideal of the knight in shining armor: honor, justice, protecting the weak.
  • Oath of VengeanceA vow to punish those who have committed grievous evil, at any cost.
  • Oath of the AncientsAn oath as old as the elves: to preserve the light, joy and life of the world against darkness.
  • Oath of GloryTasha's Cauldron of EverythingPaladins who believe that heroic deeds lead to immortal glory.
  • Oath of the WatchersTasha's Cauldron of EverythingPaladins who guard the mortal world against incursions from other planes.
  • Oath of ConquestXanathar's Guide to EverythingPaladins who bring order through strength and fear.
  • Oath of RedemptionXanathar's Guide to EverythingPaladins who believe anyone can be saved and that violence is the last resort.
  • Oath of the CrownSword Coast Adventurer's GuideAn oath to civilization, law and the sovereign: paladins are the guardians of order on the walls.
  • OathbreakerDungeon Master's GuideA paladin who broke a sacred oath for dark desires or to serve evil; they must be evil and at least 3rd level. If the oathbreaker atones, the DM can restore their oath. A Dungeon Master's Guide subclass for evil characters: it isn't in the set available by default — take it only with your DM's permission.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 1 Expanded spells

New spells from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything are added to the paladin spell list — you can choose them on the Spells step.

Level 2 Spellcasting Focus

You can use a holy symbol as a spellcasting focus for your paladin spells.

Level 2 Fighting Style Options

The Blessed Warrior, Blind Fighting and Interception styles become available.

Level 3 Channel Divinity: Harness Divine Power

As a bonus action, expend a use of Channel Divinity to regain a spell slot of a level no higher than half your proficiency bonus (rounded up). Once per long rest (twice from 7th level, three times from 15th).

Level 4 Martial Versatility

When you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace a fighting style with another available one (a fighter can also replace a Battle Master maneuver).

Starting equipment

  • Longsword, Shield, Javelin (5), Priest's Pack, Chain Mail, Holy Symbol
  • Starting wealth: about 125 GP
