Archdevils and demon lords: fiend patrons of warlocks (2024)

Who a warlock with the Fiend Patron can make a pact with in the 2024 rules: archdevils of the Nine Hells, demon lords of the Abyss and other mighty fiends. What changed between the editions: Fiend patrons: 2014 vs 2024.

Who can be a patron · Devils and demons · Archdevils · Demon lords · Other fiends

Who can be a patron

A fiend patron is a being of the Lower Planes whose aims are evil: it craves the corruption or destruction of all things, ultimately including the warlock. The warlock’s path is defined by how much they resist those aims.

Examples from the 2024 Player’s Handbook: demon lords (Demogorgon, Orcus), archdevils (Asmodeus), and especially mighty pit fiends, balors, yugoloths or night hags.

A patron is not a god, though nearly as powerful as the gods. More on the difference between a cleric and a warlock: Religion in D&D.

Devils and demons

Devils are the lawful evil inhabitants of the Nine Hells: they live by a strict hierarchy and honor the letter of a contract. Demons are the chaotic evil spawn of the Abyss, driven by rage and a thirst for destruction. The two races of fiends wage the endless Blood War. A pact with an archdevil is a deal with clearly written terms; a pact with a demon lord is far less predictable.

Other fiends

A patron need not be a lord, just an especially mighty fiend: a pit fiend (a devil), a balor (a demon), a yugoloth or a night hag. Yugoloths are neutral evil mercenaries who serve whoever pays more, including both sides of the Blood War.
