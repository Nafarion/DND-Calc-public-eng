Warlock

D&D 2024 class. A spellcaster who gained power through a pact with a powerful entity. The warlock casts fewer spells but regains them after a Short Rest.

Class features

Level 1 Eldritch Invocations

You learn invocations that grant permanent abilities and extra spells. Their number grows with your level, and you can change them when you level up.

Level 1 Pact Magic

You cast warlock spells with Charisma. Your slots are always of the same (highest available) level and recharge on a Short Rest.

Level 2 Magical Cunning

With a 1-minute rite you regain expended Pact Magic slots — no more than half your maximum (rounded up). Once per Long Rest.

Level 3 Warlock Subclass

You choose the patron who granted you power.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 6 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen patron.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Level 9 Contact Patron

You can contact your patron and receive an answer to a question.

Level 10 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen patron.

Level 11 Mystic Arcanum (level 6)

You choose a 6th-level spell from the warlock list and can cast it once per Long Rest.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Level 20 Eldritch Master

When you use Magical Cunning, you regain all expended Pact Magic slots.

Subclasses: Warlock Subclass

  • Archfey PatronA pact with a lord of the Feywild: deceptive gifts, steps through the mist and charms that are hard to resist.
  • Celestial PatronA pact with a being of the Upper Planes. A healer warlock whose light burns the undead.
  • Fiend PatronA pact with a demon or devil: fire, dark luck and a bargain best forgotten.
  • Great Old One PatronA pact with an entity that may not even have noticed it was made. Magic of the mind and madness.
  • Vestige PatronArcana UnleashedThe patron is a fading forgotten god who longs to regain its former might and shares the remains of its power with you.
  • Undead PatronRavenloft: The Horrors WithinThe patron is a lich, a vampire or another mighty undead who has gone beyond the cycle of life and death.
