Ranger: Fey Wanderer with Two Blades

Steady damage every turn: Hunter’s Mark, Dreadful Strikes and two-weapon fighting with Nick.

Why It’s Strong

  • The 2024 ranger casts Hunter’s Mark for free several times a day, and from level 13 damage can’t break its Concentration.
  • The Fey Wanderer’s Dreadful Strikes add 1d4 Psychic damage once per turn — at no cost.
  • The scimitar’s Nick mastery moves the off-hand attack into the Attack action, freeing your Bonus Action for Hunter’s Mark.
  • The Fey Wanderer adds Wisdom to Charisma checks — the ranger becomes the party’s face too.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Fey Wanderer (from level 3).
  • Species: Human, Alert feat.
  • Background: Guide — Magic Initiate (Druid) feat, bonuses: Dexterity +2, Wisdom +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Dexterity 15 + 2 = 17, Wisdom 14 + 1 = 15, Constitution 13, Strength 12, Charisma 10, Intelligence 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Athletics, Insight, Nature, Perception (Expertise), Persuasion, Stealth (Expertise), Survival (Expertise).
  • Weapon Mastery: Scimitar, Shortsword (as you gain them).
  • Fighting Style: Two-Weapon Fighting.
  • Equipment: Studded Leather Armor, Scimitar, Shortsword, Longbow, Arrows (20), Quiver, Explorer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Dual Wielder feat (+1 Dexterity)
  • Level 8: Dexterity +2
  • Level 12: Wisdom +2
  • Level 16: Lucky feat
  • Level 19: Boon of Combat Prowess feat (+1 Dexterity)

Level by Level

  • 1: Human: Alert and a skill. Guide background: Magic Initiate (Druid) — Guidance, Starry Wisp, Healing Word. Hunter’s Mark always prepared. Weapon Mastery: scimitar (Nick), shortsword (Vex).
  • 2: Deft Explorer (Expertise in Perception), Two-Weapon Fighting style.
  • 3: Fey Wanderer: Dreadful Strikes, Otherworldly Glamour (Persuasion). Charm Person always prepared.
  • 4: Dual Wielder (+1 Dexterity).
  • 5: Extra Attack. Misty Step always prepared.
  • 6: Roving: +10 ft. Speed, climbing and swimming.
  • 7: Beguiling Twist.
  • 8: Dexterity 20.
  • 9: Two more Expertises (Stealth, Survival).
  • 11: Fey Reinforcements; Dreadful Strikes 1d6.
  • 12: Wisdom +2.
  • 13: Relentless Hunter — damage can’t break Hunter’s Mark.
  • 15: Misty Wanderer.
  • 16: Lucky.
  • 17: Precise Hunter: Advantage on attacks against your Hunter’s Mark target.
  • 19: Boon of Combat Prowess (Dexterity 21).
  • 20: Foe Slayer: the Hunter’s Mark die becomes a d10.

Spells

Prepared (by priority): Goodberry, Cure Wounds, Ensnaring Strike, Alarm, Hail of Thorns, Pass without Trace, Spike Growth, Summon Beast, Lesser Restoration, Conjure Animals, Revivify, Lightning Arrow, Conjure Barrage, Summon Elemental, Freedom of Movement, Conjure Woodland Beings, Steel Wind Strike, Greater Restoration, Swift Quiver.

How to Play

  • Attack action — two scimitar strikes and, thanks to Nick, a shortsword strike. Bonus Action — Hunter’s Mark, and once the mark is up, one more attack from Dual Wielder.
  • At long range, use the longbow; Hunter’s Mark works with it too.
  • Pass without Trace gives the party +10 to Stealth — a great way to start an ambush.

Alternatives

  • Winter Walker (Heroes of Faerûn); Hollow Warden (Ravenloft); compatible option — Drakewarden (Fizban’s).
  • The Sharp Eye origin feat (Ravenloft) — for an archer.
  • Gloom Stalker — ambushes and Wisdom added to Initiative.
  • An archer with Sharpshooter instead of two blades — less risk in melee.
