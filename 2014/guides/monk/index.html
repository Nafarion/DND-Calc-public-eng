Monk (2014): Way of Mercy

Healer and killer in one: Flurry of Blows heals allies and deals Necrotic damage, and Tasha’s optional features fix ki.

Why It’s Strong

  • Hand of Healing and Hand of Harm: healing and damage of a Martial Arts die + Wisdom; from level 11, Flurry of Blows combines them without spending ki.
  • Physician’s Touch removes a disease or one condition: Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned or Stunned.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron: Focused Aim (+2 to an attack per ki point after a miss), Ki-Fueled Attack, Quickened Healing, Dedicated Weapon.
  • Hand of Ultimate Mercy (17) — a resurrection once a day.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Way of Mercy (from level 3).
  • Race: Elf — Wood Elf.
  • Background: Outlander.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Dexterity 15 + 2 = 17, Wisdom 14 + 1 = 15, Constitution 13, Strength 12, Charisma 10, Intelligence 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Acrobatics, Athletics, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Stealth, Survival.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Dedicated Weapon, Ki-Fueled Attack, Quickened Healing, Focused Aim.
  • Equipment: Spear, Dart ×10, Explorer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Dexterity +1, Wisdom +1
  • Level 8: Dexterity +2
  • Level 12: Wisdom +2
  • Level 16: Mobile feat
  • Level 19: Wisdom +2

Level by Level

  • 1: Wood Elf: Dexterity +2, Wisdom +1, Speed 35 ft. Martial Arts, Unarmored Defense.
  • 2: Ki, Unarmored Movement; Dedicated Weapon (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 3: Way of Mercy: Insight, Medicine, Herbalism Kit, Hand of Healing and Hand of Harm. Ki-Fueled Attack.
  • 4: Dexterity +1, Wisdom +1; Quickened Healing.
  • 5: Extra Attack, Stunning Strike; Focused Aim.
  • 6: Physician’s Touch, Ki-Empowered Strikes.
  • 8: Dexterity 20.
  • 11: Flurry of Healing and Harm.
  • 12: Wisdom +2.
  • 14: Diamond Soul.
  • 16: Mobile.
  • 17: Hand of Ultimate Mercy.
  • 19: Wisdom 20.

How to Play

  • Flurry of Blows: replace one strike with Hand of Healing — the ally gets up, and you keep hitting.
  • Hand of Harm and Stunning Strike — one target per turn.
  • Use Focused Aim on a Stunning Strike that mustn’t miss.

Alternatives

  • Way of the Kensei (Xanathar’s) — a weapon monk; Way of the Ascendant Dragon (Fizban’s) — breath and flight.
  • Race: tabaxi or githzerai (Monsters of the Multiverse).
  • Way of the Open Hand — the best Flurry control.
  • Way of the Astral Self (Tasha’s Cauldron) — for a monk focused on Wisdom.
