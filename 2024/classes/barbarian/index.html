Barbarian

D&D 2024 class. A warrior who draws strength from rage. The barbarian holds the line where others fall and hits harder the closer they are to the edge.

Class features

Level 1 Rage

As a bonus action you enter a rage for 10 minutes: Advantage on Strength checks and saving throws, a bonus to melee weapon damage (+2, rising to +3 at level 9 and +4 at level 16) and resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage. Your number of rages per Long Rest grows with your level.

Level 1 Unarmored Defense

While you aren't wearing armor, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier. You can use a shield.

Level 1 Weapon Mastery

You can use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons you're proficient with (three from level 4, four from level 10). You can change the list after a Long Rest.

Level 2 Danger Sense

You have Advantage on Dexterity saving throws unless you have the Incapacitated condition.

Level 2 Reckless Attack

When you make your first attack on your turn, you can attack recklessly: you gain Advantage on Strength-based attacks this turn, but attacks against you also have Advantage.

Level 3 Barbarian Subclass

You choose a primal path that shapes how your rage develops.

Level 3 Primal Knowledge

You gain proficiency in another skill from the barbarian list. While raging, you can replace Dexterity, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma checks with Strength checks (for certain skills).

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 5 Extra Attack

You attack twice instead of once when you take the Attack action.

Level 5 Fast Movement

Your speed increases by 10 feet while you aren't wearing heavy armor.

Level 6 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen primal path.

Level 7 Feral Instinct

You have Advantage on Initiative rolls.

Level 7 Instinctive Pounce

When you enter a rage, you can move up to half your speed.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 9 Brutal Strike

When you attack recklessly, you can forgo Advantage to deal an extra 1d10 damage and apply an effect: push the target 15 ft. or slow it.

Level 10 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen primal path.

Level 11 Relentless Rage

If you drop to 0 hit points while raging but aren't killed outright, you can make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw to instead drop to hit points equal to twice your barbarian level.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 13 Improved Brutal Strike

Brutal Strike gains additional effects to choose from.

Level 14 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen primal path.

Level 15 Persistent Rage

When you roll Initiative, you regain all uses of Rage (once per Long Rest). Your rage lasts 10 minutes and doesn't need to be extended.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 17 Improved Brutal Strike

Brutal Strike deals 2d10 extra damage and gains new effects.

Level 18 Indomitable Might

If the result of a Strength check is less than your Strength score, you can use the score instead.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Epic Boons: Boon of Combat Prowess, Boon of Dimensional Travel, Boon of Energy Resistance, Boon of Fate, Boon of Fortitude, Boon of Irresistible Offense, Boon of Recovery, Boon of Skill, Boon of Speed, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Spell Recall, Boon of Truesight, Boon of Bloodshed, Boon of the Furious Storm, Boon of the Soul Drinker, Boon of the Bright Sun, Boon of Terror, Boon of Revelry, Boon of Poison Mastery, Boon of Fortune’s Favor, Boon of Exquisite Radiance, Boon of Fluid Forms, Boon of Desperate Resilience, Boon of Communication, Boon of Bountiful Health, Boon of Siberys, Boon of Erupting Spellpower, Boon of the Iron Mind, Boon of Magic School Mastery, Boon of Blazing Dawn, Boon of Looming Shadows, Boon of Misty Escape.

Level 20 Primal Champion

Your Strength and Constitution increase by 4, and their maximum becomes 25.

Subclasses: Barbarian Subclass

  • Path of the BerserkerRage as pure destruction. The Berserker turns combat into a storm and doesn't stop while still standing.
  • Path of the Wild HeartA barbarian whose rage calls upon animal spirits. Each fight summons the power of a totem animal.
  • Path of the World TreeA barbarian whose rage draws power from Yggdrasil — the tree that binds the planes of existence.
  • Path of the ZealotRage consecrated by a deity. The Zealot fights like one possessed and doesn't let death take them easily.
