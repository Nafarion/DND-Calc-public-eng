Undead Patron

A subclass of the Warlock class (warlock subclass), D&D 2024. Chosen at level 3. The patron is a lich, a vampire or another mighty undead who has gone beyond the cycle of life and death. Source: Ravenloft: The Horrors Within.

Create a character: Warlock — Undead Patron

Subclass features

Level 3 Undead Spells

Always prepared: Phantasmal Force, Blindness/Deafness, Ray of Sickness, Bane (3), Summon Undead, Speak with Dead (5), Phantasmal Killer, Greater Invisibility (7), Cloudkill, Antilife Shell (9).

Level 3 Form of Dread

As a Bonus Action you transform for 1 minute (until Incapacitated or at will): 1d10 + warlock level Temporary Hit Points; Immunity to the Frightened condition (an existing one ends); once per turn, when you hit with an attack, you force the target to make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC, or it's Frightened until the end of your next turn. Number of uses — your Charisma modifier (minimum 1), regained after a Long Rest.

Level 6 Grave Touched

Arcane Necrosis: the Necrotic damage of your attacks, spells and Warlock features ignores Resistance; once per turn you can change a spell's damage type to Necrotic. Dreaded Necrosis: once per turn while using your Form of Dread, when you hit with an attack that deals Necrotic damage, you roll one additional damage die. Undead Endurance: no Exhaustion from suffocation, hunger or thirst; you don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep.

Level 10 Necrotic Husk

Resistance to Necrotic damage, Immunity while using your Form of Dread. Unholy Resuscitation: if your Hit Points drop to 0 but you aren't killed, chosen creatures in a 30-foot Emanation make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC — 2d10 + Charisma modifier Necrotic damage, half on a success; your Hit Points become twice your warlock level, and you gain 1 Exhaustion level. Once per Short or Long Rest.

Level 14 Superior Dread

While using your Form of Dread: Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage; a Fly Speed equal to your Speed, hover and moving through creatures and objects as Difficult Terrain (1d10 Force damage if you end your turn inside); Conjuration and Necromancy Warlock spells — without Verbal, Somatic and Material components, except costly or consumed ones.

Subclass spells

Always prepared; they don't count against your prepared spells.
