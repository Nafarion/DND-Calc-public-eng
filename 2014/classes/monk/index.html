Monk

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. A martial artist who has turned their body into a weapon. The monk channels ki and moves faster than the eye can follow.

Class features

Level 1 Unarmored Defense

Without armor or a shield, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Wisdom modifier.

Level 1 Martial Arts

Unarmed strikes and monk weapons use Dexterity, with a d4 damage die (d6 from level 5, d8 from level 11, d10 from level 17). After attacking with such a weapon, you can make an unarmed strike as a bonus action.

Level 2 Ki

Ki points equal your monk level and recharge on a Short Rest. Flurry of Blows — two unarmed strikes as a bonus action; Patient Defense — Dodge as a bonus action; Step of the Wind — Disengage or Dash as a bonus action and double jump distance. Save DC is 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Wisdom modifier.

Level 2 Unarmored Movement

Speed +10 ft. without armor or a shield (up to +30 ft. by level 18). From level 9 you can run along vertical surfaces and water.

Level 3 Monastic Tradition

You choose a monastic tradition.

Level 3 Deflect Missiles

As a reaction, reduce the damage from a ranged weapon attack by 1d10 + your Dexterity modifier + your monk level. If the damage drops to 0, you can catch the missile and throw it back for 1 ki.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 4 Slow Fall

As a reaction, reduce falling damage by five times your monk level.

Level 5 Extra Attack

You attack twice when you take the Attack action.

Level 5 Stunning Strike

When you hit with a melee weapon attack, spend 1 ki: the target makes a Constitution saving throw or is stunned until the end of your next turn.

Level 6 Ki-Empowered Strikes

Your unarmed strikes count as magical for overcoming resistance.

Level 6 Monastic Tradition feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Monastic Tradition).

Level 7 Evasion

When you make a Dexterity saving throw for half damage, you take no damage on a success and half on a failure.

Level 7 Stillness of Mind

As an action you end the charmed or frightened condition on yourself.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 9 Unarmored Movement improvement

Without armor or a shield, you can move along vertical surfaces and across liquids on your turn without falling.

Level 10 Purity of Body

Immunity to disease and poison.

Level 11 Monastic Tradition feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Monastic Tradition).

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 13 Tongue of the Sun and Moon

You understand all languages, and any creature that knows a language understands you.

Level 14 Diamond Soul

Proficiency in all saving throws; you can reroll a failed saving throw for 1 ki.

Level 15 Timeless Body

You don't suffer the frailty of old age and don't need food or water.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 17 Monastic Tradition feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Monastic Tradition).

Level 18 Empty Body

For 4 ki you become invisible for 1 minute and gain resistance to all damage except force; for 8 ki you cast Astral Projection on yourself without components.

Level 19 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 20 Perfect Self

If you have no ki points when you roll Initiative, you regain 4.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 2 Dedicated Weapon

After a rest, choose a simple or martial weapon you're proficient with that lacks the heavy and special properties: it counts as a monk weapon for you.

Level 3 Ki-Fueled Attack

If you spend ki as part of your action on your turn, you can make one unarmed strike or monk weapon attack as a bonus action.

Level 4 Quickened Healing

As an action, spend 2 ki points to regain hit points equal to a roll of your Martial Arts die + your proficiency bonus.

Level 5 Focused Aim

When you miss with an attack, you can spend 1 to 3 ki points to gain +2 to the attack roll for each.
