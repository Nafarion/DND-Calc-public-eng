D&D 2024 between adventures: crafting, work, training

Between expeditions characters rest, spend their earnings, make things and prepare for new adventures. Sometimes the DM simply says that time has passed and asks what your character has been doing.

Lifestyle costs are on the page “Services and lifestyle”.

Crafting equipment

To make a nonmagical item you need a tool, raw materials and time.

Tools
You need the tool that makes the item and proficiency with it (helpers too). What each tool can make is listed on the page “Tools”.
Raw materials
Worth half the item’s cost (rounded down): Plate Armor costing 1,500 GP needs 750 GP of materials.
Time
The cost in GP divided by 10 gives the number of 8-hour days (round up). A Heavy Crossbow costing 50 GP takes 5 days. The days needn’t be consecutive. A helper halves the time; more helpers only if the DM allows.

Potions of Healing

A character proficient with the Herbalism Kit can brew a Potion of Healing in 1 day (8 hours) with 25 GP of raw materials.

Spell Scrolls

A spellcaster can transfer a spell to a scroll. You need proficiency in Arcana or with Calligrapher’s Supplies, the spell prepared on each day of work and its Material components (consumed ones are used up when you finish). The scroll uses your spell save DC and attack bonus; a cantrip on a scroll works as if cast at your level.

Activities from the 2014 rules (with your DM’s approval)

The 2024 Player’s Handbook has no separate rules for other activities between adventures. The 2024 rules are compatible with the older books, so your DM can allow the activities from the 2014 Player’s Handbook:

Practicing a profession
Pays for a modest lifestyle; a member of an organization lives comfortably, and a performer proficient in Performance lives wealthy.
Recuperating
After 3 days of rest, a DC 15 Constitution saving throw to end an effect that prevents regaining Hit Points or to gain Advantage against a disease or poison.
Researching
Searching libraries for information or taverns for rumors: 1 GP per day; the DM sets the time and checks.
Training
With an instructor, 250 days at 1 GP per day — a new language or a tool proficiency.

Details are on the page “Between adventures” (2014 rules). Count crafting by the 2024 rules (above): they differ — cost / 10 days instead of 5 gp of work per day. Rules for Bastions are in the 2024 Dungeon Master’s Guide.

Source: the 2024 Player’s Handbook (free rules on D&D Beyond). A short summary in our own words — see the book for exact wording.
