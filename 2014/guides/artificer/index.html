Artificer (2014): Armorer Guardian in Heavy Armor

An Intelligence-based tank: heavy armor from the subclass, thunder gauntlets that make enemies hit you, and infusions for AC.

Why It’s Strong

  • The Armorer (Tasha’s Cauldron) gains heavy armor; the Guardian model grants Thunder Gauntlets (Intelligence-based attacks, Disadvantage for the enemy against others) and Temporary Hit Points as a Bonus Action.
  • The Enhanced Defense and Enhanced Weapon infusions as early as level 2: AC 19–20 by level 3.
  • The artificer is proficient in Constitution and Intelligence saving throws — Concentration holds even without Resilient.
  • A variant human takes War Caster at level 1: Advantage on Concentration and casting with your hands full.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Armorer (from level 3).
  • Race: Variant Human, War Caster feat.
  • Background: Sage.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Intelligence 15 + 1 = 16, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 12, Strength 10, Charisma 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Arcana, Athletics, History, Investigation, Perception.
  • Equipment: Scale Mail, Light Crossbow, Bolts (20), Thieves’ Tools, Tinker’s Tools, Dungeoneer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Intelligence +2
  • Level 8: Intelligence +2
  • Level 12: Lucky feat
  • Level 16: Alert feat
  • Level 19: Constitution +2

Level by Level

  • 1: Variant Human: Intelligence +1, Constitution +1, Athletics and War Caster. Magical Tinkering; artificer spells: Absorb Elements, Cure Wounds.
  • 2: Infusions: Enhanced Defense on your armor, Enhanced Weapon, Homunculus Servant, Mind Sharpener.
  • 3: Armorer: heavy armor, Smith’s Tools, Arcane Armor; the Guardian model — Thunder Gauntlets and Defensive Field. Magic Missile and Thunderwave always prepared.
  • 4: Intelligence 18.
  • 5: Extra Attack. Mirror Image and Shatter always prepared.
  • 6: Resistant Armor and Spell-Refueling Ring infusions; Tool Expertise.
  • 7: Flash of Genius: add Intelligence to an ally’s check or saving throw.
  • 8: Intelligence 20.
  • 9: Armor Modifications: four infusions in your armor. 3rd-level slots: Haste, Fly.
  • 10: Helm of Awareness and Armor of Magical Strength infusions.
  • 11: Spell-Storing Item: Haste without a slot several times a day.
  • 12: Lucky.
  • 14: Arcane Propulsion Armor infusion; Magic Item Savant.
  • 15: Perfected Armor: the Guardian pulls enemies in and strikes with thunder.
  • 16: Alert.
  • 19: Constitution +2.
  • 20: Soul of Artifice.

Spells

Cantrips: Booming Blade, Guidance, Mending, Fire Bolt.

Prepared (by priority): Absorb Elements, Cure Wounds, Faerie Fire, Sanctuary, Catapult, Grease, Detect Magic, Aid, Web, Enhance Ability, Magic Weapon, Lesser Restoration, Invisibility, Haste, Fly, Dispel Magic, Revivify, Glyph of Warding, Blink, Summon Construct, Freedom of Movement, Stoneskin, Otiluke’s Resilient Sphere, Arcane Eye, Greater Restoration, Animate Objects, Bigby’s Hand, Wall of Stone, Creation, Skill Empowerment.

How to Play

  • Thunder Gauntlets: an enemy you hit attacks others with Disadvantage — stand next to the main threat.
  • Defensive Field as a Bonus Action grants Temporary Hit Points; your AC is already high, so save your Reaction for Absorb Elements.
  • Concentrate on Haste (on yourself or the fighter) or Web.

Alternatives

  • Battle Smith (Tasha’s Cauldron) — a steel defender and Intelligence-based weapon attacks; Artillerist — ranged spell damage.
  • Race: hobgoblin or tortle (Monsters of the Multiverse); mountain dwarf — Constitution and toughness.
