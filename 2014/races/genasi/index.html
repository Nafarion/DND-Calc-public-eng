Genasi

D&D 5e (2014 rules) race. Descendants of the genies of the elemental planes: each genasi carries the power of air, earth, fire or water. Source: Mordenkainen Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse.

Create a character: Genasi

Basics

Size
Small or Medium
Speed
30 ft.
Creature type
Humanoid
Ability Score Increase
+2 and +1 or +1/+1/+1 to abilities of your choice

Traits

Darkvision

Within 60 feet you see in dim light as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light (without color).

Languages

Common and one more language of your choice.

Size

Small or Medium — your choice.

Small

Disadvantage with Heavy weapons.

Medium

The normal size of a humanoid.

Element

The legacy of one of the genies.

Air Genasi

Speed 35 feet, unending breath, resistance to lightning.

Unending Breath. You can hold your breath indefinitely while you're not incapacitated.

Lightning Resistance. Resistance to lightning damage.

Mingle with the Wind. You know the Shocking Grasp cantrip. From 3rd level — Feather Fall, from 5th — Levitate without a material component; each once per long rest or with a spell slot. Spellcasting ability — Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (chosen with the race).

Spells: Shocking Grasp, Feather Fall (from level 3), Levitate (from level 5)

Earth Genasi

Earth walk and the protection of stone.

Earth Walk. You can move across difficult terrain made of earth or stone without expending extra movement.

Merge with Stone. You know the Blade Ward cantrip and can cast it as a bonus action a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest. From 5th level — Pass without Trace without a material component, once per long rest or with a spell slot of 2nd level or higher. Spellcasting ability — Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (chosen with the race).

Spells: Blade Ward, Pass without Trace (from level 5)

Fire Genasi

Resistance to fire and fire magic.

Fire Resistance. Resistance to fire damage.

Reach to the Blaze. You know the Produce Flame cantrip. From 3rd level — Burning Hands, from 5th — Flame Blade without a material component; each once per long rest or with a spell slot. Spellcasting ability — Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (chosen with the race).

Spells: Produce Flame, Burning Hands (from level 3), Flame Blade (from level 5)

Water Genasi

Swimming speed, amphibious, resistance to acid.

Swim Speed. Swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

Acid Resistance. You have resistance to acid damage.

Amphibious. You can breathe both air and water.

Call to the Wave. You know the Acid Splash cantrip. From 3rd level — Create or Destroy Water, from 5th — Water Walk without a material component; each once per long rest or with a spell slot. Spellcasting ability — Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma (chosen with the race).

Spells: Acid Splash, Create or Destroy Water (from level 3), Water Walk (from level 5)
