Familiars

Find Familiar summons a spirit in a chosen form. A familiar can help and deliver touch spells. Attacking requires a feature that allows it.

The spell also permits any Beast with CR 0. The forms below come from the SRD.

  • Baboon
  • Badger
  • Bat
  • Cat
  • Crab

Druid Wild Shape

A druid knows 4 forms at level 2, 6 at level 4 and 8 at level 8. Swimming is available immediately and flying at level 8. Ask your DM about forms outside the Player’s Handbook list. For Circle of the Moon, maximum CR is druid level divided by 3, rounded down.
