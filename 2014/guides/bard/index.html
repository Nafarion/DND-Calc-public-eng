Bard (2014): College of Eloquence

The strongest bard of Tasha’s Cauldron: Unsettling Words cut an enemy’s save right before your Hypnotic Pattern.

Why It’s Strong

  • Unsettling Words: as a Bonus Action, subtract a Bardic Inspiration die from an enemy’s next saving throw, then cast control — Hold Person, Hypnotic Pattern, Slow.
  • Unfailing Inspiration: an ally who fails keeps the die. Silver Tongue: Persuasion and Deception never roll lower than 10 on the d20.
  • Additional Bard Spells (Tasha’s Cauldron): Slow, Command, Intellect Fortress, Mass Healing Word.
  • Magical Secrets at levels 10, 14 and 18: Counterspell, Fireball, Wall of Force, Simulacrum, Wish, Mass Heal.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: College of Eloquence (from level 3).
  • Race: Half-Elf.
  • Background: Entertainer.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Charisma 15 + 2 = 17, Dexterity 14 + 1 = 15, Constitution 13 + 1 = 14, Wisdom 12, Intelligence 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Acrobatics, Deception (Expertise), Insight, Perception (Expertise), Performance, Persuasion (Expertise), Stealth (Expertise).
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Additional Bard Spells, Magical Inspiration, Bardic Versatility.
  • Equipment: Leather Armor, Rapier, Dagger, Musical Instrument, Entertainer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Charisma +1, Constitution +1
  • Level 8: Charisma +2
  • Level 12: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 16: War Caster feat
  • Level 19: Lucky feat

Level by Level

  • 1: Half-Elf: Charisma +2, Dexterity +1, Constitution +1, Perception and Stealth. Bardic Inspiration (d6).
  • 2: Jack of All Trades (Initiative too), Song of Rest; Magical Inspiration (Tasha’s Cauldron): add the Inspiration die to a spell’s damage or healing.
  • 3: College of Eloquence: Silver Tongue, Unsettling Words. Expertise: Persuasion and Deception.
  • 4: Charisma +1, Constitution +1; Bardic Versatility.
  • 5: Hypnotic Pattern and Slow; Font of Inspiration.
  • 6: Unfailing Inspiration, Universal Speech; Countercharm.
  • 8: Charisma 20.
  • 10: Magical Secrets: Counterspell and Fireball. Expertise: Perception and Stealth.
  • 12: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 14: Magical Secrets: Wall of Force and Simulacrum; Infectious Inspiration.
  • 16: War Caster.
  • 18: Magical Secrets: Wish and Mass Heal.
  • 19: Lucky.
  • 20: Superior Inspiration.

Spells

Cantrips: Vicious Mockery, Minor Illusion, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation.

Known (by priority): Healing Word, Dissonant Whispers, Faerie Fire, Hold Person, Suggestion, Hypnotic Pattern, Slow, Counterspell, Fireball, Wall of Force, Simulacrum, Wish, Mass Heal, Greater Invisibility, Polymorph, Hold Monster, Mass Suggestion, Forcecage, Power Word Stun, Foresight, True Polymorph, Command, Heat Metal, Leomund’s Tiny Hut, Dimension Door, Animate Objects, Otto’s Irresistible Dance, Teleport, Mind Blank.

How to Play

  • Unsettling Words on the target as a Bonus Action, then control with your action: −1d6…−1d12 (the Inspiration die grows with level) to the save nearly guarantees a failure.
  • Hand out Inspiration freely: an ally who fails doesn’t lose it.
  • From level 10, save your Reaction for Counterspell.

Alternatives

  • College of Swords (Xanathar’s) — a fencing bard; College of Spirits (Ravenloft) — strong random tale effects.
  • Race: satyr or yuan-ti (Monsters of the Multiverse) — Advantage on saves against magic and flexible bonuses.
  • College of Lore — Magical Secrets as early as level 6.
  • College of Creation (Tasha’s Cauldron) — a dancing item and Notes of Potential.
