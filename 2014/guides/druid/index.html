Druid (2014): Circle of the Stars

The Dragon starry form makes your Concentration almost unbreakable, the Archer adds Bonus Action damage, and Tasha’s Cauldron adds summons.

Why It’s Strong

  • Starry Form for a Wild Shape use: Dragon — a Concentration save roll never lower than 10 on the d20; Archer — a 1d8 + Wisdom attack as a Bonus Action; Chalice — extra healing.
  • Star Map: Guidance and Guiding Bolt without a slot several times a day.
  • Cosmic Omen (6): +1d6 or −1d6 to any creature’s roll as a Reaction.
  • Additional Druid Spells (Tasha’s Cauldron): Summon Beast, Summon Fey, Summon Elemental — reliable allies.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Circle of the Stars (from level 2).
  • Race: Elf — Wood Elf.
  • Customizing Your Origin (Tasha’s Cauldron): Wisdom +2, Dexterity +1 instead of the race’s book bonuses.
  • Background: Hermit.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Wisdom 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14, Dexterity 13 + 1 = 14, Charisma 12, Intelligence 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Insight, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Religion.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Additional Druid Spells, Wild Companion, Cantrip Versatility.
  • Equipment: Leather Armor, Shield, Scimitar, Druidic Focus, Explorer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Wisdom +1, Constitution +1
  • Level 8: Wisdom +2
  • Level 12: War Caster feat
  • Level 16: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 19: Constitution +2

Level by Level

  • 1: Wood Elf with Customizing Your Origin (Tasha’s Cauldron): Wisdom +2, Dexterity +1, Speed 35 ft., Perception.
  • 2: Circle of the Stars: Star Map (Guidance, Guiding Bolt), Starry Form. Wild Companion (Tasha’s Cauldron): a familiar for a Wild Shape use.
  • 3: Moonbeam, Summon Beast (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 4: Wisdom +1, Constitution +1; Cantrip Versatility.
  • 5: Call Lightning, Summon Fey.
  • 6: Cosmic Omen.
  • 8: Wisdom 20.
  • 10: Twinkling Constellations: 2d8 and flight in Dragon form.
  • 12: War Caster.
  • 14: Full of Stars: Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage in your form.
  • 16: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 20: Archdruid.

Spells

Cantrips: Produce Flame, Shillelagh, Thorn Whip, Druidcraft.

Prepared (by priority): Healing Word, Faerie Fire, Entangle, Goodberry, Moonbeam, Spike Growth, Pass without Trace, Summon Beast, Call Lightning, Conjure Animals, Summon Fey, Dispel Magic, Polymorph, Summon Elemental, Wall of Fire, Mass Cure Wounds, Wall of Stone, Greater Restoration, Sunbeam, Heal, Fire Storm, Regenerate, Animal Shapes, Incendiary Cloud, Foresight, Shapechange, Thunderwave, Lesser Restoration, Plant Growth.

How to Play

  • First turn: a Concentration spell (Moonbeam, a Summon spell), then Dragon form as a Bonus Action.
  • Without Concentration — Archer form: a Bonus Action attack every turn.
  • Druids don’t wear metal armor.

Alternatives

  • Circle of the Shepherd (Xanathar’s) — empowered summoned beasts; Circle of Spores and Circle of Wildfire (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • Race: firbolg (Monsters of the Multiverse) — spells without slots and invisibility as a Bonus Action.
  • Circle of the Moon — the best at levels 2–5.
  • Circle of Wildfire (Tasha’s Cauldron) — teleporting allies and boosting fire.
