Wizard

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. A scholarly mage who has mastered magic through years of study. The wizard knows more spells than anyone and records them in a spellbook.

Class features

Level 1 Spellcasting

Your spellbook holds six 1st-level spells, plus two more each level. Each day you prepare from it a number of spells equal to your wizard level + Intelligence modifier. You can cast rituals from the book without preparing them.

Level 1 Arcane Recovery

Once per day after a Short Rest, regain spell slots with a combined level up to half your wizard level (rounded up, none higher than 5th).

Level 2 Arcane Tradition

You choose a school of magic that you study especially deeply.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

You can cast one 1st-level and one 2nd-level spell from your book at their lowest level without expending a slot.

Level 20 Signature Spells

Two 3rd-level spells are always prepared and can each be cast once per Short Rest without a slot.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 1 Expanded spells

New spells from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything are added to the wizard spell list — you can choose them on the Spells step.

Level 3 Cantrip Formulas

After a long rest, you can replace one wizard cantrip with another from the wizard spell list.

Starting equipment

  • Quarterstaff, Component Pouch, Scholar's Pack, Spellbook
  • Starting wealth: about 100 GP
