Version of October 27, 2026

By registering on the site d20hero.ru or d20hero.online, or by signing in to it for the first time through an external authorization service, I, acting freely, of my own will and in my own interest, give my consent to the owner of the D20Hero site (hereinafter, the operator) to process my personal data on the following terms.

1. List of Personal Data

  • email address, username;
  • data that the external authorization service I chose transmits with my permission: the user ID in the service, name, email address, profile picture;
  • data that I enter in the character sheets saved in the account, including the player name and notes;
  • IP address, date and time of requests to the account server.

2. Purposes of Processing

  • registering an account and signing in to the site, including through an external authorization service;
  • confirming the email address and restoring access;
  • storing characters and accessing them from different devices;

3. Actions with Personal Data and Method of Processing

Collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access), blocking, deletion, destruction. The processing is automated.

4. Entrusting Processing

I agree that the operator may entrust the processing of personal data to third parties. These are persons providing the operator with hosting, email delivery, user authorization and protection against automated actions. Entrusting is permitted to the extent necessary for the purposes stated in section 2, provided that such persons comply with applicable personal data protection law.

5. Term and Withdrawal

The consent is valid from the day it is given until the account is deleted or until the consent is withdrawn. You can withdraw consent through the “Contact us” form at the bottom of any page of the site, stating the email address or username of the account. After consent is withdrawn, the operator stops processing and destroys the personal data within a period not exceeding 30 days, unless otherwise required by law.
