Grave Domain

A subclass of the Cleric class (cleric subclass), D&D 2024. Chosen at level 3. Clerics who stand on the border between life and death: they destroy undead, lay souls to rest and briefly stave off death. Source: Ravenloft: The Horrors Within.

Create a character: Cleric — Grave Domain

Subclass features

Level 3 Grave Domain Spells

Always prepared: Ray of Enfeeblement, Gentle Repose, Detect Evil and Good, False Life, Spare the Dying (3), Revivify, Vampiric Touch (5), Death Ward, Blight (7), Raise Dead, Dispel Evil and Good (9).

Level 3 Circle of Mortality

Push toward Death: once per turn, when you deal damage with a spell or attack to a creature below its Hit Point maximum, you add 1d4 Necrotic damage (1d6 from level 11). Return to Life: Spare the Dying as a Bonus Action; if a spell or Channel Divinity heals a creature with 0 Hit Points, the healing dice aren't rolled but use their maximum.

Level 3 Path to the Grave

As a Bonus Action you present your holy symbol and spend Channel Divinity: a creature you can see within 30 feet is cursed until the start of your next turn and makes attack rolls and saving throws with Disadvantage. When you or an ally you can see hits it with an attack, the curse can be ended — the attack deals extra Necrotic or Radiant damage equal to your cleric level.

Level 6 Sentinel at Death's Door

When you or a Bloodied creature you can see within 60 feet is hit by an attack, as a Reaction you halve the damage (rounded down); a Critical Hit becomes a normal hit. Number of uses — your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1), regained after a Long Rest.

Level 17 Divine Reaper

Improved Necromancy: a single-target Necromancy spell of up to level 5 or a Grave domain spell gains, for Channel Divinity, a second target within range (costly and consumed components — for each target). Keeper of Souls: when an enemy dies within 60 feet, you or another creature within 60 feet regains Hit Points equal to twice your cleric level (while you aren't Incapacitated); once per Short or Long Rest, again — for a spell slot of level 6 or higher.

Subclass spells

Always prepared; they don't count against your prepared spells.
