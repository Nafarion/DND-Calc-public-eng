Species D&D 2024

Species of the 2024 Player's Handbook and supplements (Forge of the Artificer, The Horrors Within, Tasha's): size, speed, traits and options such as lineages and legacies.

  • AasimarMortals who carry a spark of the Upper Planes. Aasimar are born among any people and bear the burden of a celestial mission.
  • ChangelingForge of the ArtificerShapechangers with fey blood: they change appearance and voice at will and easily pass for anyone.
  • Custom LineageTasha'sYour own lineage instead of a set species — a Small or Medium humanoid with an Origin feat and a variable trait. From a 2014 book: under the 2024 rules, with your DM's agreement; the ability score increase doesn't apply (the background grants it).
  • DhampirThe Horrors WithinThe living with the strength and hunger of vampires: they crave blood, dreams or life force and decide whether to restrain that hunger. The species also appears in Astarion's Book of Hungers.
  • DragonbornDescendants of dragons who carry their blood, scales and breath. A proud people for whom the honor of their line stands above their own life.
  • DwarfA people of mountain halls and deep mines. Dwarves are famed for stubbornness, long memory and unmatched mastery of stone and metal.
  • ElfA long-lived people tied to the magic of the Feywild. Elves don't sleep but enter a trance, and remember centuries the way humans remember years.
  • GnomeA small people with boundless curiosity and an innate spark of magic. Gnomes find wonder in every little thing and are rarely idle.
  • GoliathTowering descendants of giants who live in the highlands. Goliaths value fair competition and measure themselves by their own achievements, not others' praise.
  • HalflingA small people who value comfort, a good meal and a quiet life — and yet time and again find themselves at the center of great events.
  • HexbloodThe Horrors WithinBearers of fey magic — after a bargain with a hag or another otherworldly influence; gifted with foresight and charm.
  • HumanThe most numerous and diverse people. Humans win through ambition and adaptability — where other peoples wait centuries, a human gets it done in one lifetime.
  • KalashtarForge of the ArtificerHumans bound to the quori dream spirits: telepaths with a dual mind.
  • KhoravarForge of the ArtificerThe half-elves of Eberron: diplomats and jacks-of-all-trades tied to fey magic.
  • LupinThe Horrors WithinSurvivors of a werewolf encounter in whom lycanthropy never reached full strength: forever frozen in a half-wolf form.
  • OrcA people built for endurance and swift onslaught. Orcs don't retreat while they're still standing — and they stay standing longer than almost anyone.
  • RebornThe Horrors WithinThe dead who have returned to life — risen, stitched together from different bodies or awakened in an artificial shell.
  • ShifterForge of the ArtificerDescendants of lycanthropes: they briefly take on a bestial form.
  • TieflingMortals marked by the legacy of the Lower Planes. Horns, a tail and heat in the blood — the price of a bargain struck by an ancestor.
  • WarforgedForge of the ArtificerLiving constructs built for the Last War and searching for their place in the world.
