College of Spirits

A subclass of the Bard class (bard subclass), D&D 2024. Chosen at level 3. Bards who summon spirits from ancient legends — willful ones, so it's not always the expected spirit that answers. Source: Ravenloft: The Horrors Within.

Create a character: Bard — College of Spirits

Subclass features

Level 3 Channeler

Guiding Whispers: you know the Guidance cantrip, and its range is 60 feet. Spiritual Focus: you gain playing cards and proficiency with them; cards, a crystal or orb, a candle or a quill serve as your Spellcasting Focus for Bard spells.

Level 3 Spirits from Beyond

When you give a Bardic Inspiration die as a Bonus Action, you can summon a random spirit: roll the Inspiration die and consult the table. The spirit stays until you release it or until a Short or Long Rest. Controlled Channeling: as a Bonus Action, spending a use of Bardic Inspiration, you choose the spirit yourself — with a number no higher than the Inspiration die's maximum. Unleashing a Spirit: with a Magic action, the spirit's effect is applied to a creature you can see within 30 feet (DC — your Bard spell save DC). Spirits: 1 Beloved — healing equal to the die + Charisma; 2 Sharpshooter — Force damage equal to the die + Charisma; 3 Avenger — until the end of your next turn, a creature that hits the target with a melee attack takes Force damage equal to the die; 4 Renegade — the target teleports up to 30 feet as a Reaction; 5 Fortune Teller — Advantage on all D20 Tests until the start of your next turn; 6 Wayfarer — Temporary Hit Points equal to the die + bard level and +10 feet of Speed while they last; 7 Trickster — Wisdom saving throw: Psychic damage of 2 dice and Charmed until the start of your turn, half damage on a success; 8 Shade — Invisible until the end of the target's next turn (ends on an attack, damage or a spell), then a Constitution saving throw in a 5-foot Emanation: Necrotic damage of 2 dice; 9 Arsonist — Dexterity saving throw: Fire of 4 dice, half on a success; 10 Coward — the target and chosen creatures within 30 feet: Wisdom saving throw, or Frightened until the start of your turn, Speed halved and only an action or a Bonus Action; 11 Brute — chosen creatures within 30 feet of the target: Strength saving throw, Thunder damage of 3 dice and knocked Prone, half damage on a success; 12 Priest — healing of 2 dice and ending one condition: Deafened, Blinded, Poisoned, Charmed, Stunned or Paralyzed.

Level 6 Empowered Channeling

Power from Beyond: once per turn, when you cast with a spell slot a Bard spell that deals damage or heals, you add 1d6 to one damage roll or to the healing. Spiritual Manifestation: Spirit Guardians is always prepared, once per Long Rest — without a spell slot; once per Short or Long Rest you can alter the guardians — you and allies in their Emanation have Half Cover.

Level 14 Mystical Connection

When you roll on the Spirits from Beyond table, you roll twice and choose the spirit; on matching results — any spirit from the table.

Subclass spells

Always prepared; they don't count against your prepared spells.
