Infuse Item: Artificer — D&D 5e (2014 rules)

Artificer infusions from Tasha's Cauldron: you know 4 infusions at 2nd level, 6 at 6th, 8 at 10th, 10 at 14th and 12 at 18th. After a long rest you infuse them into nonmagical items (2 items at a time, from 6th level 3, from 10th 4, from 14th 5, from 18th 6). When you gain a level, you can replace one known infusion.

Known: level 2: 4 · level 6: 6 · level 10: 8 · level 14: 10 · level 18: 12.

Create a character: Artificer

From level 2

  • Enhanced Arcane Focus

    Item: a rod, staff or wand. Requires attunement. +1 to spell attack rolls (+2 from 10th artificer level); spell attacks ignore half cover.

  • Enhanced Defense

    Item: armor or a shield. +1 to AC (+2 from 10th artificer level).

  • Enhanced Weapon

    Item: a simple or martial weapon. +1 to attack and damage rolls with this weapon (+2 from 10th artificer level).

  • Homunculus Servant

    Item: a gem or crystal worth at least 100 gp. A loyal homunculus servant appears: it flies, can deliver your spells with a range of touch, and its hit points grow with your level. It acts immediately after you and takes the Dodge action unless you command otherwise with a bonus action.

  • Mind Sharpener

    Item: armor or robes. Requires attunement. 4 charges. When the wearer fails a Constitution saving throw to maintain concentration, they can use their reaction to expend a charge and succeed. Regains 1d4 charges at dawn.

  • Repeating Shot

    Item: a simple or martial weapon with the ammunition property. Requires attunement. +1 to attack and damage with ranged attacks made with this weapon; the weapon ignores the loading property and produces its own ammunition.

  • Replicate Magic Item

    Item: an item from the list. You create one item from the list available at your level. 2nd: bag of holding, alchemy jug, goggles of night, rope of climbing, sending stones, wand of magic detection, wand of secrets and others. 6th: boots and cloak of elvenkind, gloves of thievery, lantern of revealing, ring of water walking and others. 10th: cloak of protection, bracers of archery, gauntlets of ogre power, headband of intellect, helm of telepathy, winged boots and others. 14th: amulet of health, boots of speed, bracers of defense, ring of protection, ring of free action and others. You can choose this infusion multiple times, a different item each time.

  • Returning Weapon

    Item: a simple or martial weapon with the thrown property. +1 to attack and damage with this weapon; after a ranged attack it immediately returns to your hand.

From level 6

  • Boots of the Winding Path

    Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer

    Item: footwear. Requires attunement. As a bonus action, the wearer teleports up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space they occupied this turn.

  • Radiant Weapon

    Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer

    Item: a simple or martial weapon. Requires attunement. +1 to attack and damage. As a bonus action, the weapon starts or stops shedding light (bright light 30 feet). 4 charges: as a reaction, when the wearer is hit by an attack, blind the attacker until the end of its next turn (Constitution saving throw). Regains 1d4 charges at dawn.

  • Repulsion Shield

    Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer

    Item: a shield. Requires attunement. +1 to AC. 4 charges: as a reaction, when the wearer is hit by a melee attack, push the attacker 15 feet away. Regains 1d4 charges at dawn.

  • Resistant Armor

    Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer

    Item: armor. Requires attunement. Resistance to one damage type chosen when you infuse it: acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant or thunder.

  • Spell-Refueling Ring

    Prerequisite: 6th-level artificer

    Item: a ring. Requires attunement. As an action, the wearer regains one expended spell slot of 3rd level or lower. Once per day, until dawn.

From level 10

  • Armor of Magical Strength

    Prerequisite: 10th-level artificer

    Item: armor. Requires attunement. 6 charges. The wearer can expend a charge to add their Intelligence modifier to a Strength check or saving throw, or as a reaction to avoid being knocked prone. Regains 1d6 charges at dawn.

  • Helm of Awareness

    Prerequisite: 10th-level artificer

    Item: a helmet. Requires attunement. Advantage on initiative rolls; the wearer can't be surprised while not incapacitated.

From level 14

  • Arcane Propulsion Armor

    Prerequisite: 14th-level artificer

    Item: armor. Requires attunement. The wearer's walking speed +5 feet. The armor's gauntlets are magic melee weapons (1d8 force), and they can be thrown (20/60 feet), returning after the throw. The armor can't be removed against your will, and it replaces missing limbs.
