Eldritch Invocations: Warlock — D&D 5e (2014 rules)

Eldritch invocations are a warlock's permanent magical abilities. At 2nd level you know two, then more (up to eight at 18th level); when you gain a warlock level, you can replace one known invocation. Tasha's Cauldron invocations are available if its optional feature Eldritch Versatility (new invocations and Pact of the Talisman) is enabled.

Known: level 2: 2 · level 5: 3 · level 7: 4 · level 9: 5 · level 12: 6 · level 15: 7 · level 18: 8.

Create a character: Warlock

No level requirement

  • Agonizing Blast

    Prerequisite: Eldritch Blast cantrip

    Add your Charisma modifier to the damage of eldritch blast on a hit.

  • Armor of Shadows

    You can cast mage armor on yourself at will, without expending a spell slot or material components.

  • Aspect of the Moon

    Prerequisite: Pact of the Tome. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    You don't need to sleep, and magic can't put you to sleep; a long rest is 8 hours of light activity.

  • Beast Speech

    You can cast speak with animals at will, without expending a spell slot.

  • Beguiling Influence

    Proficiency in the Deception and Persuasion skills.

  • Book of Ancient Secrets

    Prerequisite: Pact of the Tome

    Inscribe two 1st-level ritual spells from any classes' lists in your Book of Shadows; with the book in hand, you can cast them as rituals. You can copy rituals you find of a level no higher than half your warlock level (rounded up): 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level.

  • Devil's Sight

    You can see normally in darkness, both magical and nonmagical, to a distance of 120 feet.

  • Eldritch Mind

    Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

    Advantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration.

  • Eldritch Sight

    You can cast detect magic at will, without expending a spell slot.

  • Eldritch Spear

    Prerequisite: Eldritch Blast cantrip

    The range of eldritch blast is 300 feet.

  • Eyes of the Rune Keeper

    You can read all writing.

  • Fiendish Vigor

    You can cast false life on yourself at will as a 1st-level spell, without expending a spell slot or material components.

  • Gaze of Two Minds

    As an action, touch a willing humanoid: until the end of your next turn you perceive through its senses (including its special senses), while you're blinded and deafened to your own surroundings. While you're on the same plane, you can extend the link with an action.

  • Gift of the Ever-Living Ones

    Prerequisite: Pact of the Chain. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    While your familiar is within 100 feet of you, dice you roll to regain hit points give you the maximum.

  • Grasp of Hadar

    Prerequisite: Eldritch Blast cantrip. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    Once per turn, when you hit with eldritch blast, pull the creature up to 10 feet toward you in a straight line.

  • Improved Pact Weapon

    Prerequisite: Pact of the Blade. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    Your pact weapon is your spellcasting focus; a nonmagical one gains +1 to attack and damage rolls; you can also summon a shortbow or longbow, light or heavy crossbow.

  • Investment of the Chain Master

    Prerequisite: Pact of the Chain. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

    The familiar from find familiar gains a flying or swimming speed of 40 feet, attacks on your bonus action, its weapon attacks are magical, its save DC is yours; use your reaction to give it resistance to damage it takes.

  • Lance of Lethargy

    Prerequisite: Eldritch Blast cantrip. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    Once per turn, when you hit with eldritch blast, reduce the target's speed by 10 feet until the end of your next turn.

  • Mask of Many Faces

    You can cast disguise self at will, without expending a spell slot.

  • Misty Visions

    You can cast silent image at will, without expending a spell slot or material components.

  • Rebuke of the Talisman

    Prerequisite: Pact of the Talisman. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

    When the talisman's wearer is hit by an attack, use your reaction to deal psychic damage equal to your proficiency bonus to the attacker you can see within 30 feet and push it 10 feet away.

  • Repelling Blast

    Prerequisite: Eldritch Blast cantrip

    When you hit with eldritch blast, you can push the creature up to 10 feet away from you in a straight line.

  • Thief of Five Fates

    Once per long rest, you can cast bane with a warlock spell slot.

  • Voice of the Chain Master

    Prerequisite: Pact of the Chain

    While you're on the same plane, you communicate telepathically with your familiar, perceive through its senses and can speak through it in your own voice.

From level 5

  • Cloak of Flies

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    As a bonus action, surround yourself with a 5-foot aura of flies: advantage on Intimidation, disadvantage on other Charisma checks; a creature that starts its turn in the aura takes poison damage equal to your Charisma modifier. Once per short or long rest.

  • Eldritch Smite

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock, Pact of the Blade. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    Once per turn, when you hit with your pact weapon, expend a warlock spell slot: an extra 1d8 force damage + 1d8 per slot level, and you can knock a target of Huge size or smaller prone.

  • Far Scribe

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock, Pact of the Tome. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

    A page for names (equal to your proficiency bonus) appears in your Book of Shadows. You can send creatures written there a sending without a slot or material components by writing it on the page; the reply appears on the same page.

  • Gift of the Depths

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    You can breathe underwater and gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed; once per long rest — water breathing without a slot.

  • Maddening Hex

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock, hex spell or a warlock feature that curses (e.g. Hexblade's Curse). Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    As a bonus action, the target cursed by you within 30 feet and chosen creatures within 5 feet of it take psychic damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

  • Mire the Mind

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock

    Once per long rest, you can cast slow with a warlock spell slot.

  • One with Shadows

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock

    In dim light or darkness, use your action to become invisible until you move or take an action or a reaction.

  • Sign of Ill Omen

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock

    Once per long rest, you can cast bestow curse with a warlock spell slot.

  • Thirsting Blade

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock, Pact of the Blade

    When you take the Attack action, you attack twice with your pact weapon.

  • Tomb of Levistus

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    When you take damage, use your reaction to encase yourself in ice until the end of your next turn: 10 temporary hit points per warlock level against that damage, but vulnerability to fire, speed 0 and incapacitated. Once per short or long rest.

  • Undying Servitude

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

    Once per long rest — animate dead without a slot.

From level 7

  • Bewitching Whispers

    Prerequisite: 7th-level warlock

    Once per long rest, you can cast compulsion with a warlock spell slot.

  • Dreadful Word

    Prerequisite: 7th-level warlock

    Once per long rest, you can cast confusion with a warlock spell slot.

  • Ghostly Gaze

    Prerequisite: 7th-level warlock. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    As an action, for 1 minute (as if concentrating) you see through solid objects within 30 feet and gain darkvision there. Once per short or long rest.

  • Protection of the Talisman

    Prerequisite: 7th-level warlock, Pact of the Talisman. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

    When the talisman's wearer fails a saving throw, they can add a d4. Uses equal your proficiency bonus, regained after a long rest.

  • Relentless Hex

    Prerequisite: 7th-level warlock, hex spell or a warlock feature that curses (e.g. Hexblade's Curse). Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    As a bonus action, teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see within 5 feet of the target cursed by you that you can see.

  • Sculptor of Flesh

    Prerequisite: 7th-level warlock

    Once per long rest, you can cast polymorph with a warlock spell slot.

  • Trickster's Escape

    Prerequisite: 7th-level warlock. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    Once per long rest — freedom of movement on yourself without a slot.

From level 9

  • Ascendant Step

    Prerequisite: 9th-level warlock

    You can cast levitate on yourself at will, without expending a spell slot or material components.

  • Gift of the Protectors

    Prerequisite: 9th-level warlock, Pact of the Tome. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

    A page for names (equal to your proficiency bonus) appears in your Book of Shadows. When a creature written there drops to 0 hit points but isn't killed outright, it's left with 1 hit point instead. Once per long rest for all of them.

  • Minions of Chaos

    Prerequisite: 9th-level warlock

    Once per long rest, you can cast conjure elemental with a warlock spell slot.

  • Otherworldly Leap

    Prerequisite: 9th-level warlock

    You can cast jump on yourself at will, without expending a spell slot or material components.

  • Whispers of the Grave

    Prerequisite: 9th-level warlock

    You can cast speak with dead at will, without expending a spell slot.

From level 12

  • Bond of the Talisman

    Prerequisite: 12th-level warlock, Pact of the Talisman. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

    While another creature wears your talisman, you can use your action to teleport to it (or it to you), if you're on the same plane. Uses equal your proficiency bonus, regained after a long rest.

  • Lifedrinker

    Prerequisite: 12th-level warlock, Pact of the Blade

    A hit with your pact weapon deals extra necrotic damage equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1).

From level 15

  • Chains of Carceri

    Prerequisite: 15th-level warlock, Pact of the Chain

    You can cast hold monster at will, without expending a spell slot or material components, if the target is a celestial, fiend or elemental. On the same creature — again only after a long rest.

  • Master of Myriad Forms

    Prerequisite: 15th-level warlock

    You can cast alter self at will, without expending a spell slot.

  • Shroud of Shadow

    Prerequisite: 15th-level warlock. Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything

    You can cast invisibility at will, without expending a spell slot.

  • Visions of Distant Realms

    Prerequisite: 15th-level warlock

    You can cast arcane eye at will, without expending a spell slot.

  • Witch Sight

    Prerequisite: 15th-level warlock

    You see the true form of shapechangers and creatures concealed by illusion or transmutation magic, if they're within 30 feet and within line of sight.
