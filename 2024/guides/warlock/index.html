Warlock: Fiend Patron — Eldritch Blast and Fire

The best cantrip in the game, Temporary Hit Points for every kill and Fireball on your list.

Why It’s Strong

  • Eldritch Blast with the Agonizing Blast and Repelling Blast invocations — steady damage and position control at 120 ft.; Eldritch Spear adds another 30 ft. per warlock level.
  • Dark One’s Blessing grants Temporary Hit Points (Charisma + warlock level) when an enemy near you drops to 0 Hit Points.
  • The Fiend keeps Fireball, Wall of Fire, Suggestion and Command prepared.
  • The 2024 Magical Cunning restores half your slots once a day without a Short Rest.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Fiend Patron (from level 3).
  • Species: Tiefling — Infernal.
  • Background: Merchant — Lucky feat, bonuses: Charisma +2, Constitution +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Charisma 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 12, Intelligence 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Animal Handling, Arcana, Deception, Persuasion.
  • Equipment: Leather Armor, Sickle, Dagger ×2, Arcane Focus, Scholar’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Fey-Touched feat (+1 Charisma)
  • Level 8: Charisma +2
  • Level 12: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 16: War Caster feat (+1 Wisdom)
  • Level 19: Boon of Spell Recall feat (+1 Charisma)

Level by Level

  • 1: Tiefling (Infernal legacy): Fire Bolt (another legacy spell at level 3 and a second one at level 5). Merchant background: Lucky. Invocation: Pact of the Tome or Eldritch Mind (Advantage on Concentration saves).
  • 2: Agonizing Blast and Repelling Blast invocations. Magical Cunning.
  • 3: Fiend Patron: Dark One’s Blessing. Burning Hands, Command, Scorching Ray and Suggestion always prepared. Tiefling: Hellish Rebuke.
  • 4: Fey-Touched (+1 Charisma): Misty Step and Bless once a day for free.
  • 5: 3rd-level slots: Fireball, Hunger of Hadar, Counterspell. Eldritch Blast fires two beams. Tiefling: Darkness.
  • 6: Dark One’s Own Luck: +1d10 to a check or saving throw.
  • 8: Charisma 20.
  • 10: Fiendish Resilience: Resistance to a damage type of your choice.
  • 11: Mystic Arcanum: Mass Suggestion. Three beams.
  • 12: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 13: Arcanum: Forcecage.
  • 14: Hurl Through Hell: 8d10 Psychic damage.
  • 15: Arcanum: Power Word Stun.
  • 16: War Caster.
  • 17: Arcanum: True Polymorph. Four beams.
  • 19: Boon of Spell Recall (Charisma 21).
  • 20: Eldritch Master.

Spells

Cantrips: Eldritch Blast, Mind Sliver, Prestidigitation, Minor Illusion.

Prepared (by priority): Hex, Armor of Agathys, Protection from Evil and Good, Unseen Servant, Misty Step, Hold Person, Mirror Image, Invisibility, Hunger of Hadar, Counterspell, Hypnotic Pattern, Fly, Banishment, Dimension Door, Summon Aberration, Synaptic Static, Hold Monster, Jallarzi’s Storm of Radiance.

Mystic Arcanum: 6th — Mass Suggestion, 7th — Forcecage, 8th — Power Word Stun, 9th — True Polymorph.

How to Play

  • Your main turn is Eldritch Blast: each beam adds Charisma and pushes 10 ft. (into Hunger of Hadar or the druid’s thorns).
  • Hex as a Bonus Action adds 1d6 to every beam — with four beams that’s a lot.
  • Warlock slots are always your highest level — spend them on control and deal damage with your cantrip.

Alternatives

  • Vestige Patron (Arcana Unleashed); Undead Patron (Ravenloft); compatible options — The Genie (Tasha’s Cauldron) and The Hexblade (Xanathar’s).
  • Ravenloft Dark Gifts (feats): Touch of Death for a melee warlock.
  • Pact of the Blade: weapon attacks with Charisma, Thirsting Blade and Eldritch Smite.
  • Great Old One Patron — stronger control and Psychic damage.
