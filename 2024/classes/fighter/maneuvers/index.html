Maneuvers: Battle Master — D&D 2024

Battle Master maneuvers expend Superiority Dice; only one maneuver per attack. New maneuvers come at Fighter levels 7, 10 and 15, and at those levels you can replace one known maneuver.

Known: level 3: 3 · level 7: 5 · level 10: 7 · level 15: 9.

Create a character: Fighter

No level requirement

  • Ambush

    When you make a Dexterity (Stealth) check or an Initiative roll, you can expend a Superiority Die and add it to the roll, if you don't have the Incapacitated condition.

  • Bait and Switch

    On your turn, next to (within 5 feet of) a willing creature without the Incapacitated condition, expend a Superiority Die and 5 feet of movement to swap places with it without provoking Opportunity Attacks. You or it gain a bonus to AC equal to the die roll until the start of your next turn.

  • Commander's Strike

    When you take the Attack action, forgo one attack and expend a Superiority Die: a willing ally who can see or hear you immediately uses their Reaction to make an attack with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike and adds the die to the damage on a hit.

  • Commanding Presence

    When you make a Charisma (Performance, Intimidation or Persuasion) check, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the roll.

  • Disarming Attack

    When you hit with an attack, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the damage: the target makes a Strength saving throw or drops an object of your choice in its space.

  • Distracting Strike

    When you hit with an attack, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the damage: the next attack against the target by another creature before the start of your turn has Advantage.

  • Evasive Footwork

    As a Bonus Action, expend a Superiority Die and take the Disengage action; the die roll is added to your AC until the start of your next turn.

  • Feinting Attack

    As a Bonus Action, expend a Superiority Die to feint against a creature within 5 feet: your next attack against it this turn has Advantage, and on a hit the die is added to the damage.

  • Goading Attack

    When you hit with an attack, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the damage: on a failed Wisdom saving throw, the target has Disadvantage on attacks against anyone but you until the end of your next turn.

  • Lunging Attack

    As a Bonus Action, expend a Superiority Die and take the Dash action; if you moved at least 5 feet in a straight line before hitting with a melee attack of the Attack action, the die is added to the damage.

  • Maneuvering Attack

    When you hit with an attack, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the damage: a willing ally who can see or hear you can use their Reaction to move up to half their Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks from the target.

  • Menacing Attack

    When you hit with an attack, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the damage: on a failed Wisdom saving throw, the target has the Frightened condition until the end of your next turn.

  • Parry

    When you take damage from a melee attack, use your Reaction to expend a Superiority Die and reduce the damage by the die roll + your Strength or Dexterity modifier.

  • Precision Attack

    When you miss with an attack, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the attack roll — the miss can become a hit.

  • Pushing Attack

    When you hit with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the damage: a target of size Large or smaller is pushed 15 feet on a failed Strength saving throw.

  • Rally

    As a Bonus Action, expend a Superiority Die: an ally within 30 feet who can see or hear you gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the die roll + half your Fighter level.

  • Riposte

    When a creature misses you with a melee attack, use your Reaction to expend a Superiority Die and attack it with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike; on a hit, the die is added to the damage.

  • Sweeping Attack

    When you hit with a melee attack, expend a Superiority Die: another creature within 5 feet of the target and within your reach, if the attack would also hit it, takes damage equal to the die roll, of the same type.

  • Tactical Assessment

    When you make an Intelligence (History or Investigation) or Wisdom (Insight) check, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the roll.

  • Trip Attack

    When you hit with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike, expend a Superiority Die and add it to the damage: a target of size Large or smaller has the Prone condition on a failed Strength saving throw.
