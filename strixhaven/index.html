Strixhaven: the five colleges of the university of magic

The colleges of Strixhaven university from Strixhaven: A Curriculum of Chaos: what they teach, which cantrips the Strixhaven Initiate feat grants and which spells a student background adds.

The university · Colleges · Strixhaven Initiate · College spells

Strixhaven university

Strixhaven is a university of magic from the Magic: The Gathering multiverse. Five great dragons founded it, and each college bears its founder’s name and combines two of the five colors of magic. A student chooses their college’s background; it grants the Strixhaven Initiate feat and adds the college’s spells to the spell lists of their classes. Among the students there are also owlin.

The Strixhaven Initiate feat

The feat grants two cantrips of the college and one level 1 spell from the list of one of two classes; you can cast that spell once per long rest without a spell slot, and with spell slots. Spellcasting ability — Intelligence, Wisdom or Charisma.

  • Lorehold: two cantrips from Light, Sacred Flame or Thaumaturgy; a level 1 spell from the cleric or wizard spell list.
  • Prismari: two cantrips from Fire Bolt, Prestidigitation or Ray of Frost; a level 1 spell from the bard or sorcerer spell list.
  • Quandrix: two cantrips from Druidcraft, Guidance or Mage Hand; a level 1 spell from the druid or wizard spell list.
  • Silverquill: two cantrips from Sacred Flame, Thaumaturgy or Vicious Mockery; a level 1 spell from the bard or cleric spell list.
  • Witherbloom: two cantrips from Chill Touch, Druidcraft or Spare the Dying; a level 1 spell from the druid or wizard spell list.

From level 4, a character with this feat can take the Strixhaven Mascot feat: they cast Find Familiar as a ritual, and the familiar takes the form of their college’s mascot.

College spells

If a student has the Spellcasting or Pact Magic feature, their college’s spells are added to the spell lists of their classes.

Lorehold

  • Level 1: Comprehend Languages, Identify
  • Level 2: Borrowed Knowledge, Locate Object
  • Level 3: Speak with Dead, Spirit Guardians
  • Level 4: Arcane Eye, Stone Shape
  • Level 5: Flame Strike, Legend Lore

Prismari

  • Level 1: Chromatic Orb, Thunderwave
  • Level 2: Flaming Sphere, Kinetic Jaunt
  • Level 3: Haste, Water Walk
  • Level 4: Freedom of Movement, Wall of Fire
  • Level 5: Cone of Cold, Conjure Elemental

Quandrix

  • Level 1: Entangle, Guiding Bolt
  • Level 2: Enlarge/Reduce, Vortex Warp
  • Level 3: Aura of Vitality, Haste
  • Level 4: Control Water, Freedom of Movement
  • Level 5: Circle of Power, Passwall

Silverquill

  • Level 1: Dissonant Whispers, Silvery Barbs
  • Level 2: Calm Emotions, Darkness
  • Level 3: Beacon of Hope, Daylight
  • Level 4: Compulsion, Confusion
  • Level 5: Dominate Person, Rary's Telepathic Bond

Witherbloom

  • Level 1: Cure Wounds, Inflict Wounds
  • Level 2: Lesser Restoration, Wither and Bloom
  • Level 3: Revivify, Vampiric Touch
  • Level 4: Death Ward, Blight
  • Level 5: Greater Restoration, Antilife Shell
