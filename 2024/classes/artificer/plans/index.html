Magic Item Plans: Artificer — D&D 2024

Plans for the Replicate Magic Item feature: the artificer knows four plans at level 2 and one more each at 6, 10, 14 and 18. After a Long Rest, holding Tinker's Tools, you create items from them (2 at a time, from level 6 — 3, from 10 — 4, from 14 — 5, from 18 — 6). When you gain a level, you can replace one plan.

Known: level 2: 4 · level 6: 5 · level 10: 6 · level 14: 7 · level 18: 8.
