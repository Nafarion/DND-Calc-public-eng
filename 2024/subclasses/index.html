D&D 2024 subclasses

All 76 subclasses of the 2024 Player's Handbook and supplements (Heroes of Faerûn, Forge of the Artificer, Arcana Unleashed, The Horrors Within), by class.

Barbarian: barbarian subclass, from level 3

  • Path of the BerserkerRage as pure destruction. The Berserker turns combat into a storm and doesn't stop while still standing.
  • Path of the Wild HeartA barbarian whose rage calls upon animal spirits. Each fight summons the power of a totem animal.
  • Path of the World TreeA barbarian whose rage draws power from Yggdrasil — the tree that binds the planes of existence.
  • Path of the ZealotRage consecrated by a deity. The Zealot fights like one possessed and doesn't let death take them easily.

Bard: bard subclass, from level 3

  • College of DanceA bard for whom movement is the spell. Dance makes them elusive and deadly.
  • College of GlamourA bard who trained in the Feywild. Their performance bends the will of the audience.
  • College of LoreA collector of secrets and others' magic. The Lore bard can undercut another's success with a well-chosen word.
  • College of SpiritsBards who summon spirits from ancient legends — willful ones, so it's not always the expected spirit that answers.
  • College of the MoonBards of the druidic circles of the Moonshae Isles: they keep the history of the isles and bolster their allies with fey magic and the power of the moonwells.
  • College of ValorA skald who sings of heroes — and fights alongside them in the front rank.

Cleric: cleric subclass, from level 3

  • Arcana DomainClerics of the gods of magic see arcane knowledge not as a source of personal power but as a gift to bring to others.
  • Grave DomainClerics who stand on the border between life and death: they destroy undead, lay souls to rest and briefly stave off death.
  • Knowledge DomainServants of the gods of knowledge and invention (Oghma, Gond): they value learning above wealth and master the mind.
  • Life DomainA healing cleric whose calling is to preserve life and undo wounds.
  • Light DomainA servant of the sun gods who drives back darkness with flame and radiance.
  • Trickery DomainA cleric of trickster gods. Illusion and switching places are as sacred to them as an altar is to others.
  • War DomainA battle cleric for whom the blade is an extension of prayer.

Druid: druid subclass, from level 3

  • Circle of the LandA keeper of nature lore whose magic reflects the character of their home land: arid, polar, temperate or tropical.
  • Circle of the MoonA shapeshifter druid for whom beast form is the main combat form.
  • Circle of the SeaA druid of tides and storms, around whom a squall rises of its own accord.
  • Circle of the StarsA druid who reads fate in the constellations and takes on their form.

Fighter: fighter subclass, from level 3

  • Arcane ArcherArchers who weave magic into their shots in the old elven way.
  • BanneretWarlord knights of Cormyr, Damara and the Silver Marches: leadership turns allies into a cohesive unit.
  • Battle MasterA tactician who has broken combat down into maneuvers. Every maneuver is calculation, not impulse.
  • ChampionA warrior who has perfected simple things: hitting more often, more accurately, and lasting longer.
  • Eldritch KnightA warrior who weaves magic into swordplay. Blade and spell work in tandem.
  • Psi WarriorA warrior whose power is born in the mind: telekinesis, a psionic shield and strikes of pure energy.

Monk: monk subclass, from level 3

  • Warrior of MercyA monk whose hands can heal and end a life equally well.
  • Warrior of ShadowAn infiltrator monk who melts into darkness and strikes from where no one expects.
  • Warrior of the ElementsA monk who channels Focus into the elements: fire, ice, wind and stone obey their movements.
  • Warrior of the Mystic ArtsMonks who have raised their focus on inner power to magic: Sorcerer spells combined with martial arts.
  • Warrior of the Open HandThe classic school of unarmed combat, where an Unarmed Strike is deadlier than any blade.

Paladin: paladin subclass, from level 3

  • Oath of DevotionThe classic paladin: honor, truth and protecting the weak without compromise.
  • Oath of GloryA paladin who strives for deeds worthy of a song. Glory isn't vanity but an obligation.
  • Oath of the AncientsA paladin of light and life who protects the world's beauty from the darkness that would snuff it out.
  • Oath of the Noble GeniesPaladins who revere the powers of the four kinds of genies — dao, djinn, efreet and marids; many hail from Calimshan.
  • Oath of VengeanceA paladin who pursues the enemy to the end. Justice matters more than mercy.

Ranger: ranger subclass, from level 3

  • Beast MasterA ranger who fights side by side with a primal companion — a beast bound to them by magic.
  • Fey WandererA ranger marked by the Feywild: their strikes carry Psychic damage, and their speech is strangely persuasive.
  • Gloom StalkerA ranger of darkness and the first strike. They see where it's dark even for Darkvision.
  • Hollow WardenRangers who draw power from the ancient horrors of the wilds and become ruthless monstrous guardians.
  • HunterA versatile ranger who adapts tactics to specific prey — from a lone giant to a horde.
  • Winter WalkerRuthless rangers of Icewind Dale: they hunt the monsters of the arctic wastes and become the terror of the ice themselves.

Rogue: rogue subclass, from level 3

  • Arcane TricksterA rogue who has added illusions and enchantment magic to their thieving kit.
  • AssassinA rogue who specializes in the prepared kill: disguise, poison and one decisive strike.
  • PhantomA rogue who has stepped beyond the line between life and death: empowers strikes with deathly power and steals knowledge from passing souls.
  • Scion of the ThreeRogues empowered by the Dead Three of Baldur's Gate — Bhaal, Bane and Myrkul: the embodiment of malice and dread.
  • SoulknifeA psionic rogue who manifests blades of pure thought and speaks to allies without words.
  • ThiefThe classic rogue: fast hands, fast feet and a knack for being where no one expects.

Sorcerer: sorcerer subclass, from level 3

  • Aberrant SorceryMagic that came from the Far Realm. The sorcerer's mind is open to what would better stay closed.
  • Clockwork SorceryThe magic of absolute order from the plane of Mechanus. The sorcerer evens out chance into predictability.
  • Draconic SorceryDragon blood runs in the sorcerer's veins: scales, wings and breath come naturally.
  • Shadow SorceryA sorcerer whose magic comes from the Shadowfell or another nexus of darkness: commands darkness, sorrow and undeath.
  • Spellfire SorcerySorcerers bound to the Weave itself through the rare gift of spellfire: they heal allies, burn foes and absorb spells.
  • Wild Magic SorceryMagic that can't be tamed. Every spell can turn into anything.

Warlock: warlock subclass, from level 3

  • Archfey PatronA pact with a lord of the Feywild: deceptive gifts, steps through the mist and charms that are hard to resist.
  • Celestial PatronA pact with a being of the Upper Planes. A healer warlock whose light burns the undead.
  • Fiend PatronA pact with a demon or devil: fire, dark luck and a bargain best forgotten.
  • Great Old One PatronA pact with an entity that may not even have noticed it was made. Magic of the mind and madness.
  • Undead PatronThe patron is a lich, a vampire or another mighty undead who has gone beyond the cycle of life and death.
  • Vestige PatronThe patron is a fading forgotten god who longs to regain its former might and shares the remains of its power with you.

Wizard: wizard subclass, from level 3

  • AbjurerA school of protection: shields, dispelling and barriers that other spells shatter against.
  • BladesingerA tradition of elven realms like Myth Drannor that combines wizardry, swordplay and dance.
  • ConjurerA wizard for whom distance and matter are flexible landmarks: they move in an instant and summon allies.
  • DivinerA school of foresight: the wizard reads the future and replaces others' rolls with ones they saw in advance.
  • EnchanterA wizard whose magic clouds and captivates minds.
  • EvokerA school of destructive magic. The wizard learns to shape a Fireball around allies.
  • IllusionistA school of deceiving the senses. The illusionist makes the unreal convincing enough to work.
  • NecromancerA wizard who controls the forces of life, death and undeath.
  • TransmuterA wizard who alters matter and energy: magic is the hammer in the forge of reality.

Artificer: artificer subclass, from level 3

  • AlchemistA specialist in mixing reagents: brews elixirs, heals allies and corrodes foes.
  • ArmorerTurns armor into a second skin: a dreadnaught, an infiltrator or a guardian with built-in weapons.
  • ArtilleristRains elements, missiles and explosions on the battlefield: creates an eldritch cannon and empowers spells through an arcane firearm.
  • Battle SmithA defender and medic: fights with a magic weapon alongside their steel defender.
  • CartographerA navigator and scout: magical maps link the party, illuminate enemies and open portals.
  • ReanimatorAn artificer who has turned necromancy into a gruesome science: stitches servants together from scattered bodies and empowers the living with foul magic.
