Cleric: War Domain — Spirit Guardians in Heavy Armor

Tank and healer: heavy armor and a shield, Spirit Guardians around you and extra attacks.

Why It’s Strong

  • The Protector Divine Order grants Heavy armor and Martial weapons at level 1.
  • The War Domain keeps Spiritual Weapon, Shield of Faith and Magic Weapon prepared, and from level 5 — Spirit Guardians.
  • Spirit Guardians is one of the best area damage sources: enemies near you take 3d8 every turn.
  • War Caster and Resilient keep your Concentration on the Guardians almost always.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: War Domain (from level 3).
  • Species: Dwarf.
  • Background: Farmer — Tough feat, bonuses: Wisdom +2, Constitution +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Wisdom 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Strength 13, Charisma 12, Intelligence 10, Dexterity 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Nature.
  • Equipment: Chain Mail, Shield, Warhammer, Holy Symbol, Priest’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: War Caster feat (+1 Wisdom)
  • Level 8: Wisdom +2
  • Level 12: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 16: Constitution +2
  • Level 19: Boon of Fortitude feat (+1 Constitution)

Level by Level

  • 1: Dwarf: +1 Hit Point per level, Resistance to Poison. The Farmer background gives the Tough feat (+2 Hit Points per level). Protector order: chain mail, shield, warhammer.
  • 3: War Domain: War Priest — an extra attack as a Bonus Action; Guided Strike for +10 to an attack. Always prepared: Guiding Bolt, Magic Weapon, Shield of Faith, Spiritual Weapon.
  • 4: War Caster (+1 Wisdom).
  • 5: Spirit Guardians (always prepared) and Crusader’s Mantle. Sear Undead.
  • 6: War God’s Blessing: Shield of Faith or Spiritual Weapon without a spell slot and without Concentration.
  • 7: Blessed Strikes: Divine Strike (+1d8 to a weapon hit once per turn) — this build fights with a hammer; a pure caster would prefer Potent Spellcasting.
  • 8: Wisdom 20.
  • 10: Divine Intervention: any cleric spell of level 5 or lower without a spell slot.
  • 12: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 17: Avatar of Battle: Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage.
  • 19: Boon of Fortitude: +40 Hit Points.

Spells

Cantrips: Toll the Dead, Guidance, Word of Radiance, Sacred Flame, Light.

Prepared (by priority): Bless, Healing Word, Command, Sanctuary, Cure Wounds, Aid, Hold Person, Lesser Restoration, Prayer of Healing, Revivify, Dispel Magic, Mass Healing Word, Spirit Shroud, Glyph of Warding, Banishment, Death Ward, Guardian of Faith, Aura of Life, Greater Restoration, Circle of Power, Mass Cure Wounds, Raise Dead, Heal, Blade Barrier, Regenerate, Conjure Celestial, Holy Aura, Mass Heal, Gate.

How to Play

  • Start of combat: Spirit Guardians, then charge into the thick of the enemies. As a Bonus Action — Spiritual Weapon or a War Priest attack.
  • Chain mail needs Strength 13 — this build has exactly 13. Plate (Strength 15) would slow you by 10 ft.: wear chain mail until you raise Strength.
  • Healing Word as a Bonus Action gets a fallen ally back up without spending your action.

Alternatives

  • Arcana Domain (Arcana Unleashed) — wizard spells; Knowledge Domain (Heroes of Faerûn); Grave Domain (Ravenloft).
  • Compatible options: Peace Domain and Twilight Domain (Tasha’s Cauldron) — with the DM’s approval.
  • Life Domain — the best healer: Disciple of Life and Aura of Life.
  • Light Domain — area damage: Fireball and Wall of Fire always prepared.
