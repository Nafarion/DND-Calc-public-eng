Paladin

D&D 2024 class. A warrior bound by an oath. The paladin combines heavy armor with divine magic, and their aura protects everyone nearby.

Class features

Level 1 Lay on Hands

You have a pool of healing power equal to five times your paladin level. As a bonus action you touch a creature to restore hit points from the pool, or spend 5 points to remove the Poisoned condition.

Level 1 Spellcasting

You cast paladin spells using Charisma as your spellcasting ability.

Level 1 Weapon Mastery

You can use the mastery properties of two kinds of weapons.

Level 2 Fighting Style

You gain a Fighting Style feat or the Blessed Warrior option (two cleric cantrips).

Fighting styles: Archery, Blind Fighting, Defense, Dueling, Great Weapon Fighting, Interception, Protection, Thrown Weapon Fighting, Two-Weapon Fighting, Unarmed Fighting, Arcane Warrior.

Level 2 Divine Smite

You always have Divine Smite prepared and can cast it once per Long Rest without expending a slot.

Level 3 Channel Divinity

You can channel divine energy in the ways your oath grants.

Level 3 Paladin Subclass

You swear a sacred oath that defines your path.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 5 Extra Attack

You attack twice instead of once when you take the Attack action.

Level 5 Faithful Steed

You always have Find Steed prepared and can cast it without a slot once per Long Rest.

Level 6 Aura of Protection

You and allies within 10 ft. gain a bonus to saving throws equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum +1).

Level 7 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen oath.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 9 Abjure Foes

By expending a use of Channel Divinity, you force creatures within 60 ft. (up to your Charisma modifier) to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure they're frightened for 1 minute and on their turn can either move or take an action or bonus action.

Level 10 Aura of Courage

You and allies within 10 ft. can't be frightened.

Level 11 Radiant Strikes

Your weapon attacks deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 14 Restoring Touch

With Lay On Hands you can also remove the Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed or Stunned condition.

Level 15 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen oath.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 18 Aura Expansion

The radius of your auras increases to 30 feet.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Epic Boons: Boon of Combat Prowess, Boon of Dimensional Travel, Boon of Energy Resistance, Boon of Fate, Boon of Fortitude, Boon of Irresistible Offense, Boon of Recovery, Boon of Skill, Boon of Speed, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Spell Recall, Boon of Truesight, Boon of Bloodshed, Boon of the Furious Storm, Boon of the Soul Drinker, Boon of the Bright Sun, Boon of Terror, Boon of Revelry, Boon of Poison Mastery, Boon of Fortune’s Favor, Boon of Exquisite Radiance, Boon of Fluid Forms, Boon of Desperate Resilience, Boon of Communication, Boon of Bountiful Health, Boon of Siberys, Boon of Erupting Spellpower, Boon of the Iron Mind, Boon of Magic School Mastery, Boon of Blazing Dawn, Boon of Looming Shadows, Boon of Misty Escape.

Level 20 Subclass feature

You gain the capstone feature of your chosen oath.

Subclasses: Paladin Subclass

  • Oath of DevotionThe classic paladin: honor, truth and protecting the weak without compromise.
  • Oath of GloryA paladin who strives for deeds worthy of a song. Glory isn't vanity but an obligation.
  • Oath of the AncientsA paladin of light and life who protects the world's beauty from the darkness that would snuff it out.
  • Oath of VengeanceA paladin who pursues the enemy to the end. Justice matters more than mercy.
  • Oath of the Noble GeniesForgotten Realms: Heroes of FaerûnPaladins who revere the powers of the four kinds of genies — dao, djinn, efreet and marids; many hail from Calimshan.
