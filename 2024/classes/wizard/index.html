Class features

Level 1 Spellcasting

You cast spells from your spellbook using Intelligence. When you gain a level, you write two new spells in your book.

Level 1 Ritual Adept

You can cast any spell in your book with the Ritual tag as a ritual, even if it isn't prepared.

Level 1 Arcane Recovery

After a Short Rest you regain spell slots with a combined level equal to half your wizard level (rounded up).

Level 2 Scholar

You gain Expertise in one skill: Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature or Religion.

Level 3 Wizard Subclass

You choose a school of magic for in-depth study.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 5 Memorize Spell

After a Short Rest you can replace one prepared spell with another from your book.

Level 18 Spell Mastery

Choose a 1st-level and a 2nd-level spell from your book: you can cast them at will without expending slots.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Level 20 Signature Spells

Choose two 3rd-level spells: they're always prepared and can each be cast once per Short Rest without a slot.

Subclasses: Wizard Subclass

  • AbjurerA school of protection: shields, dispelling and barriers that other spells shatter against.
  • DivinerA school of foresight: the wizard reads the future and replaces others' rolls with ones they saw in advance.
  • EvokerA school of destructive magic. The wizard learns to shape a Fireball around allies.
  • IllusionistA school of deceiving the senses. The illusionist makes the unreal convincing enough to work.
  • BladesingerForgotten Realms: Heroes of FaerûnA tradition of elven realms like Myth Drannor that combines wizardry, swordplay and dance.
  • NecromancerArcana UnleashedA wizard who controls the forces of life, death and undeath.
  • EnchanterArcana UnleashedA wizard whose magic clouds and captivates minds.
  • TransmuterArcana UnleashedA wizard who alters matter and energy: magic is the hammer in the forge of reality.
  • ConjurerArcana UnleashedA wizard for whom distance and matter are flexible landmarks: they move in an instant and summon allies.
