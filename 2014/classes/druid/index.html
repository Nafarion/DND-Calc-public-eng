Druid

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. A keeper of nature and its balance. The druid commands the elements and takes the shape of beasts. Druids don't wear metal armor or shields.

Class features

Level 1 Druidic

You know the secret language of druids and can leave hidden messages in it.

Level 1 Spellcasting

Each day you prepare spells from the druid list: your druid level + Wisdom modifier (minimum one). A druidic focus serves as your focus.

Level 2 Wild Shape

As an action you transform into a beast you've seen of CR 1/4 or lower without a flying or swimming speed (1/2 without flying from level 4, 1 from level 8) for hours equal to half your druid level. Twice per Short or Long Rest.

Level 2 Druid Circle

You join a druid circle.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Subclasses: Druid Circle

  • Circle of the LandMystic druids who keep ancient lore. The circle's land grants extra spells.
  • Circle of the MoonShapeshifter druids who guard the wild lands in the form of beasts.
  • Circle of SporesTasha's Cauldron of EverythingDruids who see beauty in decay: their spores choke their foes and raise the dead.
  • Circle of StarsTasha's Cauldron of EverythingDruids who read the stars: a star map and the shining form of constellations.
  • Circle of WildfireTasha's Cauldron of EverythingDruids who know that fire not only destroys but also gives rise to new life.
  • Circle of DreamsXanathar's Guide to EverythingDruids tied to the Feywild and the world of dreams: healers and guides.
  • Circle of the ShepherdXanathar's Guide to EverythingDruids who protect beasts and fey and call upon nature spirits.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 1 Expanded spells

New spells from Tasha's Cauldron of Everything are added to the druid spell list — you can choose them on the Spells step.

Level 2 Wild Companion

As an action, expend a use of Wild Shape to cast Find Familiar without material components; the familiar is a fey and lasts a number of hours equal to half your druid level.

Level 4 Cantrip Versatility

When you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace one cantrip of the class with another from its list.

Starting equipment

  • Shield, Scimitar, Leather Armor, Explorer's Pack, Druidic Focus
  • Starting wealth: about 50 GP
