Rogue (2014): Arcane Trickster with Booming Blade

Booming Blade from Tasha’s Cauldron adds damage to Sneak Attack, Mobile lets you leave without Opportunity Attacks, and Shield saves you from blows.

Why It’s Strong

  • Booming Blade: a rapier attack with extra Thunder damage (from level 5) and another 1d8–4d8 if the enemy moves.
  • Mobile: after you attack an enemy, it can’t make Opportunity Attacks against you as you leave — hit and run, leaving the enemy under the thunder.
  • War Caster (10): an Opportunity Attack with Booming Blade, and Sneak Attack works on another creature’s turn.
  • Shield, Misty Step, Tasha’s Mind Whip, and Steady Aim (Tasha’s Cauldron) for ranged combat.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Arcane Trickster (from level 3).
  • Race: Variant Human, Mobile feat.
  • Background: Criminal.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Dexterity 15 + 1 = 16, Constitution 14, Intelligence 13 + 1 = 14, Wisdom 12, Charisma 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Acrobatics (Expertise), Deception, Insight (Expertise), Investigation, Perception (Expertise), Sleight of Hand, Stealth (Expertise).
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Steady Aim.
  • Equipment: Leather Armor, Rapier, Shortbow, Arrows (20), Thieves’ Tools, Burglar’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Dexterity +2
  • Level 8: Dexterity +2
  • Level 10: War Caster feat
  • Level 12: Constitution +2
  • Level 16: Lucky feat
  • Level 19: Intelligence +2

Level by Level

  • 1: Variant Human: Dexterity +1, Intelligence +1, Mobile. Expertise: Stealth and Perception. Sneak Attack.
  • 2: Cunning Action.
  • 3: Arcane Trickster: Mage Hand, Booming Blade and Minor Illusion; Shield (any school), Tasha’s Hideous Laughter, Silent Image. Steady Aim (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 4: Dexterity 18.
  • 5: Uncanny Dodge; Booming Blade +1d8.
  • 6: Two more Expertises.
  • 7: Evasion; Tasha’s Mind Whip (enchantment).
  • 8: Dexterity 20; Misty Step (any school).
  • 9: Magical Ambush.
  • 10: War Caster; Green-Flame Blade.
  • 11: Reliable Talent.
  • 13: Versatile Trickster; Hypnotic Pattern.
  • 14: Haste (any school).
  • 17: Spell Thief.
  • 20: Stroke of Luck.

Spells

Cantrips: Mage Hand, Booming Blade, Minor Illusion, Green-Flame Blade.

Known (by priority): Shield, Tasha’s Hideous Laughter, Silent Image, Disguise Self, Tasha’s Mind Whip, Misty Step, Invisibility, Suggestion, Hypnotic Pattern, Haste, Mirror Image, Greater Invisibility, Major Image.

How to Play

  • Booming Blade with Sneak Attack, then step away without Opportunity Attacks (Mobile) — the enemy either stays put or takes the thunder.
  • At range — the shortbow with Steady Aim instead of Cunning Action.
  • Keep Shield for your Reaction; from level 10 your Reaction can also be an Opportunity Attack with a cantrip.

Alternatives

  • Inquisitive and Swashbuckler (Xanathar’s) — Sneak Attack without an ally nearby; Soulknife and Phantom (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • Race: bugbear (Monsters of the Multiverse) — reach and Surprise Attack.
  • Soulknife (Tasha’s Cauldron) — psychic blades and telepathy.
  • Phantom (Tasha’s Cauldron) — damage to a second target with Wails from the Grave.
