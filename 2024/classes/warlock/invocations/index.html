Eldritch Invocations: Warlock — D&D 2024

Invocations are fragments of forbidden knowledge that grant permanent abilities. In the 2024 rules, Pact of the Blade, Chain and Tome are invocations too. With each warlock level, you can replace one invocation with another.

Known: level 1: 1 · level 2: 3 · level 5: 5 · level 7: 6 · level 9: 7 · level 12: 8 · level 15: 9 · level 18: 10.

Create a character: Warlock

No level requirement

  • Armor of Shadows

    You can cast Mage Armor on yourself without expending a spell slot.

  • Eldritch Mind

    You have Advantage on Constitution saving throws to maintain Concentration.

  • Pact of the Blade

    As a Bonus Action, you conjure a pact weapon in your hand (a Simple or Martial Melee weapon of your choice) or create a bond with a magic weapon you touch. While the bond lasts, you're proficient with the weapon and use it as a Spellcasting Focus; you attack with it using your Charisma modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity and can deal Necrotic, Psychic or Radiant damage with it. The bond ends if you use this feature again, if the weapon is more than 5 feet from you for over a minute, or if you die.

  • Pact of the Chain

    You learn Find Familiar and can cast it as a Magic action without a spell slot. The familiar can also take a special form: imp, quasit, pseudodragon, skeleton, slaad tadpole, sprite, sphinx of wonder or venomous snake. When you take the Attack action, you can forgo one of your attacks to let your familiar attack with its Reaction.

  • Pact of the Tome

    At the end of a Short or Long Rest, you conjure a Book of Shadows. Choose three cantrips and two level 1 spells with the Ritual tag from any classes' lists (not already prepared): while the book is on you, they're prepared and count as Warlock spells. The book serves as a Spellcasting Focus.

From level 2

  • Agonizing Blast

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock, a Warlock cantrip that deals damage

    Choose a damaging Warlock cantrip you know: you add your Charisma modifier to its damage rolls. You can take this more than once — for a different cantrip each time.

  • Devil's Sight

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock

    You can see normally in Dim Light and Darkness, both magical and nonmagical, within 120 feet.

  • Eldritch Spear

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock, a Warlock cantrip that deals damage

    Choose a damaging Warlock cantrip you know with a range of 10 feet or more: its range increases by 30 feet per Warlock level. You can take this more than once — for a different cantrip each time.

  • Fiendish Vigor

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock

    You can cast False Life on yourself without expending a spell slot. You don't roll for the Temporary Hit Points: you get the maximum.

  • Lessons of the First Ones

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock

    Knowledge from an ancient entity gives you one Origin feat of your choice. You can take this more than once — a different feat each time.

  • Mask of Many Faces

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock

    You can cast Disguise Self without expending a spell slot.

  • Misty Visions

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock

    You can cast Silent Image without expending a spell slot.

  • Otherworldly Leap

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock

    You can cast Jump on yourself without expending a spell slot.

  • Repelling Blast

    Prerequisite: Level 2+ Warlock, a Warlock cantrip that deals damage via an attack roll

    Choose a Warlock cantrip you know that deals damage with an attack roll: when you hit a creature of size Large or smaller with it, you can push it up to 10 feet away from you. You can take this more than once — for a different cantrip each time.

From level 5

  • Ascendant Step

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock

    You can cast Levitate on yourself without expending a spell slot.

  • Eldritch Smite

    Prerequisite: Level 5+ Warlock, Pact of the Blade invocation

    Once per turn, when you hit with your pact weapon, you can expend a Pact Magic spell slot: the target takes 1d8 Force damage plus 1d8 per slot level, and you can give a creature of size Huge or smaller the Prone condition.

  • Gaze of Two Minds

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock

    As a Bonus Action, you touch a willing creature and perceive through its senses until the end of your next turn; you can extend the link with a Bonus Action while you're on the same plane. You benefit from its special senses and, if you're within 60 feet of each other, can cast spells as if you were in its space.

  • Gift of the Depths

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock

    You can breathe underwater and gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed. Once per Long Rest, you can cast Water Breathing without a spell slot.

  • Investment of the Chain Master

    Prerequisite: Level 5+ Warlock, Pact of the Chain invocation

    Your familiar from Find Familiar gains a Fly Speed or Swim Speed of 40 feet; as a Bonus Action you command it to attack; its Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing damage can become Necrotic or Radiant; its saving throws use your spell save DC; as a Reaction you give it Resistance to damage it takes.

  • Master of Myriad Forms

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock

    You can cast Alter Self without expending a spell slot.

  • One with Shadows

    Prerequisite: 5th-level warlock

    While in Dim Light or Darkness, you can cast Invisibility on yourself without expending a spell slot.

  • Thirsting Blade

    Prerequisite: Level 5+ Warlock, Pact of the Blade invocation

    You gain Extra Attack for your pact weapon: with the Attack action, you attack with it twice.

From level 7

  • Whispers of the Grave

    Prerequisite: 7th-level warlock

    You can cast Speak with Dead without expending a spell slot.

From level 9

  • Gift of the Protectors

    Prerequisite: Level 9+ Warlock, Pact of the Tome invocation

    A page for names (equal to your Charisma modifier, minimum one) appears in your Book of Shadows. When a creature on that page drops to 0 Hit Points but isn't killed outright, it's left with 1 Hit Point instead; then the effect doesn't trigger again until you finish a Long Rest. As a Magic action, you can erase a name.

  • Lifedrinker

    Prerequisite: Level 9+ Warlock, Pact of the Blade invocation

    Once per turn, when you hit with your pact weapon, you deal an extra 1d6 Necrotic, Psychic or Radiant damage (your choice) and can spend a Hit Point Die to regain Hit Points equal to its roll + your Constitution modifier (minimum 1).

  • Visions of Distant Realms

    Prerequisite: 9th-level warlock

    You can cast Arcane Eye without expending a spell slot.

From level 12

  • Devouring Blade

    Prerequisite: Level 12+ Warlock, Thirsting Blade invocation

    The extra attack from Thirsting Blade gives you two attacks instead of one.

From level 15

  • Witch Sight

    Prerequisite: 15th-level warlock

    You gain Truesight within 30 feet.
