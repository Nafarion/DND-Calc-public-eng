D&D 2014 abilities and skills

Six abilities describe a character: how strong, agile, tough, smart, perceptive and charming they are. Skills are what they are trained in: proficiency in a skill adds your Proficiency Bonus to the check.

Abilities

  • StrengthPhysical might and athletic training.
  • DexterityAgility, reflexes and balance.
  • ConstitutionHealth, stamina and vital force.
  • IntelligenceMental acuity, memory and reasoning.
  • WisdomPerceptiveness, intuition and attunement to the world around you.
  • CharismaForce of personality, charm and confidence.

Scores and modifiers

You add the modifier, not the score, to d20 rolls: (score − 10) / 2, rounded down. A typical person has scores around 10–11; a level 1 hero has 8 to 15 before bonuses, and the usual maximum for a character is 20.

Checks, saving throws and DCs

An ability check is d20 + modifier (+ Proficiency Bonus if you are proficient in the skill or tool). If the total equals or beats the DC (Difficulty Class), you succeed. A saving throw works the same way against a danger: a spell, poison, a trap; each class is proficient in two saving throws.

Expertise doubles your Proficiency Bonus for a skill. The Proficiency Bonus is added to a roll only once, even if both a skill and a tool apply.
