The Infernal Machine of Lum the Mad

Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement)

Type
Wondrous items
Rarity
Artifact
Attunement
required
Source
Infernal Machine Rebuild

Properties

A Huge control console (about 5,500 lb.) with levers, dials and switches, practically immovable.

Silver Wire. The machine can be linked to an attuned user by a wire that works like the silver cord of astral projection: while it's intact, the user can use the machine's powers around themselves anywhere and can concentrate on its effects remotely. Only effects that can cut a silver cord can sever the wire; the user then dies instantly. The save DC of the machine's effects is 14 or your spell save DC, whichever is higher.

Conjuration. 1/day: conjure animals, summon lesser demons; 1/7 days: conjure minor elementals, conjure woodland beings, summon greater demon; 1/month: conjure elemental, conjure fey.

Wish. Once a month — the effect of wish with all its side effects. Each use gives a cumulative 10% chance of breakdown: 7 days of repairs and 500 gp in parts, after which the chance is 10% again.

Random Properties. After each long rest of the attuned user, the machine grants 1d4 + 1 beneficial and 1d4 + 1 detrimental properties (d100 on the table; the DM can roll detrimental ones secretly); they last until the end of the next long rest. "N times" — that many uses for that period.
