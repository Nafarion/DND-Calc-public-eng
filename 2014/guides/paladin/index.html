Paladin (2014): Oath of Vengeance with a Glaive and Spirit Shroud

Vow of Enmity grants Advantage, Great Weapon Master barely misses, and Spirit Shroud from Tasha’s Cauldron adds 1d8 to every hit.

Why It’s Strong

  • Vow of Enmity: Advantage on attacks against one enemy for a minute — the perfect partner for −5/+10.
  • The 2014 Divine Smite: spend a slot after a hit, any number of times per turn.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron: Spirit Shroud (+1d8 to every attack), Summon Celestial, Harness Divine Power — regain a slot for a Channel Divinity use.
  • Aura of Protection: your Charisma to all the party’s saving throws.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Oath of Vengeance (from level 3).
  • Race: Variant Human, Polearm Master feat.
  • Background: Noble.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Strength 15 + 1 = 16, Charisma 14 + 1 = 15, Constitution 13, Wisdom 12, Dexterity 10, Intelligence 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion.
  • Fighting Style: Great Weapon Fighting.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Additional Paladin Spells, Channel Divinity: Harness Divine Power, Martial Versatility, Fighting Style Options.
  • Equipment: Chain Mail, Glaive, Javelin ×5, Holy Symbol, Priest’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Strength +2
  • Level 8: Great Weapon Master feat
  • Level 12: Charisma +2
  • Level 16: Strength +2
  • Level 19: Charisma +2

Level by Level

  • 1: Variant Human: Strength +1, Charisma +1, Polearm Master. Divine Sense, Lay on Hands.
  • 2: Great Weapon Fighting style, Divine Smite.
  • 3: Oath of Vengeance: Vow of Enmity, Abjure Enemy. Harness Divine Power (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 4: Strength 18; Martial Versatility.
  • 5: Extra Attack.
  • 6: Aura of Protection.
  • 7: Relentless Avenger.
  • 8: Great Weapon Master.
  • 9: Spirit Shroud (Tasha’s Cauldron); Haste always prepared.
  • 11: Improved Divine Smite.
  • 12: Charisma +2.
  • 15: Soul of Vengeance.
  • 16: Strength 20.
  • 17: Summon Celestial (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 19: Charisma +2.
  • 20: Avenging Angel.

Spells

Prepared (by priority): Spirit Shroud, Bless, Shield of Faith, Find Steed, Aid, Revivify, Command, Cure Wounds, Heroism, Wrathful Smite, Magic Weapon, Lesser Restoration, Warding Bond, Crusader’s Mantle, Dispel Magic, Death Ward, Aura of Life, Aura of Purity, Summon Celestial, Circle of Power, Destructive Wave.

How to Play

  • Vow of Enmity as a Bonus Action, then attacks with −5/+10; from level 9, Spirit Shroud on your first turn.
  • Declare your smite on a Critical Hit.
  • Keep allies within 10 ft. — Aura of Protection matters more than your damage.

Alternatives

  • Oath of Conquest (Xanathar’s) — fear and control; Oath of the Watchers (Tasha’s Cauldron); Oathbreaker (Dungeon Master’s Guide) — for an evil paladin.
  • Gift of the Metallic Dragon / Gift of the Chromatic Dragon feats (Fizban’s).
  • Oath of Glory (Tasha’s Cauldron) — +10 ft. Speed for the party and Temporary Hit Points when you smite.
  • Oath of the Watchers (Tasha’s Cauldron) — your Proficiency Bonus added to the party’s Initiative.
