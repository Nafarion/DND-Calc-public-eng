All equipment · Weapons · Armor and shields · Tools · Adventuring gear and packs · Mounts and vehicles · Services and lifestyle

Things that are hard to adventure without: light, rope, food, water, healing, containers, ammunition and spellcasting focuses. Equipment packs cost less than buying everything separately.
