D&D 5e (2014 rules) subclasses

All 118 subclasses of the 2014 Player's Handbook and supplements, by class.

Barbarian: primal path, from level 3

  • Path of the Ancestral GuardianAncestral spirits rise to defend the barbarian and their allies.
  • Path of the BattleragerDwarven berserkers in spiked armor who throw their whole bodies into battle. In the Forgotten Realms — for dwarves only; the DM can lift the restriction.
  • Path of the BeastA bestial nature bursts out in rage: fangs, claws and a tail.
  • Path of the BerserkerRage for rage's sake: the berserker throws themselves into battle, heedless of the cost.
  • Path of the GiantWhile raging, the barbarian draws on the primal power of giants: growing in size and filling their weapon with an element.
  • Path of the Storm HeraldThe barbarian's rage becomes a storm: desert heat, sea thunderstorm or tundra frost.
  • Path of the Totem WarriorAnimal spirits guide the barbarian and grant them the powers of the totem.
  • Path of the ZealotA barbarian whose rage is a gift of a deity. The gods are in no hurry to let them leave the world of the living.
  • Path of Wild MagicA rage steeped in wild magic sets off unpredictable surges.

Bard: bard college, from level 3

  • College of CreationBards who hear the Song of Creation that made the world and craft things from it.
  • College of EloquenceOrators who persuade with words and logic — and undermine their enemies' confidence.
  • College of GlamourBards who learned the magic of the Feywild: their performances enthrall and inspire.
  • College of LoreScholar bards who gather knowledge and truths from every source.
  • College of SpiritsMedium bards who summon the spirits of legends and stories and turn their power into magic.
  • College of SwordsBlade performers: their performance is combat, and their weapon is the instrument.
  • College of ValorSkald bards who sing of heroes' deeds and fight alongside them.
  • College of WhispersSpy bards: they trade in secrets and instill fear.

Cleric: divine domain, from level 1

  • Arcana DomainGods who guard the secrets of magic: Azuth, Mystra, Corellon Larethian, the moon gods of Krynn.
  • Death DomainGods of death, undeath and negative energy: Myrkul, Chemosh, Wee Jas, as well as of murder, pain and disease — Bhaal, Loviatar, Talona. A Dungeon Master's Guide subclass for evil characters: it isn't in the set available by default — take it only with your DM's permission.
  • Forge DomainGods of craft and smithing. Their clerics forge weapons and armor for the righteous.
  • Grave DomainKeepers of the line between life and death: they lay undead to rest and keep others from dying before their time.
  • Knowledge DomainGods of learning, secrets and revelation. Clerics of this domain gather knowledge and read minds.
  • Life DomainGods of healing and vitality. Clerics of this domain are the finest healers.
  • Light DomainGods of the sun, fire and truth. Clerics of this domain burn their foes and drive back darkness.
  • Nature DomainGods of forests, beasts and fertility. Clerics of this domain command animals and plants.
  • Order DomainGods of law and order. Clerics of this domain lead their allies and subjugate their foes.
  • Peace DomainGods of harmony and friendship. Clerics of this domain bind their allies together.
  • Tempest DomainGods of thunder, sea and sky. Clerics of this domain call down lightning and thunderclaps upon their foes.
  • Trickery DomainGods of mischief, thievery and pranks. Clerics of this domain confound their foes with illusions.
  • Twilight DomainGods of twilight and sleep. Clerics of this domain watch over travelers in the dark.
  • War DomainGods of battle and valor. Clerics of this domain fight in the front ranks.

Druid: druid circle, from level 2

  • Circle of DreamsDruids tied to the Feywild and the world of dreams: healers and guides.
  • Circle of SporesDruids who see beauty in decay: their spores choke their foes and raise the dead.
  • Circle of StarsDruids who read the stars: a star map and the shining form of constellations.
  • Circle of the LandMystic druids who keep ancient lore. The circle's land grants extra spells.
  • Circle of the MoonShapeshifter druids who guard the wild lands in the form of beasts.
  • Circle of the ShepherdDruids who protect beasts and fey and call upon nature spirits.
  • Circle of WildfireDruids who know that fire not only destroys but also gives rise to new life.

Fighter: martial archetype, from level 3

  • Arcane ArcherAn elven tradition: arrows imbued with magic.
  • Battle MasterA tactician and master of maneuvers who turns combat into an art.
  • CavalierA rider and protector: draws enemies to themselves and shields allies.
  • ChampionA warrior who hones physical perfection and the deadliness of every blow.
  • Echo KnightA warrior who summons echoes of themselves from unrealized timelines and fights alongside them.
  • Eldritch KnightA warrior who complements weapon mastery with abjuration and evocation magic.
  • Psi WarriorA warrior who empowers strikes and defense with the power of the mind.
  • Purple Dragon Knight (Banneret)A warlord knight who inspires allies by personal example. Outside Cormyr, the archetype is called Banneret.
  • Rune KnightA warrior who empowers their gear with giant runes.
  • SamuraiA warrior of unbending will: their spirit won't let them retreat.

Monk: monastic tradition, from level 3

  • Way of MercyMasked monks who bring healing to the suffering and a swift end to their foes.
  • Way of ShadowNinja monks who serve shadow and stealth.
  • Way of the Ascendant DragonMonks who emulate dragons: draconic breath, spectral wings and an aura of draconic might.
  • Way of the Astral SelfMonks who summon their astral self — first the arms, then the visage and the body.
  • Way of the Drunken MasterA monk whose movements seem clumsy but are actually unpredictable.
  • Way of the Four ElementsMonks who channel ki into the powers of fire, water, air and earth.
  • Way of the KenseiA monk for whom a weapon is an extension of the body.
  • Way of the Long DeathMonks who study death and turn that knowledge into a deadly fighting style.
  • Way of the Open HandUnrivaled masters of unarmed combat.
  • Way of the Sun SoulA monk who turns ki into fiery beams.

Paladin: sacred oath, from level 3

  • Oath of ConquestPaladins who bring order through strength and fear.
  • Oath of DevotionThe ideal of the knight in shining armor: honor, justice, protecting the weak.
  • Oath of GloryPaladins who believe that heroic deeds lead to immortal glory.
  • Oath of RedemptionPaladins who believe anyone can be saved and that violence is the last resort.
  • Oath of the AncientsAn oath as old as the elves: to preserve the light, joy and life of the world against darkness.
  • Oath of the CrownAn oath to civilization, law and the sovereign: paladins are the guardians of order on the walls.
  • Oath of the WatchersPaladins who guard the mortal world against incursions from other planes.
  • Oath of VengeanceA vow to punish those who have committed grievous evil, at any cost.
  • OathbreakerA paladin who broke a sacred oath for dark desires or to serve evil; they must be evil and at least 3rd level. If the oathbreaker atones, the DM can restore their oath. A Dungeon Master's Guide subclass for evil characters: it isn't in the set available by default — take it only with your DM's permission.

Ranger: ranger archetype, from level 3

  • Beast MasterA ranger and a loyal beast who fight as one.
  • DrakewardenA ranger bonded to a draconic spirit in the form of a drake that grows along with them.
  • Fey WandererA ranger who has been to the Feywild and carries its glamour.
  • Gloom StalkerA hunter in the dark who can't be seen or heard.
  • Horizon WalkerA guardian of portals and a traveler between planes.
  • HunterA defender of civilization's borders against monsters.
  • Monster SlayerA hunter of vampires, dragons and other monsters.
  • SwarmkeeperA ranger bonded to a swarm of nature spirits — insects, birds or leaves.

Rogue: roguish archetype, from level 3

  • Arcane TricksterA rogue who enhances their agility with enchantment and illusion magic.
  • AssassinA master of poisons, disguise and the deadly first strike.
  • InquisitiveA master at uncovering lies and finding weak spots.
  • MastermindA strategist rogue: weaves intrigues and directs allies.
  • PhantomA rogue bound to death: whispers of the dead and wails from the grave.
  • ScoutA pathfinder rogue: the first to spot the enemy and the one who gets away unharmed.
  • SoulknifeA rogue who manifests blades of psychic energy.
  • SwashbucklerA fencer and duelist, charming and elusive.
  • ThiefA master of theft, burglary and infiltration.

Sorcerer: sorcerous origin, from level 1

  • Aberrant MindA sorcerer whose mind is touched by the alien power of the Far Realm or by psionics.
  • Clockwork SoulA sorcerer in whom flows the power of Mechanus — absolute order.
  • Divine SoulA sorcerer with divine power flowing through their veins.
  • Draconic BloodlineMagic that came from a dragon ancestor.
  • Lunar SorceryA sorcerer into whom (or into whose ancestor) the magic of their world's moon has entered — on Krynn these are the three moons of the gods of magic. The magic draws on a chosen phase: full moon, new moon or crescent moon.
  • Shadow MagicA sorcerer marked by the Shadowfell.
  • Storm SorceryA sorcerer in whom the power of elemental air rages.
  • Wild MagicChaotic magic that bursts out in unpredictable surges.

Warlock: otherworldly patron, from level 1

  • The ArchfeyA pact with a lord of the Feywild — capricious and powerful.
  • The CelestialA patron from the Upper Planes: an empyrean, a solar or a ki-rin.
  • The FathomlessA patron from the depths of the sea — a kraken, an ancient elemental or a forgotten god.
  • The FiendA pact with a being of the Lower Planes: a demon, a devil or another fiend.
  • The GenieThe patron is a mighty genie: a dao, djinni, efreeti or marid.
  • The Great Old OneA pact with an unfathomable entity from the depths of the Far Realm.
  • The HexbladeA patron from the Shadowfell, tied to a sentient cursed weapon.
  • The UndeadA pact with an immortal undead: Acererak, Kas the Bloody-Handed, Vlaakith, Azalin Rex.
  • The UndyingA patron who has defeated death: Vecna, the lich-queen Vlaakith, Larloch, the Shadow King.

Wizard: arcane tradition, from level 2

  • BladesingingA wizard who combines swordplay and magic in the dance of the blade.
  • Chronurgy MagicWizards who control the flow of time with dunamis. Dunamancy spells are added to their list.
  • Graviturgy MagicWizards who bend space and control gravity. Dunamancy spells are added to their list.
  • Order of ScribesA wizard whose spellbook awakens and becomes an ally.
  • School of AbjurationProtective mages who raise barriers, banish and dispel magic.
  • School of ConjurationMages who summon creatures and objects and move in the blink of an eye.
  • School of DivinationSeers who peer through the veil of space and time.
  • School of EnchantmentMages who bend the minds and wills of others.
  • School of EvocationMages who command energy: fire, cold, lightning and pure force.
  • School of IllusionMages who deceive the senses and the mind.
  • School of NecromancyMages who study the forces of life, death and undeath.
  • School of TransmutationMages who change the nature of things and living creatures.
  • War MagicA battle mage wizard who combines protection and destruction.

Artificer: artificer specialist, from level 3

  • AlchemistAn expert in chemical compounds: brews elixirs, heals allies and corrodes enemies.
  • ArmorerTurns armor into a magical suit: a guardian with thunder gauntlets or an infiltrator with a lightning launcher.
  • ArtilleristA master of destructive magic: creates a magical cannon and empowers spells through a rod or staff.
  • Battle SmithA protector and healer with a loyal steel defender at their side.
