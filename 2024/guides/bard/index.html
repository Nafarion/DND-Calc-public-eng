Bard: College of Lore — Control and Borrowed Magic

A support spellcaster who shuts enemies down with Hypnotic Pattern and from level 6 takes the best wizard and cleric spells.

Why It’s Strong

  • The College of Lore’s Magical Discoveries at level 6 give two spells from the cleric, druid or wizard lists — for example, Counterspell and Spirit Guardians.
  • From level 10, Magical Secrets lets you prepare spells from the entire cleric, druid and wizard lists.
  • Cutting Words spoils an enemy’s attack or check as a Reaction.
  • Bardic Inspiration recharges on a Short Rest from level 5, and Jack of All Trades works from level 2.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: College of Lore (from level 3).
  • Species: Human, Alert feat.
  • Background: Entertainer — Musician feat, bonuses: Charisma +2, Dexterity +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Charisma 15 + 2 = 17, Dexterity 14 + 1 = 15, Constitution 13, Wisdom 12, Intelligence 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Acrobatics, Arcana, Deception (Expertise), Insight, Investigation, Perception (Expertise), Performance, Persuasion (Expertise), Stealth (Expertise).
  • Equipment: Leather Armor, Rapier, Dagger ×2, Musical instrument, Entertainer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Fey-Touched feat (+1 Charisma)
  • Level 8: Charisma +2
  • Level 12: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 16: War Caster feat (+1 Wisdom)
  • Level 19: Boon of Spell Recall feat (+1 Charisma)

Level by Level

  • 1: Human: the Perception skill and the Alert feat (Proficiency Bonus to Initiative — act first and set up control). The Entertainer background gives the Musician feat: after a rest you hand out Heroic Inspiration to allies.
  • 2: Expertise (Persuasion and Perception), Jack of All Trades.
  • 3: College of Lore: three skills, Cutting Words.
  • 4: Fey-Touched (+1 Charisma): Misty Step and Bless once a day for free.
  • 5: Hypnotic Pattern — your main control spell for the next levels. Bardic Inspiration recharges on a Short Rest.
  • 6: Magical Discoveries: Counterspell and Spirit Guardians.
  • 8: Charisma 20.
  • 9: Two more Expertises.
  • 10: Magical Secrets: the wizard’s Wall of Force — the best answer to almost any threat.
  • 12: Resilient (Constitution) — Concentration saves with your Proficiency Bonus.
  • 14: Peerless Skill.
  • 16: War Caster — Advantage on Concentration saves.
  • 19: Boon of Spell Recall (Charisma 21).
  • 20: Words of Creation.

Spells

Cantrips: Vicious Mockery, Minor Illusion, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation.

Prepared (by priority): Healing Word, Dissonant Whispers, Faerie Fire, Tasha’s Hideous Laughter, Sleep, Hold Person, Invisibility, Heat Metal, Silence, Hypnotic Pattern, Counterspell, Spirit Guardians, Slow, Fear, Leomund’s Tiny Hut, Greater Invisibility, Polymorph, Dimension Door, Banishment, Wall of Force, Synaptic Static, Hold Monster, Animate Objects, Mass Suggestion, Otto’s Irresistible Dance, Forcecage, Teleport, Mind Blank, Power Word Stun, Foresight, Wish, True Polymorph.

How to Play

  • On your first turn, use area control (Hypnotic Pattern, Slow), then Vicious Mockery and Healing Word as a Bonus Action.
  • Save your Reaction for Cutting Words or Counterspell.
  • Don’t stand in the front line: a bard has light armor and few Hit Points.

Alternatives

  • College of the Moon (Heroes of Faerûn) — healing and support; compatible option: College of Eloquence (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • Arcana Unleashed feats: Enchantment Adept and Illusion Adept for a controller bard.
  • College of Valor — a bard with a weapon and shield.
  • College of Glamour — strong support with Temporary Hit Points.
