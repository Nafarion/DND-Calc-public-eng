Druid: Circle of the Moon — Shapeshifting Tank

In beast form it takes hits better than a fighter, and between transformations it’s a full spellcaster.

Why It’s Strong

  • In 2024 the Circle of the Moon gives your forms an AC of at least 13 + Wisdom and Temporary Hit Points equal to three times your druid level — every time you transform.
  • You can cast Circle Spells in beast form, and your Concentration isn’t broken.
  • The druid prepares spells from the whole list: Conjure Animals, Call Lightning, Polymorph, Wall of Stone.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Circle of the Moon (from level 3).
  • Species: Elf — Wood Elf.
  • Background: Guide — Magic Initiate (Druid) feat, bonuses: Wisdom +2, Constitution +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Wisdom 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Charisma 12, Intelligence 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Animal Handling, Insight, Perception, Stealth, Survival.
  • Equipment: Hide Armor, Shield, Quarterstaff, Druidic Focus, Explorer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: War Caster feat (+1 Wisdom)
  • Level 8: Wisdom +2
  • Level 12: Tough feat
  • Level 16: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 19: Boon of Fortitude feat (+1 Constitution)

Level by Level

  • 1: Wood Elf: Speed 35 ft., lineage spells at levels 3 and 5. Guide background: Magic Initiate (Druid) — Guidance, Resistance and Goodberry. Warden Primal Order: Medium armor and Martial weapons.
  • 2: Wild Shape and Wild Companion. Until level 3 you’re a regular spellcaster.
  • 3: Circle of the Moon: forms up to CR one-third of your druid level, AC 13 + Wisdom, Temporary Hit Points equal to 3 × level. Moonbeam and Starry Wisp always prepared.
  • 4: War Caster (+1 Wisdom).
  • 5: Conjure Animals always prepared; the wood elf’s Pass without Trace. Wild Resurgence — spell slots into Wild Shape uses and back.
  • 6: Improved Circle Forms: Radiant damage, Wisdom added to Constitution saves.
  • 8: Wisdom 20.
  • 10: Moonlight Step: teleport 30 ft. as a Bonus Action.
  • 12: Tough — another +2 Hit Points per level.
  • 14: Lunar Form: +2d10 Radiant once per turn.
  • 16: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 18: Beast Spells.

Spells

Cantrips: Produce Flame, Thorn Whip, Shillelagh, Druidcraft.

Prepared (by priority): Healing Word, Entangle, Faerie Fire, Thunderwave, Speak with Animals, Spike Growth, Heat Metal, Summon Beast, Lesser Restoration, Call Lightning, Dispel Magic, Revivify, Summon Fey, Plant Growth, Polymorph, Conjure Woodland Beings, Summon Elemental, Freedom of Movement, Wall of Stone, Greater Restoration, Conjure Elemental, Awaken, Heal, Sunbeam, Regenerate, Fire Storm, Animal Shapes, Sunburst, Shapechange, Foresight.

How to Play

  • Cast a Concentration spell (Moonbeam, Call Lightning), then transform — the spell keeps going.
  • Your form’s Temporary Hit Points refresh every time you transform: use Wild Shape often.
  • Druids don’t wear metal armor — hide armor and a wooden shield.

Alternatives

  • Compatible options: Circle of the Shepherd (Xanathar’s) and Circle of Spores (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • Species from Forge of the Artificer (shifter, khoravar) and Ravenloft (lupin) — alternatives to the wood elf.
  • Circle of the Stars — an all-round caster: Archer adds damage every turn, Dragon protects Concentration.
  • Circle of the Land (temperate) — extra Misty Step and Lightning Bolt.
