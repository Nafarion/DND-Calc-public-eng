Privacy Policy

Version of October 27, 2026

1. General Provisions

1.1. This Policy sets out the procedure and conditions for processing the personal data of users of the websites d20hero.ru and d20hero.online and their subdomains (hereinafter, the site).

1.2. The personal data operator is the owner of the D20Hero site (hereinafter, the operator). You can contact the operator through the “Contact us” form at the bottom of any page of the site.

1.3. The character builder and the reference can be used without registration. In that case, characters, the chosen edition of the reference and settings are stored only in the browser's memory (localStorage) on the user's device and are not transmitted to the operator.

1.4. The operator does not process special categories of personal data or biometric personal data. The operator does not make decisions that have legal consequences for the user based solely on automated processing.

2. Data, Purposes and Legal Basis for Processing

2.1. Registration and sign-in.

Data: email address, username, password (stored only as a hash; the operator does not know the password itself).
Purposes: creating an account, signing in, confirming the email address, restoring access.
Legal basis: consent.

2.2. Sign-in through an external authorization service.

Data: the user ID in the service, name, email address and profile picture, to the extent permitted by the user.
Purposes: creating an account and signing in without a password.
Legal basis: consent.

2.3. Characters in the account.

Data: information entered in the character sheet, including the player name and notes.
Purposes: storing characters and accessing them from different devices.
Legal basis: consent.

2.4. The “Share” feature.

Data: a copy of the character sheet.
Purposes: creating a short link to the sheet.
Legal basis: consent.

A sheet published through the “Share” feature is available to anyone who receives the link. The user decides what information to place in such a sheet. It is not recommended to include personal data of third parties in it.

2.5. The “Contact us” form.

Data: the message text, contact details for a reply (if provided), the page address.
Purposes: replying to the message, fixing errors.
Legal basis: consent expressed by sending the message.

2.6. Requests to the account server.

Data: IP address, date and time of the request.
Purposes: protection against password guessing and spam, keeping error logs.
Legal basis: consent.

2.7. Visiting the site.

Data: cookies, IP address, information about the browser and device, addresses of the pages viewed, actions on the page.
Purposes: visit statistics, detecting errors and inconvenient interface elements.
Legal basis: consent expressed in the cookie banner (section 7).

2.8. The “I am not a robot” check.

Data: information about the browser and actions on the check page.
Purposes: protecting registration, access recovery and the contact form from automated actions.
Legal basis: consent.

3. Actions with Personal Data

The operator carries out automated processing: collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access), blocking, deletion, destruction.

4. Entrusting Processing and Transfer to Third Parties

4.1. The operator does not sell personal data and does not transfer it for advertising purposes.

4.2. The operator may entrust the processing of personal data to persons without whose services the site cannot operate:

  • hosting service providers;
  • the web analytics service provider;
  • the provider of the service protecting against automated actions;
  • authorization service providers;
  • the email delivery service provider.

Such persons process data only to the extent necessary to provide their services. They are obliged to maintain confidentiality and comply with applicable personal data protection law.

4.3. Personal data may be provided to government authorities only in cases expressly provided for by law.

5. Place and Periods of Storage

5.1. The account server is located in the territory of the Russian Federation. It stores accounts, characters, short links and messages.

5.2. Storage periods:

  • account and characters — until the account is deleted; when an account is deleted, its characters are deleted as well;
  • short links to sheets — until the user requests deletion or until the account is deleted;
  • messages sent through the form — until a reply is given, but no longer than one year;
  • database backups — no longer than seven days;
  • web analytics data — until consent is withdrawn, but no longer than the period set by the rules of the web analytics service.

5.3. Data is destroyed within a period not exceeding 30 days in the following cases: when the purposes of processing are achieved, when consent is withdrawn, or at the user's request. A different period applies if it is required by law.

6. Security Measures

  • the site and the account server work only over HTTPS;
  • passwords are stored as a hash;
  • each user has access only to their own characters; only the operator has access to the database;
  • the number of sign-in attempts and other requests is limited;
  • the web analytics service is configured so that it does not record the contents of the email, password, username and message input fields.

7. Cookies and Web Analytics

7.1. The site uses cookies of a web analytics service to count visits and analyze errors.

7.2. You can withdraw this consent by deleting the site's cookies and blocking them in your browser settings, or through the “Contact us” form. Refusing analytics cookies does not limit the site's functionality.

8. User Rights

The user has the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of their personal data;
  • demand that data be clarified, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or not necessary for the stated purpose; the username and characters can be changed in the account page;
  • delete the account with all characters and withdraw consent;
  • appeal against the actions or inaction of the operator to the competent data protection authority or in court.

A request is sent through the “Contact us” form, stating the email address or username of the account. The operator replies within 10 working days from the date the request is received. The period may be extended by no more than 5 working days with a reasoned notice.

9. Changes to the Policy

The operator may change the Policy. A new version takes effect from the moment it is published on this page. The date of the version is stated at the beginning of the document.
