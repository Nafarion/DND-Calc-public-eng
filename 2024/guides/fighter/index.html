Fighter: Battle Master with a Greatsword

The most attacks, the most feats, and maneuvers that solve almost any problem in combat.

Why It’s Strong

  • The most Ability Score Improvements: feats at levels 4, 6, 8, 12, 14, 16 and an Epic Boon.
  • Action Surge plus Great Weapon Master — the strongest burst damage in the game.
  • Battle Master maneuvers: Menacing Attack, Trip Attack, Precision Attack — control and accuracy for a Superiority Die.
  • Six weapons with Weapon Mastery by level 16 and Tactical Master at level 9: Push, Sap or Slow on any weapon.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Battle Master (from level 3).
  • Species: Human, Tough feat.
  • Background: Soldier — Savage Attacker feat, bonuses: Strength +2, Constitution +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Strength 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 12, Intelligence 10, Charisma 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Acrobatics, Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, Survival.
  • Weapon Mastery: Greatsword, Maul, Longbow, Halberd, Javelin, Handaxe (as you gain them).
  • Fighting Style: Great Weapon Fighting.
  • Equipment: Chain Mail, Greatsword, Maul, Longbow, Arrows (20), Quiver, Dungeoneer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Great Weapon Master feat (+1 Strength)
  • Level 6: Strength +2
  • Level 8: Lucky feat
  • Level 12: Resilient feat (+1 Wisdom)
  • Level 14: Constitution +2
  • Level 16: Durable feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 19: Boon of Combat Prowess feat (+1 Strength)

Level by Level

  • 1: Human: Tough (+2 Hit Points per level) and a skill. Soldier background: Savage Attacker — reroll weapon damage once per turn. Great Weapon Fighting style. Weapon Mastery: greatsword (Graze), maul (Topple), longbow (Slow).
  • 2: Action Surge, Tactical Mind.
  • 3: Battle Master: 3 maneuvers (Menacing Attack, Trip Attack, Precision Attack), four d8 Superiority Dice. Student of War: a skill and Smith’s Tools.
  • 4: Great Weapon Master (+1 Strength).
  • 5: Extra Attack, Tactical Shift.
  • 6: Strength 20.
  • 7: 2 more maneuvers (Parry, Maneuvering Attack), a fifth die.
  • 8: Lucky — Luck Points equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
  • 9: Indomitable and Tactical Master.
  • 10: d10 Superiority Dice; 2 more maneuvers (Riposte, Commander’s Strike).
  • 11: Three attacks per Attack action.
  • 12: Resilient (Wisdom) — protection against charm and fear.
  • 13: Studied Attacks, a second use of Indomitable.
  • 15: Relentless, a sixth die, 2 more maneuvers (Sweeping Attack, Rally).
  • 18: d12 Superiority Dice.
  • 19: Boon of Combat Prowess (Strength 21): turn a miss into a hit once per turn.
  • 20: Four attacks per Attack action.

How to Play

  • Nova turn: Action Surge, every attack with Great Weapon Master and a Trip Attack on the first one — the rest go with Advantage.
  • The maul with Topple against strong single foes, the greatsword with Graze when it’s hard to hit.
  • You can wear plate (Strength 15) right away: this build has Strength 17.

Alternatives

  • Banneret (Heroes of Faerûn) — party support; Arcane Archer (Arcana Unleashed); compatible options — Echo Knight (Wildemount) and Rune Knight (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • Champion — simpler to play, Critical Hits on 18–20 and Heroic Inspiration every turn from level 10.
  • Psi Warrior — protecting allies and telekinesis.
