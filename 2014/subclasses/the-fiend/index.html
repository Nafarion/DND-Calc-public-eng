The Fiend

A subclass of the Warlock class (otherworldly patron), D&D 5e (2014 rules). Chosen at level 1. A pact with a being of the Lower Planes: a demon, a devil or another fiend.

Create a character: Warlock — The Fiend

Subclass features

Level 1 Dark One's Blessing

When you reduce an enemy to 0 hit points, gain temporary hit points: your Charisma modifier + warlock level.

Level 6 Dark One's Own Luck

Add 1d10 to an ability check or saving throw. Once per short or long rest.

Level 10 Fiendish Resilience

After a rest, choose a damage type — until the next rest you have resistance to it (except from magical and silvered weapons).

Level 14 Hurl Through Hell

When you hit with an attack, send the target through the Lower Planes until the end of your next turn: when it returns, it takes 10d10 psychic damage. Once per long rest.
