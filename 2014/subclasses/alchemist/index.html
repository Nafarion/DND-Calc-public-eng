Alchemist

A subclass of the Artificer class (artificer specialist), D&D 5e (2014 rules). Chosen at level 3. An expert in chemical compounds: brews elixirs, heals allies and corrodes enemies. Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything.

Create a character: Artificer — Alchemist

Subclass features

Level 3 Tool Proficiency

Proficiency with alchemist's supplies (if you already have it — other artisan's tools of your choice).

Level 3 Experimental Elixir

After a long rest you create an elixir (two from 6th level, three from 15th), its effect determined by a d6: healing (2d4 + Intelligence hit points), swiftness (+10 feet of speed for 1 hour), resilience (+1 to AC for 10 minutes), boldness (+1d4 to attacks and saving throws for 1 minute), flight (flying speed 10 feet for 10 minutes) or transformation (as alter self for 10 minutes). As an action, for a spell slot, you can create another elixir with an effect of your choice.

Level 5 Alchemical Savant

When you cast a spell using alchemist's supplies, add your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1) to one roll of healing or acid, fire, necrotic or poison damage.

Level 9 Restorative Reagents

Your elixirs grant 2d6 + Intelligence temporary hit points. You cast lesser restoration without a slot using alchemist's supplies. Number of times — your Intelligence modifier per long rest.

Level 15 Chemical Mastery

Resistance to acid and poison damage, immunity to being poisoned. Without a slot or material components you cast greater restoration and heal — each once per long rest.

Subclass spells

Always prepared; they don't count against your prepared spells.
