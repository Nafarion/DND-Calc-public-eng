Circle of the Land

A subclass of the Druid class (druid circle), D&D 5e (2014 rules). Chosen at level 2. Mystic druids who keep ancient lore. The circle's land grants extra spells.

Create a character: Druid — Circle of the Land

Subclass features

Level 2 Bonus Cantrip

You know one more druid cantrip.

Level 2 Natural Recovery

Once per day after a short rest, recover spell slots with a combined level up to half your druid level.

Level 3 Circle Spells

Choose a land (arctic, coast, desert, forest, grassland, mountain, swamp, Underdark): at 3rd, 5th, 7th and 9th level you gain two always-prepared spells of that land.

Level 6 Land's Stride

Nonmagical difficult terrain doesn't slow you, plants don't harm you; advantage on saving throws against magical plants.

Level 10 Nature's Ward

Immunity to poison and disease; elementals and fey can't charm or frighten you.

Level 14 Nature's Sanctuary

Beasts and plants that attack you make a Wisdom saving throw, or choose another target.

Circle Land: spells

The spells of the chosen land are always prepared and don't count against your prepared spells.
