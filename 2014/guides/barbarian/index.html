Barbarian (2014): Bear Totem Warrior with Great Weapon Master

Even after Tasha’s Cauldron, the Bear is the toughest barbarian: while raging, you resist all damage except Psychic.

Why It’s Strong

  • Bear Totem: while raging you have Resistance to all damage except Psychic — effectively double Hit Points. Tasha’s subclasses (Path of the Beast, Path of Wild Magic) are strong, but they don’t soak damage like this.
  • A variant human takes Great Weapon Master at level 1: Reckless Attack grants Advantage and nearly cancels the −5 penalty.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron: Primal Knowledge — two barbarian skills (levels 3 and 10); Instinctive Pounce — move half your speed when you enter a rage.
  • Wolf Totemic Attunement (14) knocks an enemy prone as a Bonus Action; Primal Champion (20) — Strength and Constitution 24.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Path of the Totem Warrior (from level 3).
  • Race: Variant Human, Great Weapon Master feat.
  • Background: Outlander.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Strength 15 + 1 = 16, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13, Wisdom 12, Charisma 10, Intelligence 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, Survival.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Primal Knowledge, Instinctive Pounce.
  • Equipment: Greatsword, Handaxe ×2, Javelin ×4, Explorer’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Strength +2
  • Level 8: Strength +2
  • Level 12: Constitution +2
  • Level 16: Constitution +2
  • Level 19: Constitution +1, Dexterity +1

Level by Level

  • 1: Variant Human: Strength +1, Constitution +1, Stealth and Great Weapon Master. Rage, Unarmored Defense.
  • 2: Reckless Attack and Danger Sense.
  • 3: Totem Spirit: Bear. Primal Knowledge (Tasha’s Cauldron): Animal Handling.
  • 4: Strength 18.
  • 5: Extra Attack, Fast Movement.
  • 6: Aspect of the Beast: Eagle (sight up to a mile); Bear if carrying capacity matters more.
  • 7: Feral Instinct; Instinctive Pounce (Tasha’s Cauldron).
  • 8: Strength 20.
  • 9: Brutal Critical.
  • 10: Primal Knowledge: Nature.
  • 11: Relentless Rage.
  • 12: Constitution +2.
  • 14: Totemic Attunement: Wolf.
  • 16: Constitution +2.
  • 19: Constitution +1, Dexterity +1.
  • 20: Primal Champion: Strength 24, Constitution 24.

How to Play

  • Rage as a Bonus Action with Instinctive Pounce — straight to the enemy, then a Reckless Attack with −5/+10.
  • Against high AC, skip the −5/+10.
  • Killed an enemy or scored a Critical Hit — one more attack as a Bonus Action.

Alternatives

  • Path of the Beast (Tasha’s Cauldron) — more claw attacks and healing bites, but no resistance to all damage.
  • Custom Lineage (Tasha’s Cauldron): Strength 17 and Great Weapon Master at level 1.
  • Path of the Zealot (Xanathar’s) — extra damage every turn and near-immortality at high levels; Path of the Ancestral Guardian — the best protector of allies.
  • Path of the Giant (Bigby’s) — reach and Strength-based weapon throws; Tiger and Elk totems (Sword Coast) — mobile totems.
  • A race from Monsters of the Multiverse (goliath, bugbear, minotaur) — flexible bonuses and useful traits instead of the human’s feat.
