Monk

D&D 2024 class. A fighter who has turned their own body into a weapon. The monk spends focus on supernatural techniques and strikes faster than the eye can follow.

Class features

Level 1 Martial Arts

Without armor or a shield, you can use Dexterity for attacks with unarmed strikes and monk weapons, deal damage with your Martial Arts die (1d6, growing with your level) and gain an unarmed strike as a bonus action.

Level 1 Unarmored Defense

While you wear no armor and no shield, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Wisdom modifier.

Level 2 Monk's Focus

You gain focus points equal to your monk level and can spend them on techniques: Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind.

Level 2 Unarmored Movement

Your speed increases by 10 ft. without armor or a shield; the bonus grows with your level.

Level 2 Uncanny Metabolism

When you roll Initiative, you regain all focus points and hit points equal to your monk level + your Martial Arts die. Once per Long Rest.

Level 3 Monk Subclass

You choose a monastic tradition.

Level 3 Deflect Attacks

As a reaction you reduce the damage from a bludgeoning, piercing or slashing attack by 1d10 + your Dexterity modifier + your monk level. If you reduce the damage to zero, you can spend a focus point to throw the missile back.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 4 Slow Fall

As a reaction you reduce falling damage by five times your monk level.

Level 5 Extra Attack

You attack twice instead of once when you take the Attack action.

Level 5 Stunning Strike

When you hit with an attack, you can spend a focus point: the target makes a Constitution saving throw or is stunned until the start of your next turn; on a success its speed is halved and the next attack against it has Advantage. Once per turn.

Level 6 Empowered Strikes

Your unarmed strikes deal force damage.

Level 6 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen tradition.

Level 7 Evasion

When you make a Dexterity saving throw to take half damage, you take no damage on a success and half on a failure.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 9 Acrobatic Movement

You can move along vertical surfaces and across liquids without falling.

Level 10 Heightened Focus

Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense and Step of the Wind become stronger.

Level 10 Self-Restoration

At the end of each of your turns, you can end one condition on yourself: Charmed, Frightened or Poisoned. Going without food and water doesn't give you exhaustion.

Level 11 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen tradition.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 13 Deflect Energy

Deflect Attacks now works against any damage type.

Level 14 Disciplined Survivor

You gain proficiency in all saving throws and can spend focus to reroll.

Level 15 Perfect Focus

When you roll Initiative with 3 or fewer focus points, you regain focus points up to 4.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a general feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all General feats (96) →

Level 17 Subclass feature

You gain a feature from your chosen tradition.

Level 18 Superior Defense

At the start of your turn you can spend 3 focus points to gain resistance to all damage except force for 1 minute.

Level 19 Epic Boon

You gain an Epic Boon feat or another Ability Score Improvement of your choice.

Epic Boons: Boon of Combat Prowess, Boon of Dimensional Travel, Boon of Energy Resistance, Boon of Fate, Boon of Fortitude, Boon of Irresistible Offense, Boon of Recovery, Boon of Skill, Boon of Speed, Boon of the Night Spirit, Boon of Spell Recall, Boon of Truesight, Boon of Bloodshed, Boon of the Furious Storm, Boon of the Soul Drinker, Boon of the Bright Sun, Boon of Terror, Boon of Revelry, Boon of Poison Mastery, Boon of Fortune’s Favor, Boon of Exquisite Radiance, Boon of Fluid Forms, Boon of Desperate Resilience, Boon of Communication, Boon of Bountiful Health, Boon of Siberys, Boon of Erupting Spellpower, Boon of the Iron Mind, Boon of Magic School Mastery, Boon of Blazing Dawn, Boon of Looming Shadows, Boon of Misty Escape.

Level 20 Body and Mind

Your Dexterity and Wisdom increase by 4, and their maximum becomes 25.

Subclasses: Monk Subclass

  • Warrior of MercyA monk whose hands can heal and end a life equally well.
  • Warrior of ShadowAn infiltrator monk who melts into darkness and strikes from where no one expects.
  • Warrior of the ElementsA monk who channels Focus into the elements: fire, ice, wind and stone obey their movements.
  • Warrior of the Open HandThe classic school of unarmed combat, where an Unarmed Strike is deadlier than any blade.
  • Warrior of the Mystic ArtsArcana UnleashedMonks who have raised their focus on inner power to magic: Sorcerer spells combined with martial arts.
