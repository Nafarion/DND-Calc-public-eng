Warlock (2014): Efreeti Genie

Genie’s Wrath adds your Proficiency Bonus to damage, the efreeti expanded list gives Fireball and Fire Shield, and at level 17 — Wish.

Why It’s Strong

  • Genie’s Wrath: once per turn, + Proficiency Bonus Fire damage on a hit — on Eldritch Blast with Agonizing Blast.
  • The efreeti and genie expanded list: Burning Hands, Scorching Ray, Fireball, Fire Shield, Flame Strike, Creation — and Wish for your 9th-level arcanum.
  • Bottled Respite and Elemental Gift (30-ft. flight, Resistance to Fire).
  • Tasha’s Cauldron: Tasha’s Otherworldly Guise for your 6th-level arcanum, Summon Fey and Summon Aberration.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: The Genie (from level 1).
  • Race: Half-Elf.
  • Background: Charlatan.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Charisma 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Dexterity 13 + 1 = 14, Wisdom 12, Intelligence 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Arcana, Deception, Intimidation, Perception, Sleight of Hand, Stealth.
  • Tasha’s Cauldron optional features: Additional Warlock Spells, Eldritch Invocation Options and Pact of the Talisman, Eldritch Versatility.
  • Equipment: Leather Armor, Light Crossbow, Bolts (20), Arcane Focus, Dagger ×2, Scholar’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Charisma +1, Constitution +1
  • Level 8: Charisma +2
  • Level 12: War Caster feat
  • Level 16: Resilient feat (+1 Constitution)
  • Level 19: Lucky feat

Level by Level

  • 1: Half-Elf: Charisma +2, Constitution +1, Dexterity +1. The Genie: genie kind — efreeti; Genie’s Vessel (Bottled Respite, Genie’s Wrath). Hex, Armor of Agathys.
  • 2: Invocations: Agonizing Blast, Repelling Blast.
  • 3: Pact of the Talisman (Tasha’s Cauldron): +d4 to a failed ability check.
  • 4: Charisma +1, Constitution +1; Eldritch Versatility.
  • 5: Fireball, Counterspell; the Eldritch Mind invocation (Advantage on Concentration saves).
  • 6: Elemental Gift: Resistance to Fire, flight.
  • 7: The Devil’s Sight invocation: see in magical and normal darkness.
  • 8: Charisma 20.
  • 9: The Protection of the Talisman invocation: +d4 to a failed saving throw.
  • 10: Sanctuary Vessel.
  • 11: Arcanum: Tasha’s Otherworldly Guise.
  • 12: War Caster; the Bond of the Talisman invocation.
  • 13: Arcanum: Forcecage.
  • 14: Limited Wish.
  • 15: Arcanum: Power Word Stun; the Witch Sight invocation.
  • 16: Resilient (Constitution).
  • 17: Arcanum: Wish (genie expanded list).
  • 18: The Rebuke of the Talisman invocation.
  • 19: Lucky.

Spells

Cantrips: Eldritch Blast, Minor Illusion, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation.

Known (by priority): Hex, Armor of Agathys, Burning Hands, Misty Step, Scorching Ray, Hold Person, Fireball, Counterspell, Summon Fey, Hunger of Hadar, Fire Shield, Banishment, Summon Aberration, Hold Monster, Flame Strike, Dimension Door, Scrying.

Mystic Arcanum: 6th — Tasha’s Otherworldly Guise, 7th — Forcecage, 8th — Power Word Stun, 9th — Wish.

How to Play

  • Eldritch Blast every turn; Genie’s Wrath on the first beam that hits.
  • Elemental Gift: fly over the battlefield, out of melee fighters’ reach.
  • Bottled Respite — a safe Short Rest to regain your slots.

Alternatives

  • The Hexblade (Xanathar’s) — a Charisma-based warlock fighter; The Fathomless (Tasha’s Cauldron); The Undead (Ravenloft).
  • A Mordenkainen’s Tome of Foes tiefling (Levistus — Armor of Agathys) — with your DM’s approval.
  • A dao or marid genie — different spells and damage type.
  • The Fiend — Dark One’s Blessing and Fireball without choosing a genie kind.
