Properties

A Huge control console (about 5,510 lb.) with levers, dials and switches, practically immovable. The machine was originally a planar vessel’s control console, recovered from a crash in the Barrier Peaks.

Silver Wire. The machine can be linked to an attuned user by a wire drawn from the machine. Once attached, the wire is invisible, astral and virtually endless, like the silver cord of Astral Projection. While it is intact, the user can use the machine's powers around themselves anywhere and can concentrate on its effects remotely. Only effects that can cut a silver cord can sever the wire; the user then dies instantly. The save DC of the machine's effects is 14 or your spell save DC, whichever is higher.

Conjuration. 1/day: conjure animals, summon lesser demons; 1/7 days: conjure minor elementals, conjure woodland beings, summon greater demon; 1/month: conjure elemental, conjure fey.

Wish. Once a month — the effect of wish with all its side effects. Each use gives a cumulative 10% chance of breakdown: 7 days of repairs and 500 gp in parts, after which the chance is 10% again.

Random Properties. After each long rest of the attuned user, the machine grants 1d4 + 1 beneficial and 1d4 + 1 detrimental properties (roll separately on the d100 table for each property, with the DM free to roll detrimental properties secretly); they last until the end of the next long rest. "N times" — that many uses for that period.
