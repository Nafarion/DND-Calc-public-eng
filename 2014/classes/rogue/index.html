Rogue

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. A master of stealth, agility and the precise strike. The rogue finds the weak spot — in an enemy's defense, a lock or someone else's pocket.

Class features

Level 1 Expertise

Choose two skills, or a skill and thieves' tools — your Proficiency Bonus is doubled for them. Two more at level 6.

Level 1 Sneak Attack

Once per turn, when you attack with a finesse or ranged weapon with Advantage (or with an ally next to the target), deal +1d6 damage. The damage grows by 1d6 every two levels (10d6 at level 19).

Level 1 Thieves' Cant

You know the secret language and signs of thieves.

Level 2 Cunning Action

Dash, Disengage or Hide as a bonus action.

Level 3 Roguish Archetype

You choose a roguish archetype.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 5 Uncanny Dodge

As a reaction you halve the damage from an attack you can see.

Level 6 Expertise

Two more skills (or a skill and thieves' tools) get double your Proficiency Bonus.

Level 7 Evasion

When you make a Dexterity saving throw for half damage, you take no damage on a success and half on a failure.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Roguish Archetype).

Level 11 Reliable Talent

Level 18 Elusive

Attacks against you don't gain Advantage while you aren't incapacitated.

Level 20 Stroke of Luck

You can turn a miss into a hit, or a failed check into a 20. Once per Short or Long Rest.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 3 Steady Aim

As a bonus action, if you haven't moved this turn, you gain advantage on your next attack roll; your speed is 0 until the end of the turn.

Starting equipment

  • Rapier, Shortbow, Quiver, Arrows (20), Burglar's Pack, Leather Armor, Dagger (2), Thieves' Tools
  • Starting wealth: about 100 GP
