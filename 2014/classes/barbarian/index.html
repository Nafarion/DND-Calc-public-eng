Barbarian

D&D 5e (2014 rules) class. A fierce warrior from the fringes of civilization. The barbarian draws strength from primal rage and withstands blows that would fell others.

Class features

Level 1 Rage

As a bonus action you enter a rage for 1 minute: Advantage on Strength checks and saving throws, +2 damage with Strength-based melee weapon attacks (+3 from level 9, +4 from level 16), resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage. You can't cast spells. 2 rages per Long Rest, more as you level.

Level 1 Unarmored Defense

Without armor, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Constitution modifier. You can use a shield.

Level 2 Reckless Attack

When you make your first attack on your turn, you can gain Advantage on Strength-based melee attacks this turn, but attacks against you also have Advantage until your next turn.

Level 2 Danger Sense

Advantage on Dexterity saving throws against effects you can see (traps, spells), unless you're blinded, deafened or incapacitated.

Level 3 Primal Path

You choose a path that shapes the nature of your rage.

Level 4 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 5 Extra Attack

You attack twice when you take the Attack action.

Level 5 Fast Movement

Speed +10 ft. while you aren't wearing heavy armor.

Level 6 Path feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Primal Path).

Level 7 Feral Instinct

Advantage on Initiative. If you're surprised but enter a rage on your first turn, you act normally.

Level 8 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 9 Brutal Critical

On a critical hit with a melee attack, roll one additional weapon damage die (two from level 13, three from level 17).

Level 10 Path feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Primal Path).

Level 11 Relentless Rage

If you drop to 0 hit points while raging, make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw: on a success you drop to 1 hit point instead. Each repeat raises the DC by 5 until you rest.

Level 12 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 13 Brutal Critical (2 dice)

On a critical hit with a melee attack, roll two additional weapon damage dice.

Level 14 Path feature

You gain a feature from your chosen subclass (Primal Path).

Level 15 Persistent Rage

Your rage ends early only if you fall unconscious or choose to end it.

Level 16 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 17 Brutal Critical (3 dice)

On a critical hit with a melee attack, roll three additional weapon damage dice.

Level 18 Indomitable Might

If your total for a Strength check is less than your Strength score, use the score instead.

Level 19 Ability Score Improvement

You can increase one ability score by 2 or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20), or take a feat.

Instead of an Ability Score Improvement, you can take a feat: all feats (120) →

Level 20 Primal Champion

Strength and Constitution +4, and their maximum becomes 24.

Subclasses: Primal Path

  • Path of the BerserkerRage for rage's sake: the berserker throws themselves into battle, heedless of the cost.
  • Path of the Totem WarriorAnimal spirits guide the barbarian and grant them the powers of the totem.
  • Path of the BeastTasha's Cauldron of EverythingA bestial nature bursts out in rage: fangs, claws and a tail.
  • Path of Wild MagicTasha's Cauldron of EverythingA rage steeped in wild magic sets off unpredictable surges.
  • Path of the Ancestral GuardianXanathar's Guide to EverythingAncestral spirits rise to defend the barbarian and their allies.
  • Path of the Storm HeraldXanathar's Guide to EverythingThe barbarian's rage becomes a storm: desert heat, sea thunderstorm or tundra frost.
  • Path of the ZealotXanathar's Guide to EverythingA barbarian whose rage is a gift of a deity. The gods are in no hurry to let them leave the world of the living.
  • Path of the GiantBigby Presents: Glory of the GiantsWhile raging, the barbarian draws on the primal power of giants: growing in size and filling their weapon with an element.
  • Path of the BattleragerSword Coast Adventurer's GuideDwarven berserkers in spiked armor who throw their whole bodies into battle. In the Forgotten Realms — for dwarves only; the DM can lift the restriction.

Optional class features (Tasha's Cauldron of Everything)

Optional features: a player takes them with the DM's agreement. Some replace Player's Handbook features — the description says so. In the builder they are turned on at the Class step.

Level 3 Primal Knowledge

Proficiency in one skill from the barbarian list; one more at 10th level.

Level 10 Primal Knowledge (2nd skill)

Proficiency in one more skill from the barbarian list.

Level 7 Instinctive Pounce

When you enter your rage, you can move up to half your speed.

Starting equipment
