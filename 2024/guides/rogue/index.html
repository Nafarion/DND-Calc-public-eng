Rogue: Soulknife

Sneak Attack every turn, psionic blades that are always at hand, and energy dice that turn misses into hits.

Why It’s Strong

  • Psychic Blades: the weapon appears in your hand, can’t be taken away, can be thrown (60 ft.) and always qualifies for Sneak Attack.
  • Psi-Bolstered Knack adds an energy die to a failed check, and Homing Strikes add one to a miss.
  • The 2024 Cunning Strike lets you trade some Sneak Attack dice for poisoning, tripping or withdrawing.
  • Steady Aim grants Advantage as a Bonus Action if you haven’t moved.

Character Creation

  • Subclass: Soulknife (from level 3).
  • Species: Halfling.
  • Background: Criminal — Alert feat, bonuses: Dexterity +2, Constitution +1.
  • Ability scores (standard array): Dexterity 15 + 2 = 17, Constitution 14 + 1 = 15, Wisdom 13, Intelligence 12, Charisma 10, Strength 8.
  • Skills by level 20: Acrobatics (Expertise), Insight (Expertise), Investigation, Perception (Expertise), Sleight of Hand, Stealth (Expertise).
  • Weapon Mastery: Shortbow, Shortsword (as you gain them).
  • Equipment: Leather Armor, Shortsword, Shortbow, Arrows (20), Quiver, Thieves’ Tools, Burglar’s Pack.

Ability Score Improvements and Feats

  • Level 4: Dexterity +1, Constitution +1
  • Level 8: Dexterity +2
  • Level 10: Resilient feat (+1 Wisdom)
  • Level 12: Constitution +2
  • Level 16: Lucky feat
  • Level 19: Boon of the Night Spirit feat (+1 Dexterity)

Level by Level

  • 1: Halfling: Luck (reroll 1s) and Naturally Stealthy. Criminal background: Alert — Initiative. Expertise: Stealth and Perception.
  • 2: Cunning Action.
  • 3: Soulknife: Psionic Power, Psychic Blades. Steady Aim.
  • 4: Dexterity +1, Constitution +1.
  • 5: Cunning Strike, Uncanny Dodge.
  • 6: Two more Expertises.
  • 7: Evasion and Reliable Talent.
  • 8: Dexterity 20.
  • 9: Soul Blades: Homing Strikes and Psychic Teleportation.
  • 10: Resilient (Wisdom).
  • 13: Psychic Veil — Invisible for an hour.
  • 15: Slippery Mind: Wisdom and Charisma saving throws.
  • 16: Lucky.
  • 17: Rend Mind: stun with a Sneak Attack.
  • 19: Boon of the Night Spirit (Dexterity 21): become Invisible in darkness as a Bonus Action.

How to Play

  • Steady Aim → a psychic blade attack with Advantage → Sneak Attack; a second blade as a Bonus Action when you don’t need Steady Aim.
  • Use the Withdraw Cunning Strike to leave melee without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
  • Throw your blades up to 60 ft. — you don’t have to fight in melee.

Alternatives

  • Scion of the Three (Heroes of Faerûn); Phantom (Ravenloft); compatible options — Inquisitive and Swashbuckler (Xanathar’s).
  • Arcana Unleashed backgrounds with the Arcane Infiltrator feat.
  • Arcane Trickster — Shield, a familiar to help, and wizard spells.
  • Assassin — maximum damage in the first round.
